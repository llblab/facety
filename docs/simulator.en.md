# Simulator contract

The simulator is one Rust crate, [`simulator/`](../simulator), standard library only (D130). It replaced the Node.js simulator; that implementation, its tests and its raw results are preserved only at the git tag `legacy-node`. This page owns what is implemented, the declared deviations from the [protocol](facety.protocol.en.md), every simulation assumption, and how the baseline was validated. Protocol meaning lives in the protocol; decisions in the [decision log](decisions.en.md); findings in the [insight register](insights.en.md); open work in [BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md).

**Selected capability not implemented (D171):** the current Rust Council ballot is binary and has no seven-day deferral. The selected three-choice rule keeps simple majority of ballots cast, no quorum and no recusal: confirmation backs the entrant; opposition leaves the last ordinary side winning; a deferral majority OR no option's majority at an ENTERED referendum cutoff adds seven days to the CURRENT LATEST shared deadline once. No ballots also cause automatic deferral. Deferrals may repeat without a selected cap; one deferred round/result or cutoff period cannot be applied twice, including by replaying old votes/checks. An ordinary case without Council entry retains its normal cutoff. Holds/custody continue and no settlement/halving follows merely from deferral. Exact round/member/authentication records remain OPEN, but the no-majority effect is selected. Existing tests and frozen D168 results validate only their pre-D171 executable, not this capability. This documentation change runs no simulation and authorizes no code or research extension.

## Layout and use

**Ticker compatibility (D172).** FCTY is the current monetary ticker; FACY was its former name. This is one asset with unchanged units and mechanics, not a conversion or a new balance. Current prose and generated SVG captions use FCTY. Saved JSON/JSONL evidence, archived figures and the historical prolongation source remain byte-for-byte as recorded. The `result-v4` field `acquired.heldFacy` retains its serialized name for compatibility and denotes FCTY amounts; historical `transferFacy`, stake fact and event identifiers are also preserved when describing their original APIs. The rename changes no configuration, randomness or numerical result and requires no result-schema migration or new experiment.

| Module | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| `graph.rs` | directed graph with sorted adjacency lists, revision counter |
| `rng.rs` | decision-keyed randomness (policy `facety-sim-rng/v2`; v1 for historical runs) |

```text
cargo test --release                     # unit tests, parity tests, documentation integrity
cargo run --release -- grown --seeds 4 --attackers 10 --horizon 60     # baseline rules
```

`grown` flags: `--seeds`, `--first-seed`, `--bound N|none`, `--no-layered` (ablation of D124, which is on in the baseline), `--allow-disputed` (ablation of D131), `--exclude-challengers` (ablation of D157), `--attackers`, `--horizon`, `--target-honest`, `--strategy cone|honest-first|supported-first`, honest levers (`--vetting`, `--vet-sens`, `--vet-fpr`, `--met`, `--double`, `--artifact`, `--collusion`, `--subtree-cases`, `--confirm`, `--confirm-public`, `--targets-only`, `--svg DIR` (one plot per seed), `--accuse`, `--accuse-hubs`, `--key-loss`, `--recovery-vouch`, `--recovery-delay`, `--befriend`, `--invite`, `--accept-far`, `--odd-share`, `--odd-mult`, `--shock-day`, `--shock-days`, `--shock-fpr-x`, `--appeal`, `--restore-links`, `--recover-liquid`, `--xp-penalty`), detection levers (`--inspect`, `--fpr`, `--sens`, `--review`, `--accuracy`, `--public-look`, `--acquit`, `--detect-prefix`), `--council-seats`, `--council-seats-per-d`, `--council-per-thousand`, `--max-d`, `--k`, `--k-step`, `--k-xp-per-level`, `--search-order`, `--careless`, `--corrupt`, `--careless-accept`, `--patience`, `--defense`, `--defense-days`, `--base-stake`, `--examiner-looks`, `--watchers`, `--keep-rights`, `--no-stakes`, `--no-halving`, `--no-integrity`, `--halving-spares-council`, `--join-rate`, `--council-rate`, `--council-turnout`, `--no-attacker-ladder`, `--disputes-from`, `--honest-genesis`, `--max-subjects`, `--threads`, `--out DIR`, `--prefix-only`. Output is one JSON line per seed, printed as each seed finishes (order by completion; sort by the `seed` field). Unknown or repeated options, missing values and invalid parameters (probabilities outside [0, 1] or not finite, zero seeds, threads or horizon, a path bound outside 2..32, layered support without a bound, a target below the genesis size) are refused with `error: ...` and exit code 2; nothing runs and nothing is saved.

With `--out DIR` each seed writes `DIR/<seed>-<configuration digest>.json` containing `meta` (build commit, dirty flag, elapsed seconds, threads, command line) and `result` (schema `facety-sim/result-v4`, engine, randomness policy, initial/full phase/effective configurations, completed days, censor reason, counters and daily history). The digest covers the seed, initial configuration and phases, including observation dependence and the Council-halving ablation. Existing files require explicit `--overwrite`. `tests/cli.rs` guards saved files and deterministic results. V4 adds cohort outstanding/repaid debt, held/debtor account-days, ongoing holds/cases, absolute holdings, acquired-account resources and wrongly invalidated accounts by cohort. `debtByCohort` remains the historical created-debt counter; use `economics.outstandingDebt` for current debt. Settled-case hold hours can double-count overlapping holds; `heldAccountDays` counts their daily union and includes ongoing cases. These day counters are discrete samples, not sub-day duration measurements. Endpoints are the last sampled day before the declared horizon, never an extrapolation.

Observation sensitivity flags: `--repeat-correlation P` and `--persistent-errors`. Default remains fresh independent observations, correlation 0. No repeat model is a protocol admission rule. Numeric stakes are exact through all amounts representable in `u32`; larger amounts are explicitly refused as a simulator representation limit (not a protocol cap), and Council entry still works. The old `u128` intermediate overflow around position 50 is corrected.

## Randomness

Every decision draws from its own stream keyed by (root seed, channel, actor, day), so unrelated decisions never share a stream offset. Generator: splitmix64 seeding into xoshiro256**. `facety-sim-rng/v2` adds channel 22 for optional persistent target errors; channels 1-21 keep v1's layout. V1 replaced the retired `sha256-xoshiro128starstar-v2`; Rust and Node outcomes are statistically comparable, not bit-identical. A new key layout or generator needs a new identifier. A snapshot clones the whole simulation state (`Sim: Clone`).

## Deviations and assumptions

- XP follows D42/D54/D163: a valid, uncancelled Self-ID credits its author and its creation-time guarantor snapshot at `recorded_at + 24h`, whatever happens to the entitlement; time moves only through `tick`, which settles every act that matured up to the new hour before any decision reads XP, so settling a batch equals processing chronologically (credits at an hour apply before the other events of that hour). The credit stamp is the maturity time. The old convention (credit when the source right is spent) is retired; it survives only as the reproducible tag `baseline-spend-credit-d156`, and results of both are not comparable.
- Matured XP is revoked only by a separately authorized correction: a chain cut (`void_chain`, the verdict path) or `revoke_source_xp`; a guarantor who leaves keeps credited units (D166) and stays in existing creation snapshots (D54). Retrospective self-disputes of a guarantor's past acts are not modeled. Cancelling an invitation no longer revokes its source XP. The identity-case abstraction does not identify one losing transaction or implement every D55 invitation-specific penalty role; its losing owner's current coefficient is halved once. Source receipts retain their own snapshots and chronology.
- Free cancellation (`cancel_self_id`, D42/D79): author or a guarantor of the snapshot, only while the act is unspent and younger than 24 hours, refused for a disputed author (the precedence of dispute freeze and cancellation is OPEN, the smallest reading is taken); the backing is burned, no XP is ever earned, the rolling 24-hour slot stays used. The runner does not exercise it yet (the freeze veto of invitations is a separate operation).
- The Council, the active count and difficulty D, the layered-support levels and the distance pruning are refreshed once per simulated day, not on every call. An account created today is therefore not seen by today's distance pruning.
- Council ties break by account index (older first).
- No Collector market or Treasury. Stakes, sanctions, debt and repayment from later emission are implemented. Custody reserves spendable liquid first, then mature own Emitter rights, exclusively across cases. Winning or mooted custody releases rights in kind; collection alone materializes those rights for transfer or burn without touching their XP receipts. Backing is snapshotted at entry, without later top-ups; rights subsequently revoked by another case provide no payment and leave a shortfall. Immediate sanctions collect both balances; later debt repayment burns rather than paying creditors (D167, no Treasury).
- Closure conformance corrections supersede executable `d9fac60` and its confirmation at `5a12885`: mature Emitter backing/collection, liability of all actual historical recipients at settlement (including pending acts matured during the hold), losing owner/Council-entrant coefficient penalties, and integrity repair during debt. D167 suspends new responsibility units for an indebted author's acts; D165 still restores the author's coefficient by 1/1000 per valid maturity, scaling existing units. No backfill occurs. Only the entering Council member is a held/penalized Council position here; other voting members' institutional costs are not modeled. Earlier quantitative results do not validate this corrected executable.
- Disputes implement the pre-D171 D158-D167 reading through `World`: each admitted position proposes its own window end, and the implemented shared deadline is its maximum. Admission is strictly before that cutoff, with same-hour submissions ordered. Council entry is terminal, collective and free of stake/XP eligibility. The implemented ballots are binary and never move the clock; a majority confirms the entrant, otherwise the last ordinary side wins. This is a declared deviation from D171: the third choice, distinct adopted decisions, repeated additive extensions and their continuing holds/custody have no implementation or validation. Ordinary stakes remain `ceil(base * 1.2^(n-1))` (base 7; 14/28 are research variants). A winner recovers its own custody and receives only the actual backing below; a losing top stake or a stake directed to the Council is burned by the declared allocation assumption. A losing accused pays the base to the accuser and every historical XP-receiving guarantor is charged the base for burning. Missing funds become debt, never immediate burn or payment. Losing participants' integrity is halved, including the modeled Council entrant; `--no-integrity` is the historical balance-halving ablation. Unbacked entry is allowed and counted under the legacy counter name `refused_funds`.
- D77 holds cover the accused and every admitted position until settlement; overlapping cases release only their own hold. The accused's unspent rights cannot be withdrawn (§17.4). Only the entering Council member is held by this position abstraction. Anyone may join; daily rates and at most one ordinary entry per day are runner behavior assumptions, not eligibility limits. A genuine accused's accepted outside proof overrides the ladder at `max(deadline, proof completion)`; no entrant can join after cutoff while proof is pending. Truth reaches supporters and Council only through declared belief accuracy; the outside-proof oracle remains an explicitly optimistic assumption, never guaranteed participation or truth in real use. Free immature-Self-ID cancellation is a separate authority without the general XP gate.
- Evaluator labels (cohort and identity kind) are read only by the observation functions of noisy looks and verdicts; the world never sees them.
- Fake accounts cost nothing beyond protocol FCTY (an upper bound on attacker ease). Attacker operators are real people; only cone accounts and double-life accounts are false identities.
- Honest behavior rates, detection parameters and the 28-day activity window are scenario assumptions, not calibrated human behavior. Raw reports of attack outcomes are conditional on scenario, policy, horizon, seeds and the success criterion (D129).
- Detection is always on (D125); the retired no-detection arms are not reproduced.
