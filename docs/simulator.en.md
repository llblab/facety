# Simulator contract

The simulator is one Rust crate, [`simulator/`](../simulator), standard library only (D130). It replaced the Node.js simulator; that implementation, its tests and its raw results are preserved only at the git tag `legacy-node`. This page owns what is implemented, the declared deviations from the [protocol](facety.protocol.en.md), every simulation assumption, and how the baseline was validated. Protocol meaning lives in the protocol; decisions in the [decision log](decisions.en.md); findings in the [insight register](insights.en.md); open work in [BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md).

**Role under conditional-mainnet preparation (D188):** close the executable selected subset, make a possible future launch measurable, and supply registered conditional decision surfaces without claiming human calibration. No rehearsal or pilot precedes mainnet development under this direction. D168 remains REVISE; neither model outcomes nor this contract authorize deployment or accept launch risk without human evidence. OPEN admission, financial, migration, ordering and privacy contracts still require owner choices. The [canonical plan](../BACKLOG.md#conditional-mainnet-preparation) owns confirmation and work order; no new mechanics, kernel extraction or research result is claimed here.

**Self-ID correction:** the [trusted Self-ID fixture](#self-id-transaction-fixture) executes D189/D192/D195 under D193: freeze the inclusive scope at filing, fully reserve the initial `N` stake from liquid FCTY at activation, collect actual accused backing before the cut and charge the creation-time guarantors separately. The population runner does not create these cases. Neither identity cases nor historical evidence are substitutes for this fixture.

**Council rounds (D171/D173):** the existing Rust ladder and World support confirmation, opposition and seven-day deferral, with simple majority of cast ballots and no quorum or recusal. Results are recognized only at the current cutoff. No majority, including zero ballots, automatically defers; either deferral adds seven days to the current latest deadline once. Every new round freezes a fresh Council snapshot and starts without old ballots. Round-qualified ballots/results reject replay; deferral preserves terminal entry, custody and holds, without settlement or halving. Tests check these transitions, not human availability or a production executor. D168's frozen binary-vote runs remain earlier-build evidence; no new research panel, session, runtime or pilot is implied.

**Appeal core and restoration boundary (D174-D178/D181):** World implements a verifiable core fixture lifecycle: affected-person request, linkage to the prior accuser's recorded opening, qualified participant or explicitly trusted collective initiation, 24-hour pending period, fresh Council review, repeatable seven-day rounds, recognized confirmation/reversal and separate restoration execution. Restoration returns status and original revoked receipts, replays integrity and cancels only unpaid attributable FIFO debt. Independent causes, other holds, prior burns and transfers remain. Completed key-recovery migration retains reversal authorization but blocks restoration atomically. Admission/evidence, collective authentication, appeal finances and production execution remain OPEN. The opt-in population `--appeal` still supplies conditional approved reversals; it does not drive this new fixture route. Older status-only appeal results do not validate it.

**Opening lifecycle (D197/D198/D199):** ordinary, Self-ID and linked appeal openings share one unresolved slot per accused simulator subject ID; an appeal targets the prior opener. The slot is acquired on accepted recording and survives pendency, activity, unlimited Council deferrals and verdict recognition until execution finishes. Case IDs and appeal IDs have separate namespaces, including in maturity holds. An accepted ordinary or Self-ID filing atomically reserves its full first stake from spendable Collector FCTY (`OpeningRecord::reserved_stake`, `SelfIdCaseRecord::reserved_stake`) and records no restrictions; a filing that cannot reserve it is refused with `UNFUNDED_OPENING` before any case ID, slot or custody is allocated. The author or a usable account in the opening author's recording-time accepted mutual roster can cancel before 24 hours. This is trusted-ID authorization, not authenticated production membership; subsequent link changes do not rewrite the stored roster, the challenged Self-ID's roster does not confer veto, and collective initiation has no individual canceller. Valid pending cancellation releases that reservation once with the slot. At 24 hours the opening activates on the same reservation, without a funding check or second reservation; the D197 `Unfunded` maturity outcome and `UnfundedOpening` type are removed. The existing appeal fixture is core-only, with no monetary execution: it occupies its slot without collateral because appeal finances are OPEN, which is a trusted boundary, not a zero-cost production appeal. Active cancellation and mooting are not modeled exits. `begin_case` remains a trusted already-active fixture without a past opening transaction or filing XP gate, but requires the same free subject slot and fully funded liquid stake.

**Currency arithmetic (D184/D185):** `Amount` stores unsigned `u128` quanta of 10^-12 FCTY; signed net accounting uses `i128` quanta. Stakes use the exact rational nominal formula, then one final upward rounding to a quantum. No intermediate rounded stake is compounded. Money never passes through floating point; approximate conversions serve ratios and plots only. Configuration `moneyPolicy = integer-quanta/12-decimals/final-stake-ceiling/v1` participates in the digest. Saved earlier whole-FCTY results retain their original arithmetic and bytes; they do not validate this later executable.

## Layout and use

**Ticker compatibility (D172).** FCTY is the current monetary ticker; FACY was its former name. This is one asset with unchanged units and mechanics, not a conversion or a new balance. Current prose and generated SVG captions use FCTY. Saved JSON/JSONL evidence, archived figures and the historical prolongation source remain byte-for-byte as recorded. The `result-v4` field `acquired.heldFacy` retains its serialized name for compatibility and denotes FCTY amounts; historical `transferFacy`, stake fact and event identifiers are also preserved when describing their original APIs. The rename changes no configuration, randomness or numerical result and requires no result-schema migration or new experiment.

**Engine history:** `runner-v9` reserves the first stake at filing (D199): an unfunded challenger is refused at recording instead of holding the accused's slot for 24 hours, and activation reuses the reservation. This is a correctness repair that can change outcomes, not an outcome-preserving efficiency claim. Independent brute-force and certificate checks remain in force. Seed, genesis and World mechanics cannot change through `begin_phase`; such changes are refused atomically rather than recorded as if the existing World had adopted them. CLI preflight rejects overflowing seed ranges, slot steps, horizon/defense clocks and invalid shock inputs. A day-zero shock applies before the first attack step; long patience cannot wrap into premature invitations. Standalone stdout summaries now include engine and randomness policy as well as seed/digest. Randomness stays v3. Frozen results retain their original builds and bytes; no broad remeasurement is claimed.

## Appeal fixture contract

The public types are exported from `world`. `request_appeal(prior_case, affected)` accepts only a settled case and an account with a recorded affected role, receipt or debt; invalid status and lost keys do not by themselves bar requesting reconsideration. A request starts no clock and supplies no verdict or restored assets.

World retains each original `case_outcome`: actual settlement time, positions, Council rounds/electorates, transfers and debt creation, plus any explicitly supplied trusted proof/evaluator winner override. Appeal requests recognize ordinary participants and actual charged guarantors even when they have no unpaid debt or XP penalty. Reversal preserves the original outcome; its effects journal records corrections separately.

`record_appeal_opening_fixture(request, initiator)` is explicitly a **core-only admitted test fixture**, with policy `trusted-core-only/canonical-opening/recording-xp/24h/council-rounds/no-financial-execution/v2` (`APPEAL_FIXTURE_POLICY`). It requires an unreversed settled case with an actual final `OpeningRecord`; the already-active `begin_case` fixture cannot fabricate that transaction. A participant must be an accepted usable account with strictly greater effective XP than the prior opening's author. The actual accused must have no unresolved dispute. Both XP values and the new author's event-time mutual guarantors are retained at recording. The prior opening separately retains the challenged author's own historical roster; this is not the old identity case's XP-recipient proxy. `TrustedCollectiveCouncil` means collective authorization has been supplied externally to the fixture, never that one member has acquired unilateral authority. Evidence acceptance and signature verification are not implemented.

`record_transaction_appeal_fixture(request, target, initiator)` is the typed-target form of that same fixture. The target must be exactly `TransactionTarget::Opening(request.prior_case)`; a Self-ID/invitation with the same number or another opening is refused before allocating an appeal ID, custody, holds or effects. The original entrypoint delegates through this check. `AppealRecord::target()` derives this typed link from the retained prior case rather than storing a second mutable copy. This adds neither a production authority nor independent Self-ID/invitation dispute admission; the fixture policy and financial omissions are unchanged.

After 24 hours, `tick` activates the appeal and its first seven-day Council round, snapshots membership, restricts the opening's ordinary author if any and the prior accuser, and freezes the latter's unspent rights and unprocessed Self-ID maturities. Accepted recording is not revalidated against later XP/status changes. Every appeal is its own referendum; it has no ordinary escalation positions. The shared scheduler completes the activation/maturity batch before taking the membership snapshot for new rounds (see [maturity ordering](#suspended-self-id-maturity)). This submission-order convention and first-ballot-per-member policy are simulator assumptions, not selected production ordering or revision contracts.

`appeal_ballot` binds appeal, member and round. Its public choices name the prior verdict explicitly: confirm, reverse and restore, or defer. Retained generic Council rounds encode these as `Confirm`, `Oppose` and `Defer` respectively. The equal-ballot majority calculation is shared with the ordinary ladder. Only the cutoff recognizes the result; no majority automatically defers seven days, snapshots membership again and carries no ballots forward. Deferral creates no stake, sanction, winner or restoration. There is no modeled deferral cap.

`resolve_appeal` executes a recognized result and then releases only that appeal's restrictions, deferred maturities and slot. Confirmation applies no new sanction. Reversal invokes case-specific restoration; a completed-key-migration blocker retains reversal authorization, holds and the slot, without partial restoration. The blocked execution is journaled and may be retried. If a separately authorized restoration already ran, execution reports `AlreadyRestored`. Completed resolution replays return the retained result without repeating effects.

No appeal stake amount, custody, new monetary sanction, opening-guarantor merits penalty or transferred-money refund is chosen. This omission is the fixture boundary, **not a zero-fee protocol default**. A participant opening permits free pending cancellation by its author or trusted recording-time guarantor. Active withdrawal refuses independently of financial scope. A collective opening cannot be cancelled by an individual. Fee-free honest-link repair remains separately trusted and checks accepted status, activity holds, solvency, current slots and absence of an existing link/valence. New emission still passes the ordinary live support/path gate. There is no automatic link reconstruction or catch-up issuance.

Evidence: [`tests/appeal.rs`](../simulator/tests/appeal.rs) exercises wrongful-sanction restoration end to end, historical receipts, debt, no asset duplication, same-accused refusal across case types and deferred review, pending cancellation, active cancellation refusal, replay/cutoff boundaries, new electorates and batched versus hourly time. Child-module tests cover retained reversal authority after completed recovery, atomic refusal, corrupt records and invalid penalty fractions. Existing World tests cover overlapping revocations, FIFO repayment/cancellation, later integrity repairs/losses, original recipients and debt-suspended missing credits. These are deterministic transition checks, not human or production validation.

## Self-ID transaction fixture

The public types are exported from `world`. Policy `trusted-core-only/typed-self-id-target/filing-frozen-n/liquid-reserved-at-filing/v5` (`SELF_ID_CASE_POLICY`); v5 reserves the frozen-`N` first stake at recording (D199). This is the engineering subset authorized by D193 on the [Q25-A1 boundary](../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-a1-first-self-id-fixture-trust-boundary), now executing the D189 amounts on the filing-frozen scope selected by D195. It is not production admission, authentication, collective authority, a general asset-priority rule or an appeal contract.

`record_self_id_case_fixture(accuser, target, premises)` accepts a typed `TransactionTarget::SelfId(act)` only; an invitation, opening or coincident numeric ID refuses before any record allocation. Admission and historical-duty qualification are `TrustedPremise` fields that must be `Supplied`; the merits are a separate `TrustedMerits` value at settlement. No evaluator label or caller allegation is treated as a verdict. Ordinary non-author eligibility is still the selected recording-time gate: both accounts accepted and distinct, the accuser's XP strictly above the challenged owner's, a live uncancelled source that was not already corrected, a free accused-subject slot and no previously settled correction of that target in this fixture. Role conflicts that would make the case unexecutable — the opener appearing in the challenged act's creation roster, a duplicate roster entry, an unknown roster account — refuse at recording, while the case owns no record, custody or hold; multiple-role overlap beyond that remains OPEN (Q25-B1).

Recording fixes the disputed scope: `violating` (the 1-based act position), the inclusive `endpoint` (the accused's chain length at filing) and `n = endpoint - violating + 1`. Nothing after filing reprices it. At 24 hours the fixture reserves all `n` FCTY of initial stake and activates the holds/clock, or records an unfunded opening with neither. `cancel_self_id_case_opening` uses the same free pending cancellation contract as the ordinary opening. Cancellation and non-activation do not settle the target; a later admissible filing is fresh.

Self-IDs recorded after the filing are outside that case: they keep their own receipts and entitlements, cannot raise its stake, charge or guarantor charges, and correcting them requires a separate accusation with its own filing-time snapshot. Later verdicts and Council deferrals also cannot reprice the frozen count.

`self_id_case_plan(case)` is the read-only effect plan: `Ok` reports the frozen affected suffix, the frozen charge, what is collectible now and the resulting unpaid amount; `Err` names why the case cannot settle at this moment. A refusal is a capability or OPEN-contract boundary, never a verdict, expiry, acquittal or zero-valued settlement. Plan and settlement share one predicate set, so a previewed plan is what execution commits.

`settle_self_id_case_fixture(case, merits)` executes that plan in one World-owned effect path. It requires the selected stake/halving/integrity settings (no ablation), one ordinary accuser with no later ordinary or Council entry, the ordinary cutoff reached, and a frozen scope consistent with the accused's recorded chain. Asset state is a plan input, not an admissibility test: a spent, transferred, burned, reserved or otherwise unavailable amount lowers actual collection and raises this case's debt instead of stranding an activated case. `TrustedMerits::NotUpheldSupplied` refuses, including on replay of a settled case.

Effects, in order: collect the accused's actual backing for the first accuser under the model's sanction collection order (spendable liquid, then mature own rights; the uncollected remainder is this case's FIFO debt); revoke exactly the frozen scope's actual receipts under a case-keyed cause; halve the losing author's coefficient once under the declared integrity convention; charge each original creation-time guarantor its own frozen `n` and burn the actual collection, recording its shortfall as that guarantor's own debt; release the accuser's own reserved backing and this case's restrictions only; and journal the result. The identity status and the valid personal prefix are preserved, and no assets outside the payer's own holdings are taken, so no case-local priority is introduced. Calling an identity verdict, a role-specific identity penalty or an appeal request on this case kind refuses.

The isolated D192 witness (`n = 1`, the accused holding only the target's mature unspent right) is the special case of this path: collection transfers exactly that unit to the accuser, source burn and unpaid charge stay zero, and the guarantor charges burn one coin each. It is an outcome of the generic rule under those premises, not a separate priority rule.

The journal (`SelfIdSettlement`) retains the target, the frozen affected suffix and endpoint/`n`, original and charged rosters, actual collection/award, source burn, unpaid amount, guarantor charge and burn, every revoked receipt with its credit time and actual recipients, and the coefficient before/after. A repeated settlement with the upheld premise returns the retained journal without a second payment, burn, penalty or receipt removal. The structural audit pins the journal's full accounting and the monetary/lifecycle counters. `restore_self_id_case_effects` reuses the case-specific restoration primitive: it returns only this case's receipt units and penalty event, replays integrity without that loss, cancels only this case's unpaid debt, keeps prior burns and transfers (no refund rule is selected), does not recreate the consumed entitlement, and preserves unrelated cases and independent causes.

Boundaries. A completed key recovery that already moved receipts or liability refuses before any effect and is recorded as a named OPEN contract (Q25-C1), not as a verdict or expiry. Value transferred away before settlement is not recovered from third parties; the shortfall is debt (transferred-money refunds stay OPEN). Repeat-enforcement identity across sequential cases and unavailable-value recovery remain OPEN. The fixture models only supplied upheld merits at cutoff, not ordinary escalation, Council entry or an acquittal for this transaction kind. An unsupported settlement does not release the holds or slot.

Evidence: [`tests/self_id_case.rs`](../simulator/tests/self_id_case.rs) covers the executed isolated path, the filing-frozen deep scope (`n` above one with actual collection and recorded debt), additional Self-IDs during the pendency, a later act that needs its own case, disposition changes before activation, disposition changes inside the pendency, an unreserved versus reserved backing path, guarantor shortfall, overlapping cases, key recovery, identity/role/appeal refusals, typed-target, alias and role-conflict refusals, missing premises, non-upheld merits, free pending cancellation, pre-cutoff refusal, departed original guarantors against a changed graph, batched versus stepwise time, replay and case-specific reversal. The event-log verifier's prologue executes an isolated case and a filing-frozen deep case with a converted target and a cancelled out-of-scope act, then reverses both. These are deterministic software checks, not human or production validation.

## Suspended Self-ID maturity

**Selected rule (D196):** an active dispute suspends the maturity of the accused participant's not-yet-processed Self-IDs without cancelling them or affecting other participants. Implementation:

- `World::settle` processes ordinary maturities at their scheduled hours. The maturity cursor skips deferred acts so other accounts continue. At an activation event, the simulator first credits unaffected maturities. Same-hour maturities of due accused subjects are set aside for the activation decision; neither staged nor credited Emitter rights provide liquid stake backing. Process ordinary/Self-ID activations in case-ID order, then establish due appeal holds in appeal-ID order. Suspend the staged acts only for successfully activated filings; an unfunded opening's staged act credits at its original hour, without a suspension or release entry. Refresh membership then recognize ordinary Council cutoffs and start or recognize appeal rounds in their respective ID order. This ordering is a simulator convention, not production precedence for competing same-hour events.
- A suspended act has no usable entitlement, XP or integrity repair and cannot repay debt. Its author or creation-time guarantor can cancel even after 24 hours, unless that would escape an established obligation (for example a live case target). Cancellation removes its pending entry.
- At closing, the authorized result applies before the case's maturity hold is removed. An adverse identity verdict applies the accused's chain cut within `settle_case`; a trusted Self-ID correction invalidates affected uncredited acts before release. If a legacy spend age already disposed of an invalidated source, suppress its unissued credit without rewriting the asset's disposition. A confirmed or executed appeal releases its accused only after applying its recognized outcome. Closing processes only uncredited and now-unheld maturities already due; future maturity stays scheduled. The source's `Credit.at` is the actual credit time (including empty debt receipts), and the scheduled time is derived from the original act. Case-linked causes are released separately; another participation hold is not a release or an extension of this one.
- The audit reconstructs slots, reservations and holds from retained records, rejects credited/invalid/unheld deferred entries, verifies scheduled ≤ receipt time ≤ present, compares late receipts with the release counter, and requires an actual credit by the time a source funds an invitation. No suspended/deferred diagnostic counters from these paths rewrite historical evidence.

Evidence: [`tests/suspension.rs`](../simulator/tests/suspension.rs) checks activation ordering, delayed release time and replay through extensions, invalidation before credit, unaffected accounts, authorized cancellation and jumps versus stepwise time. The event-log verifier compares the slot, hold, deferred set and actual receipt after every event.

## Randomness

Every decision draws from its own stream keyed by (root seed, channel, actor, period), so unrelated decisions never share a stream offset. Generator: splitmix64 seeding into xoshiro256**. In `facety-sim-rng/v3`, Council channel 21 uses actor `(case_id << 32) | member_id` and period `round_number`; each member/round has a separate turnout/belief draw. Other channels keep v2's actor/day keys. V2 added channel 22 for optional persistent target errors, retaining v1's channels 1-21. V1 replaced the retired `sha256-xoshiro128starstar-v2`; Rust and Node outcomes are statistically comparable, not bit-identical. A new key layout or generator needs a new identifier. A snapshot clones the whole simulation state (`Sim: Clone`).

### Transaction responsibility records

Rust retains distinct facts rather than deriving every duty from the current graph or from a funding source:

| Transaction | Retained facts | Execution boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-ID | `Act::subject`, recording time, immutable creation-time guarantors and actual `Credit.at` and `Credit.recipients` (possibly empty or revoked) | Source revocation and identity-case correction exist; only the [trusted Self-ID subset](#self-id-transaction-fixture) executes separate Self-ID transaction merits |
| Invitation | `Invitation::creator`, issuance time, `funding_act` and immutable issuance-time `guarantors` | The roster records accepted mutual partners, without an activity filter; it is not an adjudicated liable set or the funding Self-ID's roster. No invitation XP credit or merits penalty is created |
| Opening | `OpeningRecord` author/accused, recording-time XP, mutual roster and lifecycle timestamps | Ordinary lifecycle and linked appeal fixtures exist; opening-specific merits sanctions remain OPEN |

The invitation facts are recorded only after admission checks succeed and before consuming the right. They survive veto, acceptance, link termination and later graph changes. The funding Self-ID's snapshot and actual receipts remain attached to that Self-ID; spending it earns/revokes no XP. `invitation_provenance_survives_roster_changes_veto_and_link_termination` separates the funding, issuance and later rosters and checks foreign-funding refusal before economic mutation. `audit_rejects_invalid_invitation_funding_and_roster_provenance` checks structural history integrity: a distinct own mature consumed funding source and sorted, distinct, non-self, in-range roster IDs. The audit does not reconstruct historical membership from today's graph or authenticate a supplied history.

`World::transaction_provenance(TransactionTarget)` exposes borrowed records for `SelfId`, `Invitation` and `Opening` without mutation or cross-kind/identity fallback. Opening IDs address the ordinary-case opening table, not every protocol transaction or appeal-opening record; active fixtures without an opening record are absent. These are model-local tagged IDs, not a selected production encoding or authenticated ledger commitment. The view preserves the transaction author/time, its own guarantors, a separately typed funding link and actual XP receipt time/recipients/revocation state. A pending, suspended or pre-credit cancelled Self-ID has no receipt, a debt-suspended maturity has an explicit empty receipt, and a revoked receipt retains its original recipients. Non-Self-ID transactions do not inherit source receipts. Missing funding/receipt fields describe recorded data, not fee-free admission or zero financial obligations.

`typed_transaction_provenance_separates_namespaces_and_actual_receipts` resolves coincident IDs to distinct authors and checks missing, issued and revoked receipts; the debt-service regression checks explicit empty receipts. `typed_appeal_targets_reject_aliases_and_unrelated_openings_before_mutation` checks rejected links against balances, XP, restrictions, counters, records and ID allocation, and compares valid typed/compatibility fixture records. These focused checks are not the full-state fingerprint/differential kernel proof planned in B.

These records are not production fault or liable-party qualification, authenticated veto or admission. Author-first self-dispute, unavailable-emission recovery and repeat-enforcement remain OPEN. No independent invitation or opening merits execution is inferred from retained facts.

**Ordinary identity model boundary.** `OpeningRecord` retains author and accused but no typed challenged transaction. `settle_case` performs identity-case settlement and the authorized accused chain cut together, using the opening stake and historical actual-receipt roster. The runner can separately supply a D127 related-identity correction or trusted role-specific penalty. These are not substitutes for the Self-ID fixture, transaction-level production admission, or an appeal executor.

## Deviations and assumptions

- D191 makes understanding/accepting rules and using the correction opportunities a participation responsibility, not empirical evidence of perfect competence or timely error discovery. Rust supplies no knowledge test or automatic exclusion for misunderstanding. Existing examiner accuracy, observation dependence and participation rates remain labeled uncalibrated assumptions; no new behavioral calibration or result-policy change follows from this clarification.

- Integrity is stored in millionths: one is 1,000,000 and a valid own maturity adds 1,000 up to that cap. Each penalty stores `floor(coef * (1 - fraction))`, using the existing `f64` fraction path; replay uses the same ordering and rounding. Final effective XP is integer `floor(units * coef / 1,000,000)`. This finite-grid convention is a model deviation from exact repeated halving, not a normative interpretation of D165's final-XP floor. Seven halvings store 7,812 millionths instead of exact 7,812.5; sufficiently many units can therefore produce different XP. The existing convention is labeled `integrityPolicy = millionths/penalty-f64-floor/own-maturity-1000/cap-one/v1` in saved configurations (including phase/effective configurations) and standalone summaries; summaries also report whether integrity is enabled. The additive policy field changes configuration digests, not simulation outcomes, schema v7 or randomness v3. Earlier outputs lack this explicit precision label and retain their original arithmetic. The normative precision choice remains open in [Q26](../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q26-integrity-precision-candidate). Two labeled candidates are prepared in `integrity.rs`: the existing grid (the default, reused unchanged through `grid_penalty`/`grid_repair`) and `candidate/exact-rational-halving/own-maturity-1000/cap-one/final-floor/v1`, an exact rational reference with a declared checked domain (120-bit terms, at most 60 halvings) whose exhaustion reports `TooManyHalvings`/`TermOverflow` instead of falling back to rounding or saturation. Neither candidate enters a configuration, digest or schema; `tests/integrity_precision.rs` records the divergences (128 units with seven halvings: exact 1 versus grid 0) and the capped-replay witness.
- Unsuspended maturity credits at the scheduled hour, independently of source disposition. Late credit follows the [suspension contract](#suspended-self-id-maturity); other participants' credit is not backdated or moved by the case. The former spend-credit behavior survives only in `baseline-spend-credit-d156` and frozen evidence.
- Matured XP is revoked only by a separately authorized correction: a chain cut (`void_chain`, the verdict path) or `revoke_source_xp`; a guarantor who leaves keeps credited units (D166) and stays in existing creation snapshots (D54). Retrospective self-disputes of a guarantor's past acts are not modeled. Cancelling an invitation no longer revokes its source XP. The identity-case abstraction does not identify one losing transaction or implement every D55 invitation-specific penalty role; its losing owner's current coefficient is halved once. Source receipts retain their own snapshots and chronology. The [sanction matrix](facety.protocol.en.md#242-sanction-and-settlement-matrix) separates these identity-case charges from act-specific 1-or-N descriptions and OPEN opening/invitation merits cells; the executable 7-FCTY fixture does not fill those cells or authorize a combined fine.
- The implemented free preventive cancellation (`cancel_self_id`, D42/D79) accepts author or snapshot guarantor only for an unspent act younger than 24 hours or still under a maturity suspension, subject to existing target/custody precedence. It burns backing, prevents ordinary XP and retains the rolling slot. D191 later error correction requires an admitted author-first self-dispute; it does not extend this free unilateral operation beyond maturity. The self-dispute's priority, funding and D58/D59 source remedy are not implemented by this method; its mature-author refusal tests do not validate that separate route. The runner supplies no author-cancellation behavior. Existing invitation veto is a separate operation, and opening cancellation uses its own recording-time roster.
- The Council, active count/difficulty, layered support and distance pruning refresh on each runner day and at each due Council cutoff. A clock jump processes due rounds chronologically, settling maturity before each snapshot; it cannot skip a round or snapshot membership from the jump's endpoint. Entry snapshots use the current cached Council. Other same-hour actions follow submission order. This is an explicit whole-hour simulator convention, not production authentication or a universal membership-ordering contract. Accounts created since the latest refresh are not seen by that snapshot's distance pruning.
- Council ties break by account index (older first).
- No Collector market or Treasury is simulated. Only spendable liquid FCTY is eligible for stake custody at any position (D198). The opening needs full liquid coverage; later positions reserve at most their available liquid. Reserved liquid cannot be reused, burned by a separate balance burn, or collected under another case's sanction. Cutting a Self-ID does not remove liquid already withdrawn from it. Separate merits charges may collect spendable liquid and then mature own Emitter rights as before; a fractional collection or debt repayment converts a whole right and leaves exact liquid change. Later debt repayment burns, rather than paying creditors (D167).
- Case admission policy `trusted-ids/recording-roster/single-accused/liquid-reserved-at-filing/v5` is part of the configuration digest. Recording fixes admission XP, the trusted cancellation roster and the exclusive first-stake reservation (D199). Activation checks neither eligibility nor funding again. An unfunded filing is a refusal (`detection.casesRefused`, `openingTransactions.refusedUnfunded`), distinct from early cancellation and from merits loss. The runner files ordinary openings without a cancellation probability; a cancelled filing leaves the runner before detection picks new targets.
- Same-time scheduler order (model convention; the protocol leaves the canonical order of same-time transactions OPEN): within one event hour, maturities due strictly before it are credited, due openings activate on their reservations, that hour's maturities are credited (an activated accused's maturity is suspended), Council cutoffs close and fresh electorates are taken. Calls at the same hour run afterwards in call order, so cancellation is accepted only before `matures_at`, and a filing at another opening's boundary sees only free Collector FCTY.
- D177/D178 restoration is a trusted execution primitive keyed to a settled case. Identity invalidations and source revocations retain every independent cause, even if another case already revoked the same receipt. Reversal removes only its cause, and restores only stored `Credit.recipients`, never current guarantors, pre-maturity cancelled acts or debt-suspended empty receipts. Burned entitlements are not re-created. Integrity uses original own-maturity counts between ordered penalty events, including capped and debt-suspended repairs; only the reversed case's loss events are removed. The regression `1 -> loss -> 0.5 -> 300 repairs -> 0.8 -> other loss -> 0.4` restores to 0.5, not the 0.8 produced by algebraic undo. Live penalty application and replay share one coefficient-rounding function. Later losses and uncased inherited key-recovery caps remain. The flat-balance ablation returns the recorded cut without repricing other cuts. `low_coef` remains the historical minimum actually recorded, not a rewritten history.
- Debt tranches retain original amount, remaining amount, repaid amount and cancelled amount with their case ID. Repayment consumes the oldest outstanding tranche; same-hour creation follows simulator submission order, not a new production tie rule. Reversal cancels only that case's remainder and never reduces cumulative creation, repayment or burns. Missing/open/already-reversed cases are rejected. Restoration leaves other holds, links, slot clocks and unrelated invalidations intact; usable emission still needs accepted status, free activity, current support and path eligibility. A completed key recovery involving an affected historical source is rejected before mutation: routing the correction to a successor and revising a dependent inherited cap require a migration contract, and a consumed source cannot be revived.
- Conditional appeal execution has policy `conditional-approved-case-reversal/earned-receipts/integrity-replay/fifo-debt/v1`, stored as `appealExecutionPolicy` in configurations and `restoration.executionPolicy` in results. The original case ID is carried with the scheduled reversal; the supplied probability/delay is not Council attention, evidence sufficiency or permission to bypass the selected transaction-level appeal. Blocked migration increments `blockedConditionalAppeals` rather than claiming restoration. No selected right to recover previously transferred money is implemented.
- Case provenance also covers the runner's existing optional inviter XP penalty and the related holder invalidated under D127. Reversal removes only this case's recorded cause for each affected subject; independent status/source causes still prevail. `restoration.statusesRestored` counts actual account status changes, separately from cases reversed and the runner's primary accused-person counter. `xpHalved` and `low_coef` retain historical imposed consequences rather than claiming current irreversible XP loss. No new collateral penalty or monetary refund is added by attribution.
- Closure conformance corrections supersede executable `d9fac60` and its confirmation at `5a12885`: mature Emitter backing/collection, liability of all actual historical recipients at settlement (a suspended act that has not credited contributes no receipt), losing owner/Council-entrant coefficient penalties, and integrity repair during debt. D167 suspends new responsibility units for an indebted author's acts; D165 still restores the author's coefficient by 1/1000 per valid maturity, scaling existing units. No backfill occurs. Only the entering Council member is a held/penalized Council position here; other voting members' institutional costs are not modeled. Earlier quantitative results do not validate this corrected executable.
- Disputes implement D158-D167 with D171/D173 rounds through `World`. Each admitted position proposes its own window end; Council deferrals add to their maximum. Admission and ballots are strictly before cutoff, and only a recognized cutoff result can settle an entered Council case. Round numbers start at zero; a first timely ballot from each snapshot member is accepted, later submissions from that member are refused. This is a no-revision model assumption while production ballot revision remains OPEN. Trusted World IDs stand in for authentication. Active-round membership is fixed even if the daily Council changes; each new round snapshots again. The ladder retains ballots, recognition cutoff and deadline before/after; World retains corresponding electorates and returns both histories on settlement. Saved population output contains aggregate round counters, not per-case evidentiary records. Deferral alone changes no custody, hold, stake, XP or ordinary position. Ordinary stakes use `ceil(base * 10^12 * 6^(n-1) / 5^(n-1))` quanta (base 7; 14/28 are research variants), with one final rounding. A winner recovers its own custody and receives only the actual backing below; a losing top stake with no ordinary recipient, including Council-only intervention, burns actual collection under D179. A losing accused pays the base to the accuser and every historical XP-receiving guarantor is charged the base for burning. Missing funds become debt, never immediate burn or payment. Losing participants' integrity is halved, including the modeled Council entrant; `--no-integrity` is the historical balance-halving ablation. Unbacked entry is allowed and counted under the legacy counter name `refused_funds`.
- D77 holds cover the accused and every admitted position until settlement; overlapping cases release only their own hold. The accused's unspent rights cannot be withdrawn (§17.4). Only the entering Council member is held by this position abstraction. Anyone may join; daily rates and at most one ordinary entry per day are runner behavior assumptions, not eligibility limits. A genuine accused's accepted outside proof overrides the ladder only after its ordinary cutoff or recognized final Council result and proof completion; it cannot settle a pending/deferred Council case. Ordinary entry remains closed while awaiting proof. Truth reaches supporters and Council only through declared belief accuracy; the outside-proof oracle remains an explicitly optimistic assumption, never guaranteed participation or truth in real use. Free immature-Self-ID cancellation is a separate authority without the general XP gate.
- Evaluator labels (cohort and identity kind) are read only by the observation functions of noisy looks and verdicts; the world never sees them.
- Fake accounts cost nothing beyond protocol FCTY (an upper bound on attacker ease). Attacker operators are real people; only cone accounts and double-life accounts are false identities.
- Honest behavior rates, detection parameters and the 28-day activity window are scenario assumptions, not calibrated human behavior. Raw reports of attack outcomes are conditional on scenario, policy, horizon, seeds and the success criterion (D129).
- Detection is always on (D125); the retired no-detection arms are not reproduced.

## Verification surface

**Second certificate verifier (`certificate.rs`).** It answers *is this supplied family valid?*, never *does one exist?*. It restates the specification's predicates with its own data structures and reports one named failure: `TooFewPaths`, `PathTooShort`, `PathTooLong`, `WrongSource`, `UnknownNode`, `MissingEdge`, `NotSimple`, `BlockedIntermediate`, `SharedVertex`, `ReusedEndpoint`, `InvalidTerminal`, `DuplicateRoute`, `UnknownSource`, `DimensionMismatch`. It shares no acceptance logic with `paths::verify_family`. Tests compare both verifiers on the same families, confirm every successful exact-search family passes both, compare the exact search with an independent brute-force existence reference on decidable small graphs, and check each malformed variant. A rejected certificate never implies that no alternative family exists, and `Search::Exhausted` stays distinct from `Search::Insufficient` and is never reported as a refusal.

**Event-log verification (`event_log.rs`, test-only).** Fixed seeds (11/29/47), a fixed event count and world size, and a declared shrink budget. After every event it runs the structural audit (money conservation, exclusive custody, FIFO debt, XP reconstructed from receipts and integrity history, slot accounting) plus an explicit supply/liquid identity and a custody exclusivity check. Rejected operations must leave the declared comparison projection identical. The projection covers every protocol-semantic field; excluded are only `stats` (declared diagnostic counters) and the path-finder/`Arc` work caches (efficiency state, D145). A fixed prologue executes one isolated Self-ID settlement and its reversal on every seed, so the generated logs cover both the new fixture and arbitrary refusal. Failures print the recorded seed and a greedily reduced trace and are meant to become named regression tests.

**Declared before/after panel (finite, not an equivalence claim).** Three representative configurations — baseline 10 attackers/60 days, the `--no-layered` ablation at 60 days, and `--accuse 1 --appeal 1` with 6 attackers/40 days — on seeds 1 and 2, one thread, fixed horizons and no reserved research seeds. Earlier checkpoints used the same panel as an equivalence check for outcome-preserving work (the D193 fixture, the verifiers, the `world.rs` decomposition), where the `result` payloads were byte-identical apart from `argv`, `dirtyWhenBuilt` and `elapsedSeconds`.

For D196 the panel is deliberately a divergence check, because suspending disputed maturities changes the mechanics. Observed: the baseline configuration reproduces its previous payload exactly apart from the engine identifier, the `--accuse 1 --appeal 1` configuration keeps its dispute counts and its daily history while a few money/civil counters move (`attackerLiquid` 1 → 0, `debtCreated` 483 → 484 on seed 1) because the extended cancellation window and the suspended credits shift individual events, and the `--no-layered` ablation moves materially (settled cases and false invalidations 259 → 186 on seed 1, 59 → 145 on seed 2) because deferred maturity changes when the accused's units arrive. These differences are the intended effect of the selected rule, not a defect: no fixture was adjusted to hide them, and the schema, randomness policy, configuration digests and frozen evidence are unchanged. Frozen results keep their original engine identifier and are not revalidated by this panel.

**Defect found by that verifier and fixed.** Unknown invitation, valence, act or case identifiers and out-of-range account identifiers panicked in several public entry points instead of refusing (`accept_invitation`, `cancel_invitation`, `end_invitation_link`, `withdraw_valence`, `convert_to_collector`, `open_valence`/`create_invitation` through their maturity check, `end_case`, `probe_invitation_paths`, and the trusted `penalize_xp`/`burn_liquid`/`invalidate_identity`/`reinstate_identity`/`void_chain` primitives). They now return an explicit `Reject`, `Probe` refusal or no-op, persisted as the named regression `unknown_identifiers_refuse_instead_of_panicking`. This changes no accepted-path outcome, schema or saved result.
