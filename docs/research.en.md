## New behavioral development lines (D46–D48)

**Owner-documented 2026-10-24; documentation first, no implementation or new data.** The [protocol](facety.protocol.en.md#185-invitation-freeze) owns D46's intended model focus, [§25.2](facety.protocol.en.md#252-intent-act-validity-suspicion-and-adjudication-d47) owns D47's distinctions, and [§9](facety.protocol.en.md#changing-participation-and-network-loyalty-d48) owns D48's loyalty hypothesis. This section connects them into development questions, not another task ledger. [BACKLOG](../BACKLOG.md#new-behavioral-model-directions-d46d48-documentation-first) owns remaining implementation/validation work. Historical F48–F55 are closed reference evidence, not tests of these new lines; the broader D39 programme remains paused.

### 1. Personhood, behavior and individual act correctness (D47)

**Owner distinction:** a genuine good-faith person can produce a defective Self-ID, and an actor with bad intent need not make every act invalid. Keep personhood, intent/behavior profile, act validity and observable evidence separate. **Question:** how does a supplied behavior profile generate acts and errors without turning a hidden moral label into a defense oracle? **Open:** act-validity definition and profile-conditioned distributions, uncertainty and correction behavior; no new good-faith immunity/fault rule. **Discriminator:** retain good-faith invalid and valid-but-suspected acts under the same procedural gates; check that changing the target's unobserved intent/person label cannot directly change defense decisions when the observer's own supplied behavior profile and observations are held fixed. The existing identity-class observation reference is not this act-level model.

### 2. Probabilistic suspicion around each act and observer (D46)

**Owner direction:** model suspicion around Self-ID, invitation and guarantee acts, with roughly an order-of-magnitude relevant-guarantor advantage over ordinary observers, rather than explain attention primarily by a hard pooled-work cutoff. **Question:** which acts are exposed, noticed and suspected, by whom and before which deadline? **Open:** the probability's unit/time basis, repeat semantics, overlapping roles, the exact XP dependence/average-guarantor definition and evidence error/dependence. The qualitative ratio selects no per-tick rate, base probability, calibration or perfect detector. **Discriminator:** separate no exposure, no suspicion, false suspicion and correctly suspected violations; retain correlated-source alternatives and valid-act errors. Costs remain recorded/explicit, not assumed zero, and technical caps remain censors rather than human motivation.

### 3. Low-XP discovery, senior contact and wider audiences (D28/D46)

**Owner clarification:** low XP belongs to the noticer, who can seek a higher-XP sponsor and people beyond the immediate circle. **Question:** can useful evidence reach a willing eligible decision-maker without the source itself satisfying the formal XP gate? **Open:** contact availability, publication/privacy, audience reach, sponsor assessment/refusal/rerouting, willingness, funding and timing. Do not equate the proposed guarantor suspicion ratio with the earlier illustrative senior-XP multiplier/joining chance. **Discriminator:** strong low-XP evidence, unreachable/refusing seniors, false packages and actual eligible entry are separate cases; contact must not conjure a sponsor, grant XP, create half-network coverage or bypass stakes. Existing bounded relay and timed Chekharda are references to reuse, not proof of the full integration.

### 4. Rare false accusations and evidence-to-verdict paths (D47)

**Owner example:** a correct Self-ID can be suspected, formally challenged and proven correct, so the challenging side loses. A suspicion source and risk-bearing challenger can be different subjects. **Question:** which valid/invalid acts reach a case, and how does the evidence/procedure yield success, failure or an unresolved outcome? **Open:** conditional stage probabilities, evidence adequacy/association, declared merits accuracy and remedies, not an infallible court or a new sanction rule. **Owner frequency clarification:** correctly and honestly made photo acts should produce fewer formal cases than violating acts in comparable exposure/observer/procedural contexts, while rare false cases remain possible. This belongs in evidence-mediated case entry, not just a higher defense win rate or a hidden-validity shortcut. The claimed rarity is an expectation, not a measured frequency; conditional-chain composition must not become multiplication of independent marginal rates. **Discriminator:** preserve differentiated valid/invalid case-opening frequencies, valid-act losing challenges, invalid acts that escape, sponsor refusal and declared adjudication errors; report the eligibility/response/decision gate that stopped a case rather than calling suspicion a win.

### 5. Changing loyalty, willingness and behavior over time (D48)

**Owner hypothesis:** participants may become more loyal to the network with time; their behavior is not a permanent good/bad classification. **Candidate mechanism, not a selected equation:** useful/fair participation or valued future opportunities may change subsequent willingness to inspect, respond, sponsor or stay. **Competing explanations:** an imposed time/XP trend, unchanged profiles, favorable supplied utility, group allegiance or fear of losses can produce apparent alignment without useful learning. Costs/wrongful treatment may instead reduce participation. **Open:** behavioral state and actor/subject scope, experienced observations, update/forgetting/recovery law, heterogeneity and real calibration. **Discriminator:** compare declared feedback against fixed-profile/no-feedback and time-only controls; distinguish commitment to correctable rules from protecting one's associates. Neither an encoded rise in a loyalty descriptor nor a utility ratio establishes voluntary retention or an emergent virtuous cycle.

### 6. Separate consequences and cross-line integration (D32/D46–D48)

**Shared evaluation boundary:** report false admission → Self-ID/emission → conversion/liquidity → XP separately from genuine erroneous vetoes, actual admission delays, procedural outcomes and unfinished lifetimes. Keep work by actor/cohort/action/phase and paid/rejected/denied activity, exposure/response clocks and distribution of burdens apart. Money minted/burned/transferred/recovered, debt, supplied private utility and independently justified application effects are different facts, not one welfare score. **Open:** real workload, acquisition/liability costs, error dependence, voluntary retention/usefulness and acceptable loss thresholds; the discussion does not select limits. **Discriminator:** a larger nominal capacity, lower sampled false positives, more senior XP, additional accounts or scripted loyalty cannot alone establish safer useful growth. Integration must expose where contact/eligibility/response failed and distinguish changed opportunities after trajectory divergence.

**Development boundary.** Before implementation or capture, explicit model contracts must identify supported states, allowed observations, probability/time/key units, authority/funding, unknowns and controls. Native `FacetyModel` remains the owner of economic mutations. Any new logical random-key layout requires a new policy identity and prefix/inert controls. Future evidence uses a committed method and fresh reserved labels where required; no historical replay or retrospective confirmation. Personhood verification, D33/D34/D42 migrations and OPEN court contracts remain independent gates. This map documents a direction; it does not start a loop, choose numerical laws, remove legacy limits or claim that the six lines are already implemented.

## Strategic evaluation lens (D32)

The owner's strategic-framing directive, documented **2026-10-23**, evaluates Facety as an **accountability substrate for emission distribution and network growth**, not merely dispute resolution. The [protocol's D32 section](facety.protocol.en.md#accountability-for-emission-and-growth-d32) owns the thesis, three-layer distinction, safeguards and success/failure conditions. This is a deliberate lens, not an observed flywheel or a replacement for D31's mutual-identification architecture.

The proposed return is emission → retention/contributor growth → more accountable participation → better evidence about contribution → more defensible distribution. The competing return is emission-funded farming/collusion → obstruction or wrongful exclusion → honest exit and weaker review → further extraction. Require evidence for each return rather than encoding retention, usefulness or honest superiority into a script. Oracle validity, Sybils, collusion, arbitration capture, bootstrap vulnerability and the cost-of-attack condition remain live alternatives and failure modes.

Evaluate **uniqueness**, **contribution/reputation** and **distribution** separately. Native Self-ID eligibility, terminal XP, custody and sanctions are recorded protocol consequences; they are not a general useful-contribution metric. Actual voluntary retention, service demand and attack/private opportunity costs need independent measurement or explicit conditional valuations. A scoped metric must say what is verified, by whom and how false attribution or collusion can be challenged. Do not turn “low-value” into a personhood judgment or select contribution-weighted emission by implication. Reuse the utility boundary, survivability vector, bootstrap/authority controls and existing study families below; no new study, parameter, confirmation or calibrated result follows from the framing alone.

## Integrated research programme (D39, 2026-10-24)

**Owner-selected research mandate, not a new protocol mechanic.** [D39/§49](facety.protocol.en.md#49-integrated-research-programme-d39) asks for root questions, detailed mechanism alternatives and their combinations to remain in one programme. Prioritization orders work; it does not eliminate untested branches. The immediate outcome to explain is sustained genuine participation through maintained guarantees and readiness to check/respond. Liability risk is a **working explanation** of withdrawal, not a measured preference, the sole cause of exit or a selected reward rule.

This chapter owns design truth; the [single convergence item](../BACKLOG.md#integrated-research-programme-d39) owns execution and next checkpoints. Existing backlog items keep their domain obligations and gates. The seven roots below organize the known scope, not all conceivable explanations; a newly material root must be named and related rather than silently excluded. No extra task ledger or second mechanism register is introduced.

### Root map

- **R1 — Truth claims, duties and evidence.** What is guaranteed: unique identity, a particular Self-ID or a preventable invitation? Detail: media freshness/reuse, key/control delegation, real-person mistakes versus fabricated identities, fault/knowledge, private/public signals, privacy/disclosure costs, shared errors, source independence and evidence validity. Existing basis: §19–§21, A5, media diagnostics and the bounded relay. Owners/gates: media clarification, D34 legal event/duty and human calibration. Falsifier of a broad social-verification claim: plausible oracle degradation or correlated review defeats it despite matched attention/resources. Evaluator truth never becomes a defense input.
- **R2 — Rewards, risk and actual economic consequences.** How do flat personal emission, guarantee-derived XP and accepted responsibility interact? Detail: prospective liability limits, custody/backing, actual collection, debt, reward/fine funding, early correction and restitution. Existing basis: F1's historical XP coupling, F24 stake comparison and F35 funding worlds; none supplies calibrated attack costs. Owners/gates: stake contract, D34 recovery/deduplication, guarantee-selection and incentive alignment. Falsifier: collected losses and real supplied effort fail to offset a declared attack benefit, or protection prices out genuine guarantors. Nominal fines/debt are not recovered funds.
- **R3 — Relationships, choices and sustained participation.** Why do people establish/keep guarantees, respond to problems, pause, exit or conceal? Detail: duty-sensitive protection for correction, loss of relation XP, suspension, scarce liquidity, reciprocal/low-uncertainty selection, beliefs/adaptive choices, future opportunities and unilateral deviations. Existing basis: exploratory F10–F12/F19–F20, A3/A4/A6 and diagnostic correction routes. Owners/gates: suspension, alignment/correction and incentive tasks; preferences and actual retention are unknown. Falsifier: a safeguard increases honest exit/concealment or permits profitable report-and-repeat abuse. A utility ratio or scripted continued activity is not measured retention.
- **R4 — Formation, topology, bootstrap and growth.** Can genuine networks form and remain open without making fabricated or coordinated growth easier? Detail: genesis funding/activation, active verified count, independent paths, anchors/direction, difficulty/epochs, age/size structure, dormant entry/camouflage and matched dense genuine communities. Existing basis: historical H1/H2 and F36 supplied-graph distinctions. Owners/gates: D33/D35/D36 and bootstrap-denominator items; no guessed verifier, anchor or permanently honest founder. Falsifier: comparable legitimate groups are excluded, or supported growth increases farm authority/obstruction faster than correction capacity.
- **R5 — Procedural authority, contestability and capture.** Can established influence remain correctable? Detail: low-XP/external sponsorship, entrant incentives, ladder/market/jury alternatives, Council referral/merits, recusal/quorum/ties, defender conduct, reasoned precedent and wrongful-outcome correction. Existing basis: timed-game observations, F25/F26 relay and the narrow chain-replay boundary. Owners/gates: Council/safeguards, procedural attrition, authority/recovery and D34 cases. Falsifier: established violations causally obstruct later valid correction under matched evidence/resources. Seats, a losing defense and coordination alone do not establish capture or guilt.
- **R6 — Time, attention, scale and recovery.** Can response keep up when people are unavailable or the network grows? Detail: one-day windows, notification reach, calendars/outages, case arrivals/service, backlog age, long horizons, correlated mistakes and restoration without resetting history. Existing basis: relay capacity/neglect controls, F18 and one-day exploratory cells. Owners/gates: evidence/attention, timing and recovery tasks; real attention rates remain calibration-gated. Falsifier: more discovery grows unprocessed harm or restores only a compromised baseline. Keep unresolved stage times missing, not zero.
- **R7 — Useful contribution, distribution and external demand.** Why stay beyond eligibility for emission and influence? Detail: a scoped contribution metric, verification/attribution conflicts, application demand, future private value, price/subsidy dependence and actual funded support. Existing basis: D32's three-layer distinction and declared valuation tools, not measured usefulness. Owners/gates: contribution/Atmo and human calibration. Falsifier: apparent retention requires free assumed support, or useful-contribution rewards can be claimed through collusion/fabrication. Do not turn XP, payment, traceability or low assigned value into a truth/personhood judgment.

Roots deliberately overlap through the existing M01–M15 causal records and study families below. They do not replace those owners. A genuine dense community, a coordinated coalition of real people and fabricated-identity ownership must remain distinct evaluation contexts; algorithms consume only declared observations.

## Central proposition and competing explanations

**HYPOTHESIS:** earned influence can remain correctable through public evidence, relationship withdrawal, credible challenges and collective decisions, even with a passive majority and bounded fraud. The competing hypothesis is that accumulated violations increase the resources, dependence and procedural power available to block future correction. Neither a high Council share nor an inaccurate verdict alone demonstrates that feedback.

A density-, age- and resource-matched community of genuine early builders is a required control for a collusive community. Dense reciprocal guarantees, high XP, shared interests and coordination are not offenses. A real-person coalition and a fabricated-identity farm are different adversaries. An investigator's allegiance is not a truth criterion: a malicious source may reveal a genuine violation, and a respected source may make a false allegation.

## Executable causal core

The question is the smallest faithful model exposing who knows what, who can act, why a path stops, and what changed state does to the next decision. **D31 is owner-provided intent**, not a finding: Facety coordinates mutual identification through mutual guarantees and public accountability. Directed consent is not a reverse guarantee; XP, finances, identity credibility, adjudication record and familiarity remain distinct.

```text
public events → supplied fallible observations → costed package / routing / review
   FacetyModel ← existing shared gate / stakes / consequences ← qualified sponsor
   native state → separate evaluator diagnostics + optional bounded causal journal
```

`src/model.mjs` alone changes protocol assets and authority. `src/accountability.mjs` owns the bounded reference's information/work lifecycle and journal; `src/disputes.mjs` owns shared challenge/consequence rules used by both scheduler and reference. Examples supply schedules, signals and verdicts while retaining evaluator truth separately. No new framework or implicit full-cohort feature is enabled.

## Survivability vector and stage clocks

Retain raw values and denominators, not a single success score:

- **Safety/fairness:** wrongful sanctions and exclusion, genuine membership retained, false-allegation review cost, proportional liability, access to correction, insider/newcomer differences.
- **Liveness/capacity:** admissions, normal transfers, package/case arrival and processing rates, unresolved queue age, sponsor availability, verdict and enforcement latency.
- **Economic/governance resilience:** actually collected losses, unpaid debt separately, honest work/fees, illegitimate money and XP, threshold crossings, seat mobility, voting independence, actual wrongful obstruction.
- **Recovery/usefulness:** residual ability to correct, time after restoration to declared health criteria, cumulative irreversible harm, churn and useful participation. External subsidy dependence is separate and unknown until modeled/calibrated.

Stage clocks are event→discovery→usable package→sponsor→hearing→verdict→collection→learning. Retain missing stages as pending/censored; absence of a hearing is not zero latency. Repeated copies of one package are not independent confirmations. Publicly observable counts/costs are separate from evaluator-only genuine/false labels, violation correctness, ownership and false-negative denominators; real-world versions of the latter may be unknown.

### Candidate regime criteria (must be registered before assignment)

Choose and publish practical tolerances, window length, horizon and minimum evidence **before** confirming a regime. Do not label current runs automatically with these names.

- **Healthy growth:** genuine useful participation grows while fairness, loss and queue bounds all hold over the declared window; reward/population growth alone does not qualify.
- **Stable bounded fraud:** evaluator harm/illegitimate-money trends stay within a declared near-zero slope margin and level bound, while authority, fairness and correction-access bounds hold. Small money contamination alone does not qualify.
- **Corrective overload:** case arrivals exceed service capacity over the declared window and unresolved age/backlog rise beyond specified margins, even if discovery improves.
- **Newcomer exclusion:** matched evidence/status comparisons show materially worse correction/admission access for genuine newcomers, beyond declared uncertainty/practical margins.
- **Entrenched influence:** low measured mobility coexists with failed contestability or failed influence removal after established misconduct. Stable honest membership alone is not entrenchment.
- **Self-protecting capture:** established violations causally increase subsequent obstruction under matched evidence/resources; restored ordinary correction cannot remove the obstruction within the declared horizon. Seats, scripted loyalty and one wrong verdict alone do not qualify.

Allow mixed, inconclusive and unclassified results. Require transient, population-size, horizon and initial-condition checks before naming a collective regime.

## Small human-calibration study (design only)

**Human-gated; no recruitment or data collection has occurred.** Preregister a pilot with approximately 24–40 consenting adults, a balanced randomized within-person task order and explicit stopping/withdrawal rules. The pilot estimates uncertainty/feasibility, not population-wide fraud rates. Repeated tasks from one participant are clustered observations, not independent people.

1. **Safety and consent:** independent ethics/privacy review before recruitment; adult volunteers, no minors, no real allegations or live sanctions. Use consented staged media and clearly licensed synthetic examples, not impersonations of uninvolved people. Do not publish faces, acquaintance graphs or raw identifiable logs to a chain. Participation and payment do not depend on accusing someone or guessing correctly. Provide an opt-out from acquaintance vignettes without loss of compensation.
2. **Tasks:** time ordinary individual/group identity work; time permitted staged fabrication under a fixed resource budget; blinded genuine/fabricated review with signature/reference context; build a bounded evidence package; route it to a separate sponsor for an independent review; introduce randomized missed notifications, queue delay and availability. Use fictional acquaintance/insider scenarios to measure willingness to report and reconsider without asking volunteers to accuse actual acquaintances.
3. **Measurements:** capture active work time and failures, acquisition/packaging/review effort, hit/false-alarm rates, duplicate-source recognition, calibration of confidence, sponsor rejection/retry, notification misses, delay distributions and stated willingness. Record false-allegation burden and refusal/abstention as valid outcomes. Predeclare missingness, withdrawal, censored completion and skill/accessibility strata.
4. **Data minimization:** pseudonymous participant/task IDs; separate consent/key table under restricted custody; bounded retention and deletion date; no unnecessary biometric embeddings. Release aggregate, de-identified measurements only with explicit consent and a re-identification review. Compensation is a fixed funded research payment with payer, recipient, currency and failure log, never a protocol mint or transferable XP.
5. **Inference/update:** participant-cluster bootstrap or appropriate hierarchical intervals, exact count denominators and broad uncertainty bands; no threshold hunting. Update only measured parameter distributions (`selfId`/`fakeSelfId` effort ratios, sensitivity/false alarms, verification quality, notice/availability/review delays and capacity). Keep observed fabrication technology/resources explicit. Do not equate lab classification with Council accuracy, stated willingness with actual misconduct reporting, or a pilot cost ratio with a universal farm cost. Preserve old simulator studies; new calibration creates new configurations and registrations.
