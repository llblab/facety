# Protocol consolidation check

One-time preservation record, not a rulebook or work queue. Baseline: `393b1f508491e5357583d00981ec9b591c690c79` on `main`, clean tracked and untracked working tree at start. The [baseline protocol](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/393b1f508491e5357583d00981ec9b591c690c79/docs/facety.protocol.en.md), [baseline decision log](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/393b1f508491e5357583d00981ec9b591c690c79/docs/decisions.en.md) and [baseline guide](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/393b1f508491e5357583d00981ec9b591c690c79/docs/rules-of-the-game.en.md) are the comparison, not additional current authorities. Section numbers below are from the baseline. Decisions were checked by clause, not assigned one status per decision. Compared the rewritten owners to the baseline and selected decisions, then checked back from each selected clause to its owner.

## Filing, Council and execution

- [x] §25 / D180–181, D190, D197–199: [Challenges](facety.protocol.en.md#challenges) keeps a public eligible transaction and strict author XP gate without requiring a link. Surety is an application example, not new jurisdiction. [Filing](facety.protocol.en.md#filing) keeps typed target, admission XP, event-time guarantors, case-specific price, full exclusive Collector reservation, atomic refusal and the accused subject's one unresolved slot (not a key or transaction slot). The prior opener takes that role in an appeal; a request is not an opening. Later filing can use new facts, not automatically repeat a settled penalty.
- [x] §25 / D181, D183, D190, D197–199: [Cancellation and activation](facety.protocol.en.md#cancellation-and-activation) retains separate timestamps, no active custody, suspension, restriction or clock during pendency, free cancellation strictly before the boundary by the author or their authorized guarantor, entire release once, and no merits/payout/penalty/debt. The challenged Self-ID's role cannot veto by itself. Membership/authentication remain open. At 24 hours the reserve backs the position unchanged; neither new money nor an adverse balance order can decide whether it activates. Filing and the validity of activation are no longer separate funding decisions.
- [x] §25 / D197: [Closure](facety.protocol.en.md#closure) requires execution, never voluntary withdrawal, agreement, cancellation split or extra compensation. Apply effects then release only the case's holds and credit surviving due acts; free the slot last. Do not confuse recognition or appeal authorization with execution. D182 and the active parts of D183/D184 are retired, not the pending veto or precision. D197 activation funding/non-activation and D198 timing are replaced by D199; D198 Collector-only custody survives. Historical examples keep their original labels and arithmetic.
- [x] §26.1–26.5 / D158–159, D162, D164, D166–167, D171: [Positions and the clock](facety.protocol.en.md#positions-and-the-clock) preserves alternate sides, one ordinary person per position, strictly greater XP, no mandatory examiner or confirming position, response from activation, entries before cutoff and before Council, last ordinary side at ordinary cutoff, position time plus `max(3, 8 - n)`, latest end with separate deferral additions. Refusals/duplicate attempts/notice/evidence add no time. No daily entry cap or duration cap was inferred from code or research.
- [x] §17.4, §26.5 / D77: [Restrictions](facety.protocol.en.md#restrictions) names the three prohibited acts for actual participants, not all transfers, defense, third parties or membership. Holds from other cases remain. Open termination, already-committed actions and collective participation are not resolved by the model.
- [x] §27.1–27.4, §27.6 / D33, D66, D69–72, D111, D133, D148, D161–162, D167, D171, D173: [Council](facety.protocol.en.md#council) preserves the below-100 rule in both directions, genesis seating, nearest-root lookup and caps, XP and 28-day eligibility, and no immunity. One member can initiate the opposite side, once, immediately terminal, without stake/XP gate or extra time band. A peer case files by ordinary transaction and goes directly to collective review; the older exclusion of both parties does not survive the no-recusal rule. A queue does not enter. Candidate rank factors and verification, size transitions and peer execution are open.
- [x] §27.3 / D167, D171, D173: [Rounds](facety.protocol.en.md#rounds) keeps one equal ballot, no recusal or quorum, all timely ballots at current shared deadline (never early), confirm/oppose/defer mappings, opposition as absent for payout but not history, seven days added once for majority deferral or no majority including empty ballot set, fresh electorate and fresh ballots at every round, no invented cap, no reopening ordinary entry and no penalty or release from deferral alone. Keep authority, evidence, revision and ordering open; no separate mandatory Council pay or unilateral apex authority.
