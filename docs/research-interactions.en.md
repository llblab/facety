# Facety research: interaction designs

Design-only appendix of the [integrated programme](research.en.md#integrated-research-programme-d39-2026-10-24). The parent owns R1–R7, I1–I9 and shared measurement/method contracts; this appendix owns the detailed interaction contrasts and the first bounded discriminator. [BACKLOG.md](../BACKLOG.md#integrated-research-programme-d39) remains the only execution owner. No experiment or protocol choice follows from this design inventory.

## Reference handles and applicability

The following handles identify inspected code, not new APIs or interchangeable models:

- **A — Native accounting:** [FacetyModel](../src/model.mjs) and `accountabilitySnapshot`. Funded actions, cancellation, relation termination, custody and journal order; current-link liability remains the labeled pre-D34 reference.
- **E — Cohort experiment:** [`runExperiment` / `experimentConfig`](../src/experiment.mjs), with [configuration ownership](../src/experiment-config.mjs). Declared activity, noisy sensing, effort, graph/social settings, schedules and optional dispute references exist. Voluntary retention, external sponsorship and a verified-contribution oracle do not. Safety caps censor; they are not admission policies. Daily phase ordering is an assumption, not a human calendar.
- **R — Bounded relay:** [`EvidenceRelay`](../src/accountability.mjs) and [`runRelayArm`](../examples/causal-relay.mjs). Registered work accounts, supplied signals, explicit routing, review availability/capacity, strict subject XP and funded one-step disputes exist. Different source-group labels do not establish independent errors. The example has two opportunities and three routing arms; it is not the full cohort scheduler, five-route comparison or timed Council pipeline.
- **G — Conditional games:** [`runGame`, `analyzeGame`, `sweepValuation`](../src/games.mjs). Explicit activity menus, schedules, deviation profiles and weights on retained facts are available. Utility is not observed willingness; a new service/opportunity is not an existing fact just because an analyst wants to value it.
- **L — Local incentive fixtures:** [`runIncentiveForks`](../src/incentives.mjs) retains legacy 72/168-hour timing, zero freeze and retained relation XP. The separately tested `runOneDayIncentiveForks` emits v2 with funded 24-hour windows, distinct admission/work snapshots and provenance. Both accept only `xpFinality`, never arbitrary policy bags. Retained old outputs stay historical; the one-day counterpart is the fixed-101/current-link reference, not D33/D34 or voluntary participation.

**Applicability codes:** **N** means a restricted native-reference contrast is expressible, sometimes needing a bounded scenario/example adapter; **D** means specified design/contract work is needed for the stronger question; **H** means human/application calibration is needed for real-world inference. Codes can coexist. N is not readiness of every factor, scientific support or permission to implement the missing D contract. Execution status and ordering remain solely in the backlog.

All cells inherit the parent's [comparative contract](research.en.md#comparative-method-and-convergence-boundary), [utility boundary](research.en.md#utility-boundary-and-sensitivity-plan), [survivability vector](research.en.md#survivability-vector-and-stage-clocks) and observation/ownership, matched-resource and source/method limits. Hold age, subject/operator composition, initial funding/XP, evidence opportunities and horizon constant unless they are the named intervention; expose residual differences instead of claiming a match. Compare genuine dense communities, real-person coalitions and fabricated identities where applicable. Use raw safety, access, cost and pending-stage denominators; do not silently merge those outcomes into retention or a scalar verdict.

## Root-pair interaction inventory

**Design inventory, 2026-10-24; no new experiments.** Pij denotes the unordered pair Ri/Rj. The 21 cells cover root pairs, not every within-root alternative or numeric combination. Evidence citations refer to the [source-bound insight register](insights.en.md), not a replay or method upgrade. A proposed falsifier is not a registered test; the concrete cohort must specify margins, sampling and controls before execution.

- **P12 — Evidence error × liability/reward (R1/R2; M02/M09/M11).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** At matched observations and resources, vary declared error and the implemented sanction/backing reference; compare actual recoveries, genuine burdens and false extraction. A nominally larger fine with unchanged collection, or rising genuine harm, defeats an unconditional deterrence interpretation. Include no-report and false-report controls.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F23/F24 diagnostic custody and exploratory timed games; not a calibrated joint error/liability result. Existing owners: stake selection and alignment/correction.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** A/E/G support conditional reference contrasts. Historical duties, correction funding and penalty identity need D34; media replay interpretation and production backing remain OPEN. Error, fabrication effort and risk preferences need calibration.
- **P13 — Evidence/duty × relationship response (R1/R3; M02/M03/M08).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Hold evidence/resources fixed while comparing supplied response/exit profiles; then separate disclosure cost, mistaken belief and expected liability in a choice design. If responses differ only because they were scripted, evidence explains consequences, not willingness; stronger signals with less reported willingness would challenge simple information-only explanations.
  - **Evidence/owner:** A3/A4/A5 and F10–F12 exploratory prevention/exit; no observed relationship retention. Owners: guarantee selection, alignment/correction and human calibration.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E/R can measure consequences of supplied responses, not endogenous reporting or privacy preferences. New duty/protection/self-report rules require D34 contracts; voluntary response and disclosure burdens require consented measurements.
- **P14 — Evidence independence × network formation (R1/R4; M06/M13).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Match size/age/resources for genuine dense, real-coalition and fabricated groups; vary supplied sensing correlation separately from visible topology. If a graph score separates only evaluator ownership or rejects legitimate dense groups, it does not establish personhood/independence.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F14 diagnostic structural screening, A5 and F36 supplied-graph witnesses, not joint human verification evidence. Owners: D33 topology/bootstrap and oracle sensitivity.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E supports legacy social/observation contrasts but has no correlated-error knob; R's bounded correlation cannot be joined to its growing graph by assumption. F36 supports contract distinctions only. D33 verified-count/source-independence, anchors/traversal and genesis fields are unresolved. Human source dependence and verification errors are uncalibrated; do not generate more graph permutations to choose the rule.
- **P15 — Evidence/status × procedural access (R1/R5; M04/M07/M12/M15).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Compare equal-quality insider/newcomer or malicious/respected-source packages with equal sponsor resources. Separate access from verdict quality; denial of stronger newcomer evidence or status-conditioned errors defeats the broad contestability claim. Related defenders need their own violation/knowledge basis.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F21 diagnostic XP obstruction; F25 diagnostic access feedback and F26 exploratory routing. Owners: Council/safeguards and procedural attrition.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** R tests one-step access; E tests labeled ladder references. Neither implements reasoned merits, appeal, conflicts/quorum or D34 overlapping duties. Status/record correlation is not independent truth; human evidence quality remains H.
- **P16 — Error/correlation × finite service/time (R1/R6; M04/M05/M09).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** At the same two opportunities, latent signals, work and horizon, reverse package service priority while holding discovery and settlement ordering fixed; compare capacity-one contexts with the capacity-two reference. Material differences reveal an allocation alternative to a general relay-benefit claim; nulls do not establish population capacity.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F26 exploratory three-context relay; [F44's bounded follow-up](../studies/2026-10-24-service-order/README.md) changes one shared-review world's correction under service reversal, not a general capacity/retention result. Owners: evidence/attention and matched-resource relay follow-up. I6 retains the additional procedural/window interaction.
  - **Reference/gate — N/H:** R supplies the operations; the example's validated `processingOrder` option isolates service priority without a new protocol rule. Three contexts are not a full capacity × correlation factorial. Real service/notice rates remain H. The bounded discriminator below defines the local question.
- **P17 — Evidence validity × useful-contribution attribution (R1/R7; M01/M08).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Compare accurate identity with correct versus manipulated contribution attribution; include true-but-useless and useful-but-misattributed work. Distribution that follows fabricated contribution despite correct personhood defeats sufficiency of identity verification.
  - **Evidence/owner:** D32 separates these layers; no scoped contribution-oracle study exists. Owners: contribution/Atmo and human calibration.
  - **Reference/gate — D/H:** No native useful-contribution oracle/API exists. Specify eligible work, attribution, verifier conflict/challenge and privacy before a design can run; measure application usefulness separately. Never substitute XP or media acceptance.
- **P23 — Economic exposure × voluntary relations (R2/R3; M01/M02/M03/M08/M11).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate prospective loss, relation-earned XP loss, liquidity and future private benefit in stay/report/exit/reciprocal-selection alternatives. Profitable concealment or report-and-repeat under declared valuations challenges alignment; a scripted stay/exit difference does not show real retention.
  - **Evidence/owner:** A3/A4/A6, F10–F12/F19–F20 exploratory references and F35 diagnostic timelines. Owners: guarantee-selection game, suspension and alignment/correction.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** A/E/G can retain current-reference consequences and explicit utility thresholds. L now supplies a separately versioned [one-day local counterpart](incentives.en.md#one-day-counterpart-contract-p23); implementation/compatibility and the fresh [F45 diagnostic tables](../studies/2026-10-24-one-day-incentives/README.md#executed-diagnostic-results-f45) are retained. These are fixed-script/conditional accounting, not voluntary relations. D34 duties/recovery, prospective limits and pause/counting are not implemented; expected losses/preferences are not measured. Do not price an unknown obligation as zero.
- **P24 — Economic/XP coupling × growth/size (R2/R4; M01/M06/M07).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate guarantee intensity, population size/age and growth cessation. A size-matched contrast eliminating the historical XP effect supports the size alternative; persistence after growth stops challenges a transient-only explanation. Keep actual admission financing visible.
  - **Evidence/owner:** H1/H2 are historically confirmed under their old source/timing/Council; H1 retains its size alternative. Owners: size-matched coalition controls and new-default registrations.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E exposes guarantee/growth knobs, not a matched-admission-count policy; `maxSubjects` is censoring, not a legitimate cap control. Design that scenario before claiming matched size. Current-reference work is possible; D33 migration and real acquisition costs remain separately gated. No automatic old-study replay.
- **P25 — Liquid backing × access/authority (R2/R5; M04/M07/M09/M11).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** With equal initial wallets, XP and evidence, compare actual collateral commitments, sponsorship refusals, collection and wrongful burden. Reserving all promises but preventing needed genuine cases defeats a simple collection-equals-security claim; do not pool operators' subject XP.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F24 diagnostic backing, F25/F26 access/routing; no systemic joint finding. Owners: stake selection and procedural attrition.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** A/R/E support unsecured/reserved references. Losing-top disposition, priority, invalidated recipients and alternative challenger/sponsor rewards remain OPEN for production; any new payment must name the actual funded source. Human willingness/capital access remain H.
- **P26 — Liquidity/recovery × deadlines (R2/R6; M02/M06/M09/M11).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** At equal funded prefixes, distinguish timely veto, pending lock, terminal/spent value and later collection under missed-response schedules. An apparent recovery obtained by counting debt or burned money as refunded value fails the discriminator; separate lost opportunity from unpaid liability.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F18 exploratory timing bottleneck, F35 diagnostic funding/order and selected-default reservation regressions. Owners: timing and D34 transition/recovery audit.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** A has exact hours; E supplies daily profiles, not arbitrary intraday notification calendars. D34 legal event/order, spent/debt-repayment reversal and overlap require decisions. Real review/payment delays remain H; a supplied outage is not measured risk perception.
- **P27 — Monetary valuation × external demand (R2/R7; M08/M14).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate core accounting, explicit private FACY/effort values and independently funded application support; compare farm consolidation/holding against ordinary payments. If favorable utility requires free support or a price assumption hiding attack/review costs, the sustainability claim fails.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F15 diagnostic flow visibility, conditional games and D32; no executed external market or calibrated demand. Owners: cash-flow game and contribution/Atmo.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E/G support declared cashout/flow/valuation contrasts only. They do not execute Atmo funding, market demand or useful-service delivery. Specify payer, recipient, pricing and buy/burn flows; market/private opportunity costs remain H. Transfer visibility is not guilt or usefulness.
- **P34 — Relationship choices × graph eligibility (R3/R4; M03/M06/M08/M13).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate reciprocal selection, newcomer access, exit and pause from the resulting graph under matched opportunity budgets. If a candidate preserves privilege while removing its watcher, or excludes genuine low-connectivity people, it defeats the intended participation safeguard. Include look-ahead timing, not just random pauses.
  - **Evidence/owner:** A3/A4, old social references and F36 diagnostics; no pause/verified-count result. Owners: suspension, guarantee selection and D33 topology. I2/I3 retain economic feedback.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E can compare supplied acquaintance/guarantee/exit behavior in the legacy graph. Pause, D34 historical duties and D33 suspended-member counting are unselected/unresolved. Actual relationship/exit preferences remain H; raw degree is not verified independence.
- **P35 — Withdrawal/loyalty × contestability (R3/R5; M03/M04/M07/M12).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** At matched evidence and future-value assumptions compare reporting, withholding support and concealment; separate XP-gate effects from sponsor work/custody. A profitable individual concealment deviation or wrongful-case XP loss blocking a later valid case challenges alignment.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F25 diagnostic attrition, F26 exploratory access and A6; no equilibrium of long-term loyalty. Owners: alignment/correction, procedural attrition and incentive alignment.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** A/R/E/G expose current-reference outcomes/finite menus. No endogenous future-opportunity, sincere-belief or sponsor market exists. D34 self-report admission and Council appeal/finality remain OPEN; supplying civic value does not measure loyalty.
- **P36 — Availability/exit × response time/recovery (R3/R6; M02/M03/M09/M10).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Compare supplied neglect/churn, return and temporary suspension with equal remaining resources/history. Restoration to a compromised baseline or additional unreviewed exposure defeats recovery; distinguish a missed notification, refusal, capacity denial and actual exit.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F25 diagnostic resumption and F26 bounded capacity, not voluntary return. Owners: evidence/attention, authority/recovery and suspension.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** R review availability and E activity schedules can test conditional outages without resetting state. Pause/reinstatement and historical duties need contracts; endogenous return and human calendars need H. Do not silently clear debt, XP or cases on restoration.
- **P37 — Future usefulness × relationship participation (R3/R7; M01/M08).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate future income/service streams, social value and sunk work while testing individual stay/report/exit choices. Alignment requiring an assumed lifelong subsidy, or concealment preserving more future value, defeats a simple network-loyalty return.
  - **Evidence/owner:** A–T discussion E and D32 are hypotheses/intent; no measured usefulness-retention return. Owners: contribution/Atmo and incentive alignment.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** G can stress supplied values of existing retained facts; L supplies historical local consequences only. Neither contains measured service demand, future-opportunity facts or voluntary adoption. Specify/justify those streams before adding them; H supplies willingness/real externalities.
- **P45 — Bootstrap/topology × procedural authority (R4/R5; M06/M07/M15).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Match genuine/collusive size, age and resources while separating admission, eligibility, seats and an actually obstructed correction. If topology creates reviewer immunity or leaves early violations uncorrectable, it defeats correctability; seat concentration alone is not capture.
  - **Evidence/owner:** Historical H1/H2, F21/F25 diagnostics and F36 graph distinctions. Owners: D33/bootstrap and authority/recovery.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E permits legacy leaderboard/reference-case measurements, not D33 dynamic authority. Verified counts/anchors/genesis and Council merits/quorum/recusal need contracts; founder honesty and voting independence cannot be inferred from graph labels. Human coalition costs remain H.
- **P46 — Growth/geometry × service scalability (R4/R6; M06/M10/M13).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate population growth, network reach and actual per-operator review resources; compare arrival/service rates and unresolved age. More members with rising backlog or a resource-cap censor is not scalable correction; test horizons and finite-size alternatives.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F18/F26 exploratory bottlenecks and H2's historical growth transient; no population-scale sponsorship proof. Owners: evidence/attention and authority/recovery/emergence controls.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E supports bounded scale/effort/gossip profiles; R is a two-opportunity slice, not an integrated growing queue. Define arrivals/route integration before a combined model. D33 verified topology and real human service capacity remain gated; extra labor is not a free network effect.
- **P47 — Admission scale × useful-work distribution (R4/R7; M06/M08/M14).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate genuine growth, fabricated/collusive entry and verified useful work under explicit support budgets. If identity growth attracts rewards without useful work or inherited-trust farms capture support, the proposed distribution return fails.
  - **Evidence/owner:** D32 intent and historical growth observations; no verified-contribution/distribution study. Owners: contribution/Atmo and bootstrap denominator.
  - **Reference/gate — D/H:** E's subject/emission counts are not contribution or demand. Need attribution/verifier/application funding and bootstrap independence/acquisition contracts before this full contrast exists. Human usefulness and fabrication costs remain H; do not invent contribution-weighted emission.
- **P56 — Procedures/attention × finality/load (R5/R6; M04/M05/M09/M10/M12).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Separate notification, review work, entry rate, conflict refusal and finality; report unresolved stages and closure reasons. Added attention that only moves congestion or shifts closures to experimental caps defeats a capacity/fairness interpretation. Compare wrongful and correct cases, not only resolved latency.
  - **Evidence/owner:** F17–F21 exploratory/diagnostic ladder findings and F26 relay. Owners: evidence/attention, Council/safeguards and timed-game controls. I6 adds observation quality.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E has `noticeBase`, `roundsPerDay`, windows and scan/round termination references; R is one-step and must not be spliced into that ladder by assumption. Council conflicts/quorum/appeals and full route integration need design. Cap closures are not timeouts or computational censoring; real attention remains H.
- **P57 — Adjudicator incentives × contribution truth (R5/R7; M07/M08/M12/M15).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Compare honest versus conflicted contribution verification, including coordinated real people, stronger newcomer evidence and correction of bad precedents. Reliable identity with profitable collusive attribution or blocked appeal defeats a broad accountability-distribution claim.
  - **Evidence/owner:** D32/precedent hypotheses and access diagnostics, not a measured contribution tribunal. Owners: contribution/Atmo and Council/safeguards.
  - **Reference/gate — D/H:** No scoped contribution tribunal, verifier-conflict or reasoned-appeal API exists. Specify jurisdiction, metric, evidence, payment and correction before modeling; useful-work ground truth/application validity remain independently justified or H. Adjudication wins do not authenticate contribution.
- **P67 — Resource continuity × support dependence (R6/R7; M06/M08/M10).**
  - **Contrast/falsifier:** Hold inherited state fixed while removing/restoring explicitly funded support; distinguish actual service loss, delayed correction and irreversible harm. Recovery only after assumed free external labor/funds defeats self-sustaining-core claims; a compensated scenario must retain its actual funding cost.
  - **Evidence/owner:** D32 intent and diagnostic resumption; no external-support sustainability finding. Owners: contribution/Atmo and authority/recovery.
  - **Reference/gate — N/D/H:** E/R can vary hypothetical availability/effort, not execute a subsidy market. Define payer/resources, finite continuation, pause/restart and useful-service outputs for the stronger design. Real support costs, price and demand remain H; assumed capacity is not funded application benefit.

## Within-root interaction inventory

These W records preserve detailed alternatives not discharged by the 21 pairs. They are unregistered design contrasts, not additional task owners or tested interactions.

- **W1 — R1: reuse/freshness × detection error × source dependence/privacy.** Distinguish artifact-rule diagnostics from oracle accuracy; a second source label is not corroboration. Equal-quality duplicate-source and disclosure-cost controls are required. Owners: media clarification/oracle sensitivity/human calibration; no detector calibration exists.
- **W2 — R2: liquid capital × overlapping promises × debt/recovery priority.** Separate reserve reuse, nominal penalties, actual collection and unpaid duties; a funded winner is not automatically compensated harm. Owners: stake selection/D34 recovery; F24/F35 are bounded diagnostics, not selected priority or deterrence.
- **W3 — R3: relation-earned XP × beliefs/exit options × prospective exposure.** Compare reciprocal selection, timely reporting, pause and concealment without counting sunk work twice. Owner: A3/A4 guarantee-selection/alignment; D34/pause choices and real preferences remain gated.
- **W4 — R4: anchor × edge projection × verified independence/epoch/genesis.** D36 vertex disjointness does not resolve the other fields. Owner: D33; F36 preparation is complete, so naming this interaction does not authorize more permutations or migration.
- **W5 — R5: individual XP gate × referral × conflicts/quorum/finality.** Separate admission, merits, voting and enforcement; market/jury variants need their own explicit contracts. Owner: Council/safeguards/procedural attrition; current references do not select those institutions.
- **W6 — R6: arrivals × service/notice × deadlines/restoration.** Keep pending stages, processing cost, cap closures and true timeouts distinct. Owner: evidence/attention/recovery; two-opportunity diagnostics do not settle long-run queues or human availability.
- **W7 — R7: contribution metric × attribution/verification × funded demand.** Compare false contribution, honest low-demand work and collusive reward claims without redefining personhood. Owner: contribution/Atmo; metric, actual externality and funding remain unspecified/unmeasured.

## Bounded discriminator: service-order sensitivity (P16)

**Unregistered exploratory design, not a mechanism confirmation.** F26 leaves opportunity/target priority uncontrolled. The minimal next contrast asks whether its finite-service allocation depends on which already packaged allegation reaches review first, without new evidence, investigators or protocol eligibility. This is chosen for an explicit existing control gap, low policy ambiguity and possible genuine burden, not because a positive result is expected or because P23/other branches are rejected. The [backlog](../BACKLOG.md#integrated-research-programme-d39) alone owns execution.

The [completed bounded study](../studies/2026-10-24-service-order/README.md) retains source-locked inputs, 432 raw runs and descriptive results; it does not turn this exploratory design into confirmation. The original comparison specification follows.

- **Intervention/control:** Extend only the existing relay example's options with a validated permutation of the two package-processing targets. Discover/package in canonical Mira/Aster order, supply identical per-subject/stage draws, then process packages in Mira/Aster versus Aster/Mira order. Resolve opened cases in the same canonical order in both arms. Hold the funded prefix, subject XP/wallets, work allocation, two opportunities and hours 72–96 fixed. Do not reorder discovery/settlement together and attribute the composite to review capacity.
- **Declared contexts/bound:** The existing three `RELAY_CONTEXTS`, all three `RELAY_ARMS`, two service orders and 24 sequential fresh exploratory labels `d39-order-explore-00` through `d39-order-explore-23`: **432 experimental runs**, not a population sample or confirmation, with `traceLimit: 64` and dropped-record counts retained (any dropped refusal evidence remains missing). Capacity-two with shared error is untested; no full capacity × correlation interaction claim. Tests/default-preservation checks are separate from those experimental replicas.
- **Outcomes/denominators:** Per paired world retain opportunities, latent evaluation inputs, correction/missed violation, wrongful invalidation, genuine XP/collected-money burden, review/case refusals with reasons, work and pending clocks through the declared horizon. Report paired differences and descriptive uncertainty/count denominators; retain zero/adverse/inconclusive outcomes without seed replacement. Genuine burden is not automatically wrongful liability; debt is not recovery.
- **Named alternatives:** Review-capacity allocation, exhausted work/liquid stake, strict XP access and settlement-order effects are distinct. Fix discovery and settlement order; retain operation/precondition traces to distinguish the remaining denials. A capacity-two difference is evidence to inspect financial/work/access alternatives, not permission to assert capacity invariance or a pure review-capacity mechanism.
- **Required controls before interpretation:** Preserve default example outputs except documented provenance; member versus relay stays resource/quality-matched within each service order, and unrouted evidence opens no cases. Both orders use the same opportunity sets, signals/verdict inputs and budgets. A single packaged-target control isolates ordering from arbitrary metadata; validate both permutations and reject malformed orders. Trace saturation/inert reads must not change semantics or draw keys.
- **Evidence/funding boundary:** All economic effects use FacetyModel through the existing relay; reserved backing is an unselected reference. Subject XP is not pooled into an operator. Work units/accuracy are supplied, adjudication work is abstracted, and no human retention, new sponsor payment, useful contribution, D34 correction or D33 runtime claim is made. Keep keyed draws unchanged; changed key layout/generator would require a new randomness identifier.
- **Artifacts/stop:** Retain a source/design-hashed input manifest, fresh raw paired runs, default/noninterference validation and an exploratory report in one dated study; leave historical files untouched. Commit the concrete source/design before fresh execution and run native tests for behavior changes. Stop/reconcile if matching/default controls fail, if a new protocol choice is needed, or at the declared bound. A later mechanism claim would need its own supported registration/confirmation path; these labels/results are not retrospectively registered.
