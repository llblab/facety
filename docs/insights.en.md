# Insight register

This register keeps design reasoning and findings with their sources and evidence level. The [protocol](facety.protocol.en.md) owns current rules; the [decision log](decisions.en.md) owns provenance. Findings are conditional on their build, scenario, horizon and valuations. None measures real-world behavior.

- **Confirmed:** supported by reserved-seed tests within the registered scope, or by a named deterministic check for a software claim.
- **Exploratory:** a grid, screen or game that suggests a contrast, not a confirmation.
- **Diagnostic:** a targeted example or check that isolates a behavior without estimating its prevalence.

The earlier grown-network comparisons use a grown network of 1000 accounts, difficulty D = 4, an 11-seat Council, honest behavior at the stated rates, 10 cooperating operators unless noted, and detection on. Detail and tables: [grown-network study](../studies/2026-10-30-grown-network/README.md). Items 2 to 8 were first measured with the rule set before D131, D133, D134 and D136 (see the study page for the later baseline used there); the ordering of the rules, and the finding that detection precision is the main honest cost (item 5), changed in size, not in direction: with cheap proof of reality (D136) invalidated genuine accounts fall from about 57 to about 3 per 60 days, so the remaining honest cost is the time spent in spurious disputes and the paths they remove (D131). Unless stated otherwise, the findings are diagnostic within the sampled scenario family.

1. **Detection is part of every attack simulation (obvious once stated).** With no detection nothing opposes a cluster of fakes, so a no-detection arm carries no information. The research variable is the design of detection, not its presence. (D125, [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md))
2. **Width and depth alone do not stop a cluster.** A guarantor count of D and D disjoint paths of at most 7 nodes leave 9% to 43% of accounts to 10 operators after 60 days (mean 25% over 20 seeds; 60% with no bound, one seed). Vertex-disjointness is counted per issuer, so thousands of cone members share the same few bridges to the honest network.
3. **The 7-node bound costs honest growth nothing and is justified by verification cost, not by security.** Days to 1000 honest accounts are identical with and without it on 12 seeds; 5 nodes also cost nothing, 4 nodes about 4 days and 3 nodes 29 days (one seed). The bound exists to cap on-chain traversal (D123).
4. **Layered support plus detection contains the cone.** Requiring every path intermediary to have D supported guarantors closer to the Council (D124) leaves 10 operators at 0.4% to 0.7% of accounts and about 0.01% of FCTY after 60 days (3 seeds on the retired simulator, 20 on the Rust one), against the cone strategy, an honest-first adaptive attacker and a supported-first attacker (to 120 days on 3 seeds, one year on 12). Cost to honest growth: about 3 days to reach 1000 accounts.
5. **Detection false positives are the main honest cost.** At a 1% false-positive rate with each partner inspected 10% of days, about 1000 genuine accounts are invalidated per year and the honest network stalls near 2 to 3 thousand; in 60-day sweeps honest growth recovers from 1888 to 2250-2400 accounts when the false-positive rate falls to 0.3%-0.1%, and the cone stays contained (later year-long false-positive sensitivity is recorded in item 10). Detection must keep spurious cases rare.
6. **Excluding disputed accounts from paths (D131) costs honest growth in proportion to the spurious-case rate.** After 60 days with inspection at 10% of partners per day: 42% fewer honest accounts at a 1% false-positive rate, 11% fewer at 0.3%, 2% fewer at 0.1%. Containment is unchanged (0.5% of accounts either way). The rule is cheap only with a precise detector.
7. **Caution toward strangers helps only if the look is informative.** With the plain 7-node bound the cone share falls from 29% to 9% when a stranger look has sensitivity 0.8 and false-positive rate 0.01, but only to 23% at 0.3 and 0.05; poor looks cost 7 to 14 days of honest growth. Layered support already contains the cone, so caution is a secondary lever.
8. **Operators are people, not a cone.** 100 operators over a year hold about 3% of accounts and of FCTY under layered support; they add no fake multiplier.

9. **Under the baseline the cone never forms, even with many or patient operators.** One year: 10, 30 and 100 operators hold 0.09%, 0.29% and 0.95% of accounts and at most 1.5% of FCTY; patient (120 days) and honest-first operators do no better; two years with 30 operators the same (diagnostic, 3 to 20 seeds). The attacker share is the operators divided by the honest accounts: the defense turns the sybil multiplier into one account per operator. Without layered support the same scenario leaves 31.7% of accounts at 60 days.
10. **Honest growth stalled near 10,000 active accounts in this historical model (diagnostic; the false-positive load is a main cause, see the end of this entry).** When the difficulty D (digits of the active count) reaches 5, invitations need five disjoint paths of at most seven nodes to five different seats of an 11-seat Council; the Council-path gate then dominates the refusals and growth stops at about 10,100 accounts. Not an attacker effect. Levers compared at 500 days (4 seeds, 10 operators): baseline 10,482 honest accounts; path bound 9 instead of 7: 12,018; difficulty capped at 4: 12,422; owned-slot ceiling 300: 10,673; 21 Council seats from 100 active accounts: 17,715. The attackers stayed at 10 accounts and at most 0.1% of FCTY in all of them. The plateau is set mainly by the Council size against D (an 11-seat Council cannot supply five disjoint short paths), and the slot ceiling is not the cause (diagnostic). Council size against 100 operators over 500 days: 11 seats 10,324 honest accounts (attacker share 0.95%, dirty FCTY 0.92%), 21 seats 16,724 (0.60%, 0.59%), 31 seats 33,134 (0.30%, 0.43%), 51 seats 66,592 (0.15%, 0.31%): more seats grow the network several times larger and lower the attacker share at once, at the price of computation; a rule of seats at least a multiple of D gave 11,082 (3 per unit), 16,951 (4) and 24,917 (5, with attacker share 0.40% and dirty FCTY 0.55%): 6 per unit gave 32,491 and 8 per unit 49,887 (attacker share 0.31% and 0.20%); two years with 5 per unit gave 23,843, so each fixed rule has a plateau of its own, roughly 1,000 honest accounts per seat at D = 5. A Council that grows with D is a candidate rule; to remove the plateau at every power of ten the multiple has to grow with the network, which costs computation. The levers are candidates for network referendums (D144). Correction (diagnostic, 4 seeds, one year, 10 operators): the plateau is not set by the Council size alone. With the false-positive rate at 0 the honest network reaches 48,346 accounts, at 0.0003 19,619, at 0.001 11,292, at the baseline 0.003 10,373 and at 0.01 4,905; letting paths cross disputed accounts gives 35,294 at the baseline rate. Cases on genuine people lock accounts and, through the exclusion of disputed accounts from paths (D131), take intermediaries from the Council-path gate. One independent confirming look before a case opens removes almost all of those cases (2,950 to 16) and gives 38,496-39,244 accounts at the same attacker share; confirmation by the target's own partners starves in a fake cluster (attacker share 67.5% against 29.3% without layered support), confirmation by independent examiners does not (36.8%). Excluding only the accused party from paths (`--targets-only`) gives 31,068. Study: detection precision against growth (D153). Q20 was answered by D157/D164; these historical alternatives are not newly selected defaults.

11. **The baseline verdict holds in absolute terms (diagnostic).** One year, 10 to 100 operators: attacker-side accounts never exceed the operators (9.7, 29.3, 98.2), an operator mints about 250 to 280 FCTY a year (one Self-ID a day, as a participant does) and holds 130 to 160 of them, and the honest network is 0.98 to 1.00 of the attack-free control on the same seeds. The low shares are not a denominator effect. Study: [core closure](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#k1--the-metrics-measure-the-claim).
12. **Containment does not depend on the defense assumptions; the honest network does (diagnostic).** With 30 operators the dirty-FCTY share stays at most 0.67% across correlated examiner errors, a lower proof success, slower proof and less attention. The honest network is most sensitive to the time an accused person needs to prove reality (0.83 of the control at 14 days, 0.56 at 28), then to correlated errors (0.71 at 5% odd profiles with 30 times the rate); the proof success moves the number of wrongly invalidated people (16 to 138), not the network; less attention improves the network (inspection 0.003: 1.32 of the control) because the inspection itself is a cost through false cases. The mechanism is the dispute hold and the exclusion of disputed accounts from paths (D131): the Q20 variants remove the damage of a slow proof. Study: [K2](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#k2--hidden-assumptions-of-the-defense).
13. **False accusations do not starve the network but wrongly invalidate people in proportion (diagnostic).** Operators filing up to three false cases a day each reach the honest network at 1.03 to 1.05 of the control because the XP gate keeps accusers away from high-XP intermediaries and an accuser is held by its own case (its emission falls from 280 FCTY to a few FCTY a year); about 0.5% of false cases end in a wrongly invalidated person (158 people a year at the heaviest setting). This build predates D159's selected stakes and D166's soft backing; Q21 is answered. Study: [K3](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#k3--false-accusations-as-an-attack-on-the-availability-of-the-defense).
14. **The honest network recovers from an error burst without an extra mechanism (diagnostic).** A thirty-day burst with ten times the false-positive rate takes the network to a trough of 0.69 of the control about two weeks after the burst ends; it returns to 90% of the control 40 to 170 days after the burst ends (five of six seeds without remedies, six of six with appeal and link restoration, whose effect on the speed is inside the seed spread). Appeal and restoration (D151) are therefore not shown to be required for recovery. Study: [K4](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#k4--recovery-after-a-burst-of-errors).
15. **The frozen strongest adversary does not break the criterion on reserved seeds (confirmation in the stated bounds, diagnostic in the sense of AGENTS).** 100 operators, supported-first, patience 120 days, 20% bought honest accounts: 12 of 12 reserved seeds pass both D129 lines at one year (dirty-FCTY share at most 1.66%, attacker share at most 0.93%), as does the 100-operator reference (12 of 12, at most 1.49%); two years: 12 of 12 (dirty-FCTY share at most 1.54%, attacker share at most 0.89%). Study: [K5](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#k5--break-attempts-and-reserved-seed-confirmation).

16. **The verdict survives other honest connectivity regimes (diagnostic).** Sparse, dense, stranger-friendly and stranger-averse honest behavior with 30 and 100 operators for a year: the D129 lines hold in all twelve configurations (dirty-FCTY share at most 2.33%, in the sparse regime with 100 operators), attacker-side accounts never exceed the operators, and the honest network is 0.92 to 1.02 of its paired control. Sparse connectivity is the weakest regime. Study: [honest regimes](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#honest-behavior-regimes-backlog-item-diagnostic).

17. **Social recovery (D135) works but is slow and incomplete in the scenario (diagnostic).** At key-loss rates of 5% to 50% a year, about two thirds of the recoveries that start finish, in a month on average, and the D129 lines are unaffected; only 40% to 49% of lost accounts are recovered within the year, and neither a higher slot ceiling nor certain vouching moves the funnel (the new account paying one right per guarantee is a plausible but unverified cause). With 20% to 30% bought accounts 0.5% to 4% of lost accounts could be captured through recovery (counted, not executed). Study: [key loss and recovery](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#key-loss-and-social-recovery-d135-scenario-diagnostic).

18. **Excluding only the accused from paths triples the honest network at the same containment (D157, diagnostic).** One year, 4 seeds: honest accounts with 30 operators 11,330 to 31,886, honest emission 0.86 to 2.82 million FCTY, dirty-FCTY share 0.20% to 0.18% (30 operators) and 0.77% to 0.62% (100), the cone without layered support 27.0% to 24.9%; the reserved-seed confirmation of the re-frozen adversary passes 36 of 36 runs. A fixed dispute window of 3, 5, 7 and 14 days gives 41,321, 34,863, 31,886 and 26,306 honest accounts. Study: [D157 re-check](../studies/2026-11-06-core-closure/README.md#re-check-under-the-softer-d131-d157).

19. **Crediting XP at maturity instead of at spending changes nothing that matters for containment, and the retroactive-XP exploit is closed by construction (maturity: diagnostic, exploit: tested).** One year, 4 seeds: honest accounts within seed noise of the spend-credit baseline (control 38,970 against 33,610, per-seed spread 31 thousand to 50 thousand), dirty-FCTY share 0.17% and 0.55% with 30 and 100 operators against 0.18% and 0.62%. Hoarders earn XP without spending, so Council rankings differ and the prefix to 1000 accounts takes about 10 days longer. The relation that becomes active after a Self-ID never earns XP from it, by named regression tests for an invitation and for a valence that turns mutual later. (D163, [study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md))
20. **Legacy XP balance halving with single-look examiners reduced honest growth to about a third (confirmed on reserved seeds, conditional; the channel is only partly identified).** Attack-free control, seeds 3001-3004, one year: 12.4 to 12.8 thousand honest accounts with the full ladder against 34 to 50 thousand without the halving (ratio 2.7 to 4.0 on every seed); stakes, custody and joining behavior are not the cause (ablations within 1%). Sparing Council members from the halving recovers 43% to 62% of the no-halving count and cuts Council turnover by two thirds, so Council churn is a channel but not the whole explanation (the registered claim that it is the channel was killed on seed 3004). One independent confirming look before a case opens (unselected, D153) removes the loss (1.13 to 1.48 times the no-halving count, about 25 cases a year). Q24 is answered by D164: halving stays; item 26 studies the examiner interpretation. ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md), D159)
21. **Stakes make false accusation a cost and leave containment intact (exploratory; containment confirmed on reserved seeds).** Operators who hold no liquid FCTY cannot open any false case (10,640 refused first stakes a year with 30 operators); operators who convert every emission open about 400 a year, lose 7 FCTY and half their XP each time the accused proves reality, and leave the honest network unchanged (12,390 against 12,450 accounts), where the abstraction without stakes allowed 10,430 accusations a year and 355 wrongly invalidated people. The frozen strongest adversary stays at a dirty-FCTY share of 0.81% to 0.89% and an attacker share of about 0.2% on 4 of 4 reserved seeds under the full ladder. (D159, D129)
22. **The three-day window floor is not a duration bound (diagnostic, closed form plus engine test).** An adversary with a chain of eligible accounts that enters in the last hour before each deadline holds an accused person for 21.9 days with 3 entries, 42.6 with 10, 72.2 with 20 (last stake 269 FCTY, 1,587 FCTY locked); the Council's terminal entry ends the escalation, so with a Council member looking at a contested dispute with probability 0.3 per day the mean is 14 days whatever the chain length. That mean depends on uncalibrated attention in the earlier binary-vote model. Current [Council rounds](facety.protocol.en.md#rounds) can defer repeatedly; terminal entry does not guarantee settlement. (D162, D161)
23. **The ladder's guarantor charge scales with guarantor counts, not with the stake.** In this build, each current guarantor of a losing accused person is charged 7 FCTY for burning; accounts have dozens of mutual guarantors (Council members 70 to 90), so one wrongful invalidation burns on the order of 200 FCTY of honest guarantors, 17,641 FCTY for 88 wrongful invalidations in one seed-year of the control. Diagnostic for that build only: current [identity-case liability](facety.protocol.en.md#settlement) uses actual historical receipt recipients, not the current graph.

24. **After a burst of false positives the honest network recovers under the ladder, deeper and slower than under the abstract disputes (exploratory).** Thirty days at ten times the false-positive rate, four seeds: trough 0.45 to 0.72 of the paired control, back to 90% 77 to 222 days after the burst ends (4 of 4 within the year); with one confirming look before a case opens the burst leaves no trace. ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md), D141)

25. **The stress panel under the ladder keeps both D129 lines in all nine variants and shows the honest cost drivers (exploratory).** 30 operators, one year, 4 seeds: attacker share at most 0.31%, dirty-FCTY share at most 0.29%; honest accounts 12.1 to 13.4 thousand across joining rates, Council attention, proof weakening, odd-profile examiners, cash-out and key loss; sparse and dense honest regimes and Council attention up to 1.0 per day keep both lines too (honest network 0.95 to 1.02 of the paired control); a slower proof of reality is the largest cost (63 to 398 genuine invalidations, 9.9 FCTY lost per honest account-year); the ladder halves the top of the XP distribution and triples Council turnover. Corrupt examiners are modeled as absent, which is why the bought-account variant grows to 45.6 thousand. ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md))

26. **With examiners who check twice before filing, the selected ladder and halving cost honest growth nothing and containment holds (registered on reserved seeds, conditional on examiner behavior).** Seeds 4001-4004, one year: honest accounts 3.0 to 4.8 times those of single-look examiners (36 to 58 thousand), no genuine person invalidated in the control against 56 to 93, formal cases about 13 a year, and the frozen strongest adversary at a dirty-FCTY share of 0.77% to 1.11% and an attacker share of at most 0.27%. This answers finding 20 as the owner (D164) read it: the loss came from examiners filing on one noisy look; the halving rule is unchanged. Unverified: real examiner behavior. ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md))

27. **XP based on responsibility units and integrity (D165) keeps containment and makes a penalty recoverable (registered on reserved seeds, conditional on examiner behavior).** Seeds 5001-5004: the frozen strongest adversary stays at a dirty-FCTY share of 0.80% to 1.02% and an attacker share of at most 0.23%; with single-look examiners 84% to 90% of the roughly 1,500 accounts that lost integrity have recovered part of it within a year (1/1000 per own Self-ID), yet honest growth stays near 12 thousand accounts, so the slow restoration does not rescue careless examiners; with two-look examiners growth is 21 to 63 thousand accounts (1.8 to 5.1 times the single-look control; the claim of at least 2 times on every seed missed on one seed at 1.77 and was not killed). ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md), D165)

28. **With soft stake backing false accusations are limited by the XP gate and integrity, not by funds, and the base stake changes the attackers' debt but not the number of cases (exploratory).** 30 operators accusing daily with half their emissions cashed out, seeds 1-4, one year: 3,741 to 3,758 cases opened against about 400 under liquid-only backing, attacker debt 25 thousand, 51 thousand and 102 thousand FCTY at base stakes 7, 14 and 28 (the debt is repaid by burning their emission), honest network 87% to 99% of the control, 11 genuine people invalidated a year. The model allowed soft backing at opening. Under current [Filing](facety.protocol.en.md#filing), only later ordinary positions can enter without full funds. (D166, [study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md))

29. **The formed baseline (D162-D167) passes the registered containment and growth lines on reserved seeds, except one false-accusation seed (registered, conditional on the examiner and joining assumptions).** Seeds 6001-6004: the frozen strongest adversary at a dirty-FCTY share of 0.75% to 1.28% and an attacker share of at most 0.32%; control honest accounts 3.6 to 4.8 times those of single-look examiners with 1 genuine person invalidated a year against 54 to 70; under daily false accusations the honest network is 0.78 to 1.18 of the control, so the registered 80% line was crossed on one seed (6001, 0.78) and that claim was killed. ([study](../studies/2026-11-07-maturity-and-ladder/README.md))

30. **Conditional containment does not supply independent evidence or finite corrective service (D168, diagnostic).** Corrected mechanics `e9b0515`, identical-core invoker `0b90e6e`: all 18 local and 20 final population runs finish, the four required final arms each on reserved seeds 8001-8004. Every final endpoint passes D129 (worst dirty 0.040191%, minimum clean-account fraction 93.9752%), with no false accounts accepted. Default-attack honest issuance is 0.552-0.897 of matched control; joint/default-control issuance is 0.011-0.077, with headcount effects varying by seed and severe environment damage even without attackers. Controlled repeated looks show that rereading the same evidence loses independent-error reduction; human joint errors remain unmeasured. Under the earlier soft-opening rule a zero-backed losing accuser pays a winning defender zero and abandonment leaves debt unpaid; a continuing mistaken examiner repays 7 FCTY of debt by day 14 but restores integrity only on day 507. A qualified 21-position late ladder lasts 72 days 4 hours with no collected coalition forfeits under zero backing (42 preparation FCTY remain real costs); a one-case Council queue leaves two of three cases unserved and one innocent accused losing. Recovery of a wrongly invalidated accused requires an explicitly admitted appeal. These are conditional transition witnesses, not human incidence estimates. The block is the unverified premise that independent information and willing timely defense/appeal will be available and financed by nominal stakes. Actual providers, finite workload, evidence access and voluntary incentives need an explicit next-stage decision (Q25), not a new selected rule or more synthetic matrices. No profitable-honesty, universal resistance or debt-bound claim follows. Source: [final report, mechanism table and native evidence](../studies/2026-11-08-evidence-core/README.md). ## Design discussion reconciliation (2026-10-23)

These are hypotheses and design interpretations, not findings. The [dated A–T discussion](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8a9d0f52739b03c35a4b769a628d46edeefa70e0/docs/insights.en.md#design-discussion-reconciliation-2026-10-23) retains individual arguments, original references and each falsifier. [Research](research.en.md) owns current comparison designs.

- **A–E: earned influence.** Guarantees create work and exposure worth rewarding, but high XP does not prove accuracy, timeliness or better evidence. Earned early advantage is not immunity; non-transferability does not prevent bribery, coercion or controlled keys. Distinguish public history from actually reviewed evidence, and sunk work from plausible future value. Compare decisions on equal evidence and test whether concealment can remain preferable.
- **F–I: exit and correction.** The legacy exit test charged a fee and removed credit; [current exit](facety.protocol.en.md#valences-and-guarantees) is free and preserves units for unrefuted acts. Test future exposure and remaining watchers separately from past liability. Distinguish usable self-correction, guarantor prevention, public challenge, Council action and appeal. Rotation and nominal eligibility are not evidence of access or good judgments.
- **J–N: routes to action.** A motivated minority may serve many people, but bottlenecks can move from notice to qualified, willing and funded response. Outside sources may bypass local collusion without gaining entry rights. Private compensation must name a payer, work, funds and collection risk. Count distinct information rather than copied reports or presumed source loyalty.
- **O–T: scale and capture.** Compare dense genuine and collusive groups under matched resources; neither density nor gossip proves a defense. Keep money, XP, decisive authority, wrongful decisions and actual obstruction separate. Test whether violations make future correction harder, and whether restored ordinary conditions can reverse that effect. Reasoned precedent is a comparison, not a doctrine; compare equal evidence across insider and newcomer status without assuming precedents cannot entrench mistakes.

## Design-analysis insights

A2–A7 are design arguments from 2026-10-23; A8 is a dated software comparison; A9 and A10 are owner rationales. None selects a rule or demonstrates an economic outcome.

**A2 — ANALYSIS, money and authority accrue differently.** A subject's [own emission](facety.protocol.en.md#recording-a-self-id) is limited by its rolling interval; [units](facety.protocol.en.md#credit-and-revocation) accrue from eligible own and guaranteed acts, so participants with more active guarantees can gain more XP. The fairness argument about per-person issuance does not imply equal procedural authority or equal wealth from transfers. Distinguish the burn to create an offer from a premium for accepting future risk. A9 gives the design rationale; the guarantee-selection comparison tests behavior, not the arithmetic.

**A3 — ANALYSIS, fixed credit need not price historical exposure.** A qualifying maturity credits one unit per eligible guarantor, not necessarily one XP. [Event-time duties](facety.protocol.en.md#responsibility) survive exit, and the violating act's creation guarantors in a non-author Self-ID correction can face a charge that grows with the recorded suffix until filing. Collection can be less than the charge. Leaving is free and retains units for unrefuted acts, so the older flat-premium argument based on increasing exit cost applies only to the [legacy rules](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8a9d0f52739b03c35a4b769a628d46edeefa70e0/docs/insights.en.md#design-analysis-insights). Compare reciprocal and low-uncertainty pair selection, leaving and remaining oversight with matched resources. F1, F10, F11 and F21 motivate the comparison but do not measure current willingness or show that repricing is necessary.

**A4 — ANALYSIS, any liability-off mode needs a forward-only cutoff.** A proposed pause in duties could remove a watcher just when a guarantor suspects trouble. Compare it with ordinary exit and preserve past liability. Bind notice, delay, duties during notice and effects on the active count. F11 highlights a historical trade-off, not a finding under a selected pause mode.

**A5 — ANALYSIS, the media oracle's reliability is exogenous and the social layer's is endogenous.** The protocol already states that a photograph is not proof of unique personhood, and the owner's formulation extends this: the face reference is a fulcrum or anchor, not the proof itself. The village analogy supporting it holds where the population is closed and small, recognition is continuous and multimodal (gait, voice, context, history) and impersonation requires physical co-presence; Facety instead faces an open global population, single-shot artifacts and generative attackers, so the anchor supplies continuity of presentation over time rather than uniqueness, and its false-accept rate is set by oracle capability that improves adversarially. The falsifiable form: either protocol conclusions are insensitive to the declared face accuracy range — which supports the owner's claim that guarantees and history carry the budget — or they are sensitive, which makes the media layer load-bearing and requires a stated degradation path. Test recorded as the layer-sensitivity item in [BACKLOG.md](../BACKLOG.md).

**A6 — ANALYSIS, low-XP defense depends on available help.** Without Council entry, [the last ordinary side wins](facety.protocol.en.md#positions-and-the-clock), so lack of qualified response can decide a case despite good evidence. [Private sponsorship](facety.protocol.en.md#challenges) can help only if someone is willing and able to act and any promised payment is funded. Compare service, incentives and harassment risk rather than treating XP or posted evidence as access. F6 and F13 are historical probes; [Q25](../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-operational-evidence-and-finite-defense-after-closure) owns the unmeasured response question.

**A7 — ANALYSIS, price stabilization sits exactly on a D32 falsifier.** The owner proposes Treasury D buyback as the mechanism that stabilizes price over time. D32 states the thesis fails if retention depends only on assumed token prices or subsidies rather than demonstrated usefulness. Retention supported by external buyback capital therefore belongs to the falsifier side until tested: compare retained participation with and without bucket-D support under declared valuations. Already covered by the Atmo calibration item's subsidy-dependence acceptance in [BACKLOG.md](../BACKLOG.md); recorded here so the tension is not forgotten, without opening a second mandate.

**A8 — ANALYSIS from source inspection, with diagnostic software checks, 2026-10-24: equal one-day maturities remove the legacy fixture's own-XP timing premium under an earliest-use comparison.** `src/incentives.mjs` pins 72/168-hour maturity, zero invitation freeze and immediate acceptance in its legacy scenario; the retired `FacetyModel` counterpart uses 24-hour spend, conversion and invitation freeze by default. For the separately implemented [P23 counterpart](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/659ea60d16129eb6f4db4f9e774e3f2904efff74/docs/incentives.en.md#one-day-counterpart-contract-p23), a sponsor act recorded at 0 h is terminally disposed at 24 h both by earliest invitation and earliest waiting conversion, so the old day-3 versus day-7 own-XP timing difference cannot be inherited. Admission then waits until 48 h; a fresh newborn act at 48 h can convert at 72 h. Equal-hour admission/work need journal order, not invented sub-hour clocks or a D34 legal-event choice. The pre-data reporting design therefore retains both snapshots and does not estimate work-at-admission by an `atHour <= 48` filter, which would wrongly include later same-hour identity work. This conditional API/timing deduction is now checked by the [eight one-day software regression families and exact legacy preservation controls](../studies/2026-10-24-one-day-incentives/README.md), not a new empirical/mechanism claim, calibrated payoff, actual preference or retention finding. F45 now retains freshly reconstructed diagnostic tables/thresholds without copying or reclassifying historical outputs; expected future losses remain unknown despite no scripted debt/verdicts. The bounded one-day port/report is complete; the existing guarantee-selection/alignment owners retain the stronger choice and liability questions. No new reward/liability rule or general attack/benefit result is selected.

**A9 — OWNER DESIGN RATIONALE (D194, 2026-11-17).** Unequal procedural authority is intentional: sustained participation and guarantee duties can build XP but money cannot buy it directly. Ranking favors active builders while [Council votes](facety.protocol.en.md#rounds) are equal. Historical responsibility and correction make influence answerable, not immune. This accepts the gradient of procedural power, not the claim that seniority proves personhood, sanction collection or checking. F4's vigilance premise and D168's REVISE still matter. Source: [decision log](decisions.en.md#decision-log-and-supersession-ledger), A2 and A3.

**A10 — OWNER DESIGN RATIONALE (D200, 2026-11-19).** A voluntary registry of people who have lived has authority over reality and rule compliance, not moral worth. A false identity verdict against a real person is a central harm; [appeal and restoration](facety.protocol.en.md#appeal) are the intended remedy, not evidence that help arrives. Funded filing is intended to filter confidence; affordable funding or sponsorship is not established. The economy is intended to fund a durable public archive, but proofs that survive one chain need [their own contract](../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q30-registry-continuity-and-archive). Source: [decision log](decisions.en.md#decision-log-and-supersession-ledger). No new rule or empirical claim.

The response of sanctions, investigator compensation, fabrication technology, useful services and Treasury D policy needs a separate conditional model. A guaranteed inverse price/security relation, guaranteed application value, and a policy that sanctions must scale with price do not follow. The Bitcoin analogy is explanatory, not empirical validation of Facety.

The [fixture contract](simulator.en.md#appeal-fixture-contract) owns the implemented path and its omissions. Active withdrawal is not an open appeal finance question. [Q25-C](../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-c-admitted-appeal-and-exact-restoration) owns the remaining contracts.

A lookup cache keyed only to graph changes could reject paths through newly unblocked accounts. The [path tests](../simulator/src/paths.rs) compare fresh and reused searches after a snapshot changes. The correction distinguishes runner-v5 from frozen earlier outputs; it does not revalidate their conclusions.

## Method lessons

- Random scripted runs cannot model incentives. Use strategy menus, payoff facts and equilibrium or deviation analysis (see the [historical game method](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/659ea60d16129eb6f4db4f9e774e3f2904efff74/docs/games.en.md)).
- A strategy menu that is too small misleads: adding `farm-and-harass` reversed the apparent safety of inaccurate verdicts.
- Check valuation traps: debt left on abandoned fakes is not a cost to the attacker (`falseDebt: +1`).
- Exploratory candidate counts are not discoveries (228 candidates, two of them confirmed).
- Confirm on reserved seeds before calling anything a mechanism; record what was not tested.
