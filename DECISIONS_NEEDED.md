# Open choices and research assumptions

The [protocol](docs/facety.protocol.en.md) owns selected rules, the [simulator contract](docs/simulator.en.md) owns coverage, and [BACKLOG](BACKLOG.md) owns work. The [decision log](docs/decisions.en.md) and the [previous register](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8a9d0f52739b03c35a4b769a628d46edeefa70e0/DECISIONS_NEEDED.md) preserve answered questions, superseded proposals and their original assumptions. The research assumptions below do not select protocol defaults.

## Q25: operational evidence and finite defense after closure

What independent information, willing qualified response before cutoff, and admitted proof or appeal can people supply when a correct position earns little or nothing? A funded monetary opening does not ensure backing for later positions. Specify actual voluntary work, finite capacity and private valuations. An abandoned account need not have positive value; no supplied rate demonstrates willingness or service.

The [evidence-core report](studies/2026-11-08-evidence-core/README.md) retains D168's REVISE verdict: an earlier soft-funded opening paid the winning defender zero; a one-case Council queue left two of three cases unserved and an innocent person losing; a same-source reread removed the modeled independent-look benefit. Sampled containment did not prevent degraded honest activity. These are conditional results, not evidence that those frequencies hold in use. The [operating proposal](docs/operational-defense.en.md) supplies no work commitments. The prepared exercise was never run and produced no observations.

### Q25-A: claim, evidence and admission authority

What canonical record and authorized procedure admit a specific existence, duplicate or act allegation? Bind target, duty, evidence access and recognition, reply, signatures, admission and execution. An informal report is not a position; a meeting can address presence without deciding uniqueness or another act. Do not add a compulsory second examiner or an application veto. Under [Filing](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#filing) and [Cancellation and activation](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#cancellation-and-activation), bind the opening veto roster, membership changes, collective authorship and precedence between a still-valid Self-ID veto and a separate pending dispute.

#### Q25-A1: first Self-ID fixture trust boundary

D193 authorized explicit trusted premises for admission, historical duty and upheld merits, not a production procedure. See the [implemented fixture](docs/simulator.en.md#self-id-transaction-fixture) and the [original authorization and bounded alternatives](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8a9d0f52739b03c35a4b769a628d46edeefa70e0/DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-a1-first-self-id-fixture-trust-boundary). The other choices in Q25-A and Q25-B are not settled by those checks.

#### Q25-A2: retrospective correction priority and funds

[Self-dispute](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#self-dispute) is author-first and concerns the same transaction. Which admitted event fixes priority: recording, activation or an earlier admission step? Order simultaneous claims and decide how rejected or cancelled earlier filings affect priority. Bind authorization, fees, funding, unavailable backing, dependent rights, prior debt repayments and repeat filings. Specify how to correct 1 FCTY when the source is converted, transferred, consumed or already burned; no second burn, implicit refund or power to erase another verdict follows.

### Q25-B: attributable execution and economic scope

Bind the applicable duty snapshot, participant roles, authenticated entry and ballots, and replay-safe execution. Under [Council](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#council), [Rounds](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#rounds) and [Positions and the clock](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#positions-and-the-clock), peer parties retain votes when in the electorate, Council entry uses the shared clock, and votes have no quorum or recusal. Still needed: member/referral/last-position snapshots, atomic entry and same-time ordering, duplicate referrals, ballot revision, evidence and reasons, execution of a verdict or extension exactly once, peer admission/XP and collective settlement, including reciprocal filings. Queue priority and funded human response are operational questions, not clock or voting rules.

Under [Settlement](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#settlement), bind invitation and opening merits amounts, destinations and penalty roles; the opening uses its own event-time guarantors. Define overlapping duties, multiple roles and repeat-event identity across successive cases. [Self-ID correction](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#self-id-correction) fixes the inclusive count at funded filing, not at activation or verdict; the identity-case charge and receipt roster are not substitutes. Retained [transaction records](docs/simulator.en.md#transaction-responsibility-records) identify distinct events and recipients, not liable-party qualification or authority.

#### Q25-B1: recoverable-source collection and cut order

Beyond the protocol's [one-act collection-before-cut case](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#collection-before-cut), what asset order applies to competing custody and to unavailable, transferred, burned or debt-used backing? Bind priority and repeat enforcement without assuming another party pays the shortfall. The [original collection-order comparison](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/0275f6964fdec9b0c8845776c6e98a41077fa3fd/DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-b1-recoverable-source-collection-and-cut-order) preserves the rejected burn-first arm for the isolated case; the simulator's wider convention is not a production answer.

### Q25-C: admitted appeal and exact restoration

Under [Appeal](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#appeal), which evidence, linked records and authenticated authorities admit and execute the appeal and fee-free repair of wrongfully cut links? Bind financial scope, stake and allocation, opening merits sanctions and transferred-money refunds without assuming a Treasury or free filing. A request is not a reversal and status alone is not restoration. The [core fixture](docs/simulator.en.md#appeal-fixture-contract) supplies authority and omits financial effects; the population runner supplies conditional approved reversals.

#### Q25-C1: recovery migration and collective authority

After key recovery, should an authorized correction flow to the successor with explicit attribution, or should the retired source's history stay frozen and only future effects be corrected? Bind how receipts, recalculated integrity, inherited caps and liabilities carry over without reviving the source, duplicating credit or rewriting unrelated events. The simulator refuses before mutation and retains the recognized reversal for retry; that witnesses the gap, not a migration rule.

Who can authenticate collective initiation, and how do a collective decision and the entering member's action map to individual holds, penalties and payments? Keep the XP-weighted network referendum separate, with its own proposal, snapshot and executor.

### Q25-D: actual voluntary work and permission for a session

D188 does not require a rehearsal or pilot before development and authorizes neither. Any future study needs its own question, design and authorization. Who would willingly take the organizer, observer and participant tasks, and with what limits, consent, data access, storage, retention and publication terms? A compressed fictional session could show steps people actually take, not seven-day availability, representative behavior, real economic motivation or an executable answer to Q25-A–C. No staffing, funds or personal data are committed.

## Q26: dispute safety and arithmetic review

Do good-faith losing accusers need an exception to the ordinary integrity consequence, and if so what evidence and authority would establish it? None is implemented. Active withdrawal, its allocation and any associated shortfall are retired. [Dispute safety probes](docs/insights.en.md#q26-dispute-safety-probes) distinguish older rules from current custody and closure tests. Repeatable deferral and pre-verdict path exclusion can prolong restrictions or block uninvolved dependents; that is a conditional risk, not proof of guilt or grounds for a silent expiry.

### Q26-integrity-precision candidate

**OPEN, prepared as two labeled candidates; the default is unchanged.** The simulator default remains `millionths/penalty-f64-floor/own-maturity-1000/cap-one/v1`, stored in configurations and summaries. Candidates:

- **Exact:** halving and the +1/1000 repair over an exact rational coefficient, capped at one, with D165's final floor. Identifier `candidate/exact-rational-halving/own-maturity-1000/cap-one/final-floor/v1`. Its declared domain is a checked 120-bit numerator/denominator and at most 60 halvings; beyond that the reference reports a named limit (`TooManyHalvings`, `TermOverflow`) and never silently falls back to rounding, saturation or the grid. Those are reference limits, not proposed protocol limits.
- **Normative grid:** the existing millionths convention with `floor(coef * (1 - fraction))` after each penalty and a 1,000-millionth repair, i.e. the current implementation labeled as a normative-rounding rule rather than a deviation.

Deterministic divergence: 128 units and seven halvings give 1 XP with exact arithmetic and 0 XP on the grid; a ranking where the untouched single unit beats the deep holder under the grid ties under the exact rule; an ordinary gate of one is reached only under exact arithmetic. Capped chronological replay is required under both (the regression `1 -> 0.5 -> 300 repairs 0.8 -> 0.4` restores to 0.5, not 0.8). Evidence: `tests/integrity_precision.rs`. Neither candidate enters serialization, so no digest changes; if a candidate is later selected, its identity must join the digest and a new policy identity must not silently change default outcomes.

**One focused question:** *exact rational arithmetic with a declared representable domain, or the existing millionths grid stated as the normative rounding rule?*

The [prior review](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8a9d0f52739b03c35a4b769a628d46edeefa70e0/DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#kept-as-reporting-context-not-part-of-the-three-choices) retains the analysis of nominal stakes, unpriced harm and filing-delay incentives. A larger filing count raises the nominal charge, not necessarily collection.

## Q27: reference-kernel boundary and production ordering

Choose a canonical production order, record encoding, numeric and storage limits, and execution bounds. The [model scheduler](docs/simulator.en.md#suspended-self-id-maturity) and first-ballot convention do not select production order. This checkpoint is not extraction permission.

Two engineering approaches are available: a staged `core`/`alloc` transition subset behind a simulator adapter, or a larger World extraction. The staged boundary limits migration risk and supports event-by-event differential replay, but initially excludes more operations; the larger cut covers more interactions at greater coupling and verification cost. The proposal must name its event set, ordering version, fingerprint fields/encoding, state bounds and atomic rejection semantics before approval. A noncryptographic test fingerprint is not an authenticated ledger commitment; `no_std` compatibility alone proves no bounded runtime cost. Off-chain search and on-chain submitted-witness verification remain separate. No external crate or Substrate dependency is implied.

Collective initiation and individual effect attribution also need the contracts in [Q25-C1](#q25-c1-recovery-migration-and-collective-authority).

## Q28: launch observation records, identifiability and privacy

**OPEN protocol/instrumentation proposal.** Candidate records link case and target IDs, observation source/artifact/examiner references, timestamps, declared new-look/reread lineage, response events, queue admission/service and actual collection. Source labels or two signed observations do not establish independent information or distinct humans. Canonical encoding, who may assert/correct a record, retention/access and publication authority need binding before production; the simulator may exercise explicitly candidate synthetic records without selecting them.

Privacy alternatives are public detailed provenance (best direct auditability, highest correlation/harassment exposure), minimal public commitments with controlled off-chain disclosure (less immediate exposure, but availability/access and dispute verification duties), or aggregate public counters with separately governed case evidence (less detail, weaker independent reconstruction). Hashing linkable evidence is not anonymity; the existing public graph limits every option. No option is selected and no personal data is collected.

Identifiability limits must accompany every metric: evaluator-confirmed wrongful invalidations are synthetic truth, whereas history can directly count authorized reversals and recorded verdicts and pending cancellations, not all unappealed mistakes. A fresh-look flag describes provenance, not measured error independence. Defense latency needs separately recorded filing, activation and first qualifying response; zero-collection shares need declared case/position denominators and exclude returned principal. Council queue/workload counts require actual admission/service records; no response is not proof of unavailable human capacity or absence of unrecorded work. Missing records stay unknown. Tripwires are candidate reporting/intervention proposals, not owner harm tolerances or automatic pause authority.

#### Q28-C1: versioned record proposal (candidate, unimplemented)

**Compact proposal, indexed by record kind.** Five versioned kinds: *case* (case ID, kind, target reference, opening/activation/decision/execution times, outcome class, collection, returned principal, debt, denominators), *observation* (case, source artifact, examiner, time, declared fresh-look versus reread plus the referenced prior look), *response* (case, participant, role, filing/first-qualifying-response times, whether any response was recorded), *service* (queue admission, queue wait, service start/end, whether served), and *decision* (round, electorate, ballots cast, recognized result, executor). Timestamps never substitute for cause: a recording time is not a response time and an activation time is not a filing time. Values use explicit `missing`, `unknown`, `inapplicable` and `observed-zero`, so a zero denominator is distinguishable from an absent record.

Privacy alternatives stay open and are not implemented: detailed public provenance; minimal public commitments with governed off-chain disclosure; aggregate counters with separately governed case evidence. Each must state access, retention, correction authority and reconstruction limits. Provenance is not independence; authorized reversals do not reveal unappealed mistakes; and no recorded response does not prove that nobody worked on the case. No collection, service or personal-data processing is created by this proposal.

Real bootstrap capture probability cannot be obtained without a declared adversary and human model.

For the inverted-baseline packet, the owner fixes an adverse background and names counterfactual axes, not new rules. The remaining environment, attacker resources, horizon, build/configuration identity, fresh reserved seeds, per-run/aggregate budget, censoring, stop and kill rules must be committed before execution. V and bought-link price remain unpriced axes with no assigned honest-harm tolerance. One adverse environment cannot prove robustness over the entire unmeasured human range; an inconclusive surface is a valid deliverable, not permission to expand the matrix. Corrupt-examiner attacks and adversarial-search red teams remain uncommissioned until their owner-declared behavior/budget contracts exist.

## Q30: registry continuity and archive

The record may need to outlive its participants and its chain. Three questions remain for a later phase:

- **From living to lived.** How a participant's record passes from a living subject to a person who lived, and what then happens to its guarantees, owned slots, pending acts, debts and dispute exposure. Key recovery (D135) is not this transition.
- **Archive proofs beyond the chain.** The identification economy is expected to fund a public archive verifiable on the chain. To remain checkable after a particular chain stops, the archive should carry its own proofs (record hashes, block headers, inclusion paths); format, custody and funding are open.
- **Lasting voluntariness.** Self-ID records are dedicated to the public domain by a voluntary act (D186). How that lasting dedication relates to a choice made at one point in a life, and what it does not permit, is open; no erasure or withdrawal right is selected or excluded here.

## Q31: identity, admission and execution contracts

These are unresolved contracts from the protocol, not new implementation authority. The [protocol](docs/facety.protocol.en.md) owns the selected parts; [BACKLOG](BACKLOG.md#admission-and-remedies) owns follow-up.

- **Evidence and keys:** bind subject continuity and recovery authorization, required pre-registration fields, canonical media/detector and version support, freshness versus capture time, same-subject reuse at the shared boundary, lawful dedication and consent, retention/retraction, evidence access and replay after missing bytes. Distinguish attestation, available evidence and a decision on uniqueness. See also Q03, Q19 and Q30.
- **Rights and execution:** choose escrow or deferred mint and account for every terminal disposition, temporary reservations, already converted targets, source correction, dependent rights and invalidated or abandoned holdings. Do not select collection priority, temporal precedence or source recovery by storage choice. Bound execution, migration and indexing (Q25–Q27).
- **Admission and paths:** bind invitation target/authorization, recipient refusal, expiry, duplicates, closed-slot release and special-link mapping, and the initial Self-ID's units, chain-count and rolling-slot treatment. Preserve both slots at issuance and no second eligibility test at acceptance. Bind verification and independent support, genesis and activation, exact qualifying graph and path/member snapshots, activity after cancellations/disputes, adopted difficulty versus target and the XP-to-capacity schedule (Q17/Q29). Still decide whether an active dispute's accused member may end a counted Self-ID path ([Paths](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#paths) constrains transit; D157 excludes the accused from counted paths; the invitation model requires unblocked terminals). A Self-ID author's own seat never counts. Possible bootstrap age and exposure limits, including a separate issuer activity check beyond the selected invitation gates, are candidates, not defaults.
- **Duties and remedies:** define the historical chain endpoint and which cancelled, corrected or already adjudicated acts a new guarantee warrants, the evidence for fault, upstream roles, the cluster proof window and partial scope, and authority to restore wrongfully cut links. Disconnection and suspicious topology alone are not guilt. Bind peer Council admission and settlement and termination during an active case separately from the named restrictions.
- **Governance and publication:** bind referendum snapshots and execution, Atmo control electorates and pause boundaries (Q29), health measures, approved assets and budgets, actual FCTY buy-and-burn verification, recovery after asynchronous execution, and external proof disclosure. No extra Treasury, market backstop or punitive recovery fork is selected.
