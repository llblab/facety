## Question and fixed matrix

Does first-attended prevention in these supplied worlds depend on independent first-stage observation errors, and what genuine invitation harm/work accompanies a persistent target-specific common-mode signal? The [design](design.json), [capture runner](run.mjs), [case/measurement/dependency contract](contract.mjs) and [offline analyzer](analyze.mjs) are fixed before data.

- Exactly **64 planned seven-day/four-hour cases**: four fresh labels `d44-shared-evidence-01`…`04` × two descendant classes × two calendars × two evidence laws × two veto-effect arms. Four blocks of **16**; no extensions or seed search.
- Populations are `fabricated-descendants` (`sybil-cone`, shared-operator false descendants) and `genuine-coalition-descendants` (`real-cartel`, declared genuine recruited operators). Both start with twelve declared genuine trusted clique roots/three coalition operators. Coordination is not misconduct; operator labels are evaluator assumptions, not authenticated people.
- Attention is all ticks or `one-tick-phase-0`: initial subject index `i` attends at `24*day + 4*(i%6)`. IDs, not evaluator roles, define profiles. Transactions remain unrestricted. Honest invitation/general Self-ID inspection, descendant review and verification are disabled; the initial reviewer pool follows explicit profiles, not a human activity distribution.
- Timing stays `first-attended`, one trial per eligible guarantor/invitation/calendar-day, with supplied `activity.review: 1`. Only first-stage `independent` versus `shared-target` changes. Sensitivity 0.8/FPR 0.01 are **nominal single-look** quality, not matched received bits: mode switches use different keys even at a matching first-look time. Different target IDs/graphs after effect divergence are not necessarily the same later opportunities.
- Shared-target is one persistent first-stage signal per public target across reviewers/times. It is an extremal object-specific common-mode counterfactual, not shared errors across all media/targets/observers, an adversarial oracle or calibrated covariance. Ordinary observation/procedure references stay separate; disabled verification supplies no fallback after negative first-stage evidence.
- `observe-only` removes only an otherwise-authorized veto effect; `veto` retains it. Both pay inspection/response work and preserve authority/window/availability gates. Unit work weights, whole-run 100,000-unit cohort budgets and safety caps 200 subjects/100,000 actions remain supplied constraints, not real acquisition/human work/private liability valuations.

## Measurement and interpretation

Capture retains full native state/events, stage/evaluator counts, effort, money/system/impact/ledger, declared native population and seven daily endpoints with source/config/full-policy identities. Derived admissions/conversions/XP, class-specific cancellations, open invitations, declared operators and cohort work remain separate. Native criteria are false liquidity versus reference Council-majority and are **not pooled** into a cross-population success/health score.

In the genuine control false-identity zeros are structural, not defense success. Erroneous cancellation means identity-classification harm against a scenario-declared genuine invitation, not an adjudication of the guarantor's misconduct; legal free veto and compensation are separate contracts. This does not cover unaffiliated honest recruitment, ordinary Self-ID harm, voluntary retention, useful contribution or overall welfare. Real-person collusion remains distinct from fabricated identities.

The report retains four values/ranges/descriptive means per cell, `shared-target − independent` contrasts and within-law `veto − observe-only` contrasts. Event prefixes are checked **within each evidence law** before its first effect-changing veto, never treated as identical received evidence across laws. Censored pairs are explicitly skipped/count-reported for this check; partial raw measurements remain but complete means are null. Daily endpoints derive from the seven-day horizon; future/duplicated partial endpoints reject. A complete run at 168 h need not finish an invitation created at 164/deadline 188.

There are no p-values, calibrated probability/uncertainty estimates, welfare score or universal ranking. Four fixed labels, shared target signals and phase contexts are not independent human/population trials. Net work/admission/XP differences include downstream trajectories, not marginal cancellation prices, adoption or equilibrium. Dense inherited support, raw active-degree/fixed Council, current-link liability and terminal-XP/unspent-veto references remain legacy, not D33/D34/D42 compliance. Daily-slot, six-hour, phase-4, sparse/compromised support, scarce work, additional correlated/adversarial evidence and human notification/willingness are not tested here.
