## Fixed control

- Four new declared labels, seven days, four-hour decisions, three attention contexts × two arms = 24 cases; no seed search or data-dependent extension.
- Twelve genuine trusted genesis operators, three coalition operators, clique support; `sybil-cone`, sensitivity 0.8/FPR 0.01, inherited daily activity/unit work weights and whole-run caps, at most 200 subjects/100,000 actions. This is a supplied dense bootstrap, not authenticated identity independence or a calibrated population.
- All-ticks availability versus one available tick/day for initial subject `n_i`, at `4*((i+offset)%6)` hours after midnight. Offsets 0 and 1 rotate the same calendars by four hours. IDs determine the plans, never evaluator ownership/identity labels. Transaction availability remains unrestricted.
- Daily quotas remain fixed; slots assigned outside attention windows expire without catch-up. One available tick/day is **not** one guaranteed inspection/day. The test includes this scheduler intersection assumption, not an optimized human calendar or population distribution.
- Honest invitations and ordinary Self-ID inspections are zero in every arm. This closes genuine growth and isolates invitation review; all honest reviewers are in the initial calendar-covered pool, all admitted descendants are scripted fabricated coalition subjects with review zero. Genuine-invitation and Self-ID harm are not evaluated; zeros cannot demonstrate safety or useful growth.
- Within each context, observe-only pays for the same review/evidence/attempt prefix but disables the veto effect by a labeled counterfactual. Veto enables that effect; expenditure and populations need not match after the first changed cancellation. Evidence remains independent/noisy; willingness is scripted, not derived from liability valuation.
- Raw topology/current-link liability, terminal XP and indefinite unspent Self-ID veto remain legacy. This changes neither deadlines nor D42 custody/maturity. Real fabrication/acquisition costs, authentic evidence, voluntary participation, sparse/captured support and scarce funding remain outside this control.
