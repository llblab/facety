# Neutral participant packets

Distribute one wave at a time. These labels identify records, not whether an allegation is justified. Read only the current packet and explicitly requested reply cards. The factual keys remain concealed until the final debrief after all four waves' locks.

All media and signatures below are fictional text records. A declared face index is an opaque synthetic reference; no photograph or real person's identity is used. A manifest can settle which references were supplied in the fixture; it cannot establish a real person's existence, uniqueness or the legal sufficiency of evidence.
