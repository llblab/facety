# Manual operational-defense validation kit

**Prepared, not executed.** This is a proposed, synthetic, paper exercise for owner review. No people are booked, contacted or observed; no rehearsal, pilot, runtime or research simulation is authorized by this kit. It supplies materials for a separately authorized manual session, not human evidence or a new protocol institution. The [operational contract](../../docs/operational-defense.en.md), [participant rules](../../docs/rules-of-the-game.en.md) and [evidence-core conclusion](../2026-11-08-evidence-core/README.md) supply its context.

The decision it can inform is narrow: can this proposed voluntary workflow turn a specific evidence request into a timely qualified action, and where does it stop for lack of information, willingness, capacity or normative authority? It cannot establish real-world identity accuracy, attack resistance, profitable participation or a population error rate.

## Materials and separation

- Give participants only [roles.md](roles.md), the current case packet in [cases.md](cases.md), and blank copies of [worksheets.md](worksheets.md).
- Give the observer the same material and [observation-log.md](observation-log.md), blank event forms whose fields are defined below.
- Keep [facilitator-only.md](facilitator-only.md) separate. Print its reply cards individually, conceal their factual key, and reveal only an explicitly requested reply to the named requester. Its separation is logistical, not cryptographic security; these are public repository files. Participants who have read the key cannot supply blind judgments for this packet set.
- Keep all factual keys concealed until every participant has locked the last wave's judgments. Never reveal a case's key between waves. A record can support a claim without establishing every identity or duty assertion.

Use only the provided synthetic identifiers and text records. There are no face images, real signatures, documents, actual account balances or sensitive histories. A printed `signature verified` label is a fictional fixture fact, not an application capability or real attestation.

All case records were invented for this packet under the repository's [license](../../LICENSE); none is a disguised accusation or sourced personal record. The authors and anyone who has read the concealed key cannot be blind reviewers of this packet version. Do not give volunteers the owner-facing contract or key before their locks if it would disclose the case design; distribute only their listed materials.

**Council branch.** Ballots offer confirmation, opposition and seven-day deferral. Each adopted deferral adds seven days to the current latest shared deadline. If an entered Council referendum reaches its cutoff with no option holding a simple majority, including zero ballots, it automatically extends seven days. Later periods may defer again. Apply one extension per recognized decision/period, not another on every clock check or replay. There is no new position or stake, and existing holds/custody continue. Without any Council entry, the ordinary cutoff still applies. Additional paper time supplies no reviewer minutes or guaranteed seven-day human availability. An unresolved entered case may stay open indefinitely; beyond this observation it is PENDING.

## Proposed session and finite resources

Eight consenting adults are proposed: one facilitator, one observer and six volunteers. Three of the six have fictional Council identities. These are roles to offer, not personnel already committed or a staffing requirement for a network. Anyone may decline a task or stop the exercise without explanation. An optional alias replaces a name; record no reason for refusal unless the person volunteers a non-sensitive task reason.

The proposed 90-minute agenda is 12 minutes for briefing and role choice, four independent 14-minute waves, 17 minutes for key reveal/debrief and five minutes of buffer. Do not extend the session to make an unfinished case look successful. If consent, room, print separation or a neutral observer is unavailable, the future session does not start.

Budget a proposed additional 30 minutes of facilitator preparation to separate cards/check the clock and 30 minutes of observer/organizer reconciliation after the session. These are planning allowances to accept or revise before scheduling, not measured requirements or committed labor. Ordinary paper, a clock and a private place for approved forms suffice; no software service or paid defense fund is required by this exercise design. Everyone's real available time must be declared rather than inferred from this agenda.

Each wave uses this schedule:

| Real minutes within wave | Manual activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Read the neutral suspicion packet. Request a source, assess it and optionally submit a qualified filing request. Record refusal, nonadmission or missing authority. No facilitator grants admission. This prefiling step is essential in packets P01 and P02. |
| 2 | Reveal the separately labeled, supplied already-admitted fictional branch. It is imposed exercise input, even if nobody filed voluntarily; it never counts as a successful filing. For P04 reveal the supplied prior final decision instead. |
| 2-10 | Voluntary requests, replies, help, position requests and Council work. The exercise clock advances continuously. |
| 10-12 | Stop new exercise actions and lock each person's factual assessment and procedural record. A still-open paper case is PENDING, not finalized by the exercise stop. |
| 12-14 | Check the ledger and missing fields without disclosing truth; rest and distribute the next neutral packet. |

For P01-P03, the proposed paper clock is one real minute per fictional protocol day, starting at real minute 2. Record finer times if an entrant acts between minute marks. Thus the observation stops at fictional day 8. This acceleration is only an exercise convention. Actual protocol windows remain seven/six/five/four/three days, and a late entry may leave a deadline beyond the observation. The clock never pauses for a queue or a reply. For P04, minute 2 begins at fictional day 7 after the supplied opening; any longer recovery calculation is a separately labeled conditional worksheet, not elapsed exercise observation.

At role choice, each person declares a work ceiling no larger than the card's proposed ceiling; zero and refusal are valid. Reading, checking, replying, helping and preparing a position all consume that person's work budget. Record actual start/stop intervals and remaining budget. Unused budget is not evidence of willingness. A ballot is an action to time and record, not proof that its author reviewed a case.

P01, P02 and P04 propose at most three minutes of active case work per volunteer per wave. P03 proposes shared ceilings of two minutes for T04, two for T05 and one for T06 **across all three concurrent cases**, not per case; T01-T03 each have at most three. People may choose less. One person cannot work on two tasks at once. These limits deliberately expose allocation and refusal; they do not prescribe network capacity or guarantee an unserved case. No facilitator fills a refusal, supplies an extra reviewer or instructs a vote.

## Procedure and authority boundary

1. Before opening a packet, explain that the activity records task choices and timing, uses fictional stakes only, and does not rate a participant's identity or moral character. Obtain the separate session authorization and voluntary agreement required for any later execution; this preparation supplies neither.
2. Assign the six identities in [roles.md](roles.md). Do not pool their XP or transfer standing. Each wave is a separate supplied snapshot: no previous wave's outcomes, account losses or factual key carry forward.
3. On each source request, the observer logs receipt, requested source and its known lineage. The facilitator hands over exactly the corresponding reply card, including an explicit unavailable reply when no further record exists. The facilitator does not paraphrase the key or endorse a claim. Requesters may share a reply publicly; sharing retains its source lineage.
4. In the prefiling interval, separate suspicion, assessment, willingness, the XP check, a filing request and actual admission. The kit has no complete admission/evidence standard. Record `BLOCKED` where that standard or authority is required. Do not substitute the imposed admitted branch for the voluntary pipeline's outcome.
5. For an ordinary entry, record its requested side, current effective XP, prior position's XP, paper stake, backing, consent and time before the cutoff. Eligibility and willingness are separate. An entry needing unspecified execution/admission authority is `BLOCKED`; possible arithmetic is `CONDITIONAL`, never canonical execution. Council ballots include confirmation, opposition and deferral. In a recognized paper Council period, a majority confirming/ opposing selects the respective side; a majority deferring, or no option majority at cutoff, extends the latest deadline seven days once. The latter includes zero ballots. A period beyond observation is PENDING; no ordinary entrants follow Council entry. Remaining production authentication/member/period/settlement contracts are not supplied by the facilitator.
6. At the stop, each person locks a factual classification, confidence and source lineage **before** the key is revealed. Keep factual assessment, an actual/requested position, a procedural last-side result and the hidden factual key in different fields. No key changes a verdict retroactively.
7. During the debrief only, reveal keys, reconcile source origins and compare elapsed work, refusal, eligibility, admitted/requested actions and unresolved authority. Record a missing or blocked remedy as such. Do not declare a pilot ready from one session or launch another exercise automatically.

The facilitator is a material distributor and clock keeper. The observer records and checks arithmetic. Neither can issue a real identity judgment, recognize evidence as legally sufficient, grant a protocol appeal, force a person to enter, override a deadline or create money. A volunteer Council is an exercise role under a supplied snapshot, not a newly authorized real governing body.

## Paper accounting

Use exact decimal FCTY for this exercise: position 1 is 7, position 2 is 8.4, position 3 is 10.08 and position 4 is 12.096. This is a **paper precision convention**, not a selected production rounding rule. Use `7 * 1.2^(n-1)` rather than the Rust simulator's whole-FCTY ceiling. An admitted position applies `max(previous, join time + max(3, 8-n) days)` to the shared deadline. A recognized Council deferral, or its no-majority automatic cutoff event, adds seven days to the latest deadline once per period. Later periods may extend again, without a new position or stake. For example, an entered Council case with deadline day 7 can defer to 14 and then 21; an empty entered vote at day 7 does the first extension automatically. This is paper rule arithmetic, not a run or human observation. The existing binary-vote simulator does not implement it.

Track existing backing, return of one's own principal, actual hypothetical collection, transfer/burn allocation where selected, new debt and later hypothetical repayment separately. All real financial flows in this exercise are zero. No winner is assigned an uncollectible prize. A zero-backed losing first accuser creates seven units of paper debt, not seven units of a defender's reward; a later losing position owes its own nominal amount. OPEN top-stake allocation, single-act monetary allocation, collection priorities or appeal effects remain `BLOCKED`, not facilitator choices.

The selected XP arithmetic is `floor(live units * coefficient)`, with a losing participant's coefficient halved and each valid matured own Self-ID adding 0.001 up to one. New units are suspended while the author's emission balance is negative; valid own maturity still repairs the coefficient. An account held in a dispute creates no new Self-ID, invitation or guarantee; an invalidated account needs an admitted remedy before participation can be treated as restored. Do not award catch-up emission or automatic reversal of a burn.

For the three-member paper Council, one validly recognized cast ballot can form a majority without a quorum. Counts of 1/1/1 or 0/0/0 at an entered period's cutoff instead trigger the automatic seven-day extension. Re-reading that same period's outcome does not extend again; another period reaching its own cutoff can. These are arithmetic examples of the selected rule, not participant outcomes or a supplied production ballot-finalization procedure.

## Observation schema and interpretation

[observation-log.md](observation-log.md) is a blank event log. Use one form per event, optional aliases only, elapsed times from the local session start, and `NA` when a field is inapplicable. Empty means not recorded; `UNKNOWN` means unavailable; zero means an observed zero. Do not fill forms or outcome examples during kit preparation.

| Fields | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `session_alias`, `wave`, `case_id`, `actor_alias`, `identity` | Non-identifying session/role references; no real account or contact details. |
| `event_id`, `event`, `real_elapsed_s`, `logical_day` | Ordered event and two explicit clocks. Events include notice, source request/reply, task offer/accept/refuse, start/stop, filing/entry request, gate check, BLOCKED, ballot, cutoff, lock, key reveal and remedy request. |
| `source_id`, `origin_id`, `parent_source_id`, `request_id` | Information exposure and lineage. A mirror keeps the same origin; a reread does not become another observation. |
| `work_cap_s`, `work_used_s`, `queue_cases` | Declared finite availability, cumulative active work and the cases awaiting this specific person. Do not sum overlapping work intervals twice. |
| `xp_actor`, `xp_previous`, `side`, `eligibility`, `willing`, `status` | Separate authority/eligibility, consent and whether an act was REQUESTED, SUPPLIED, ADMITTED within fixture assumptions, BLOCKED, REFUSED, PENDING or CONDITIONAL. |
| `deadline_before`, `deadline_after`, `before_cutoff` | Clock provenance. A missed response is not recorded as timely merely because its source was available. |
| `nominal_stake`, `backing`, `principal_return`, `prize_collected`, `burn_collected`, `debt_created`, `debt_repaid`, `debt_left` | Paper accounting in FCTY, never real funds or an assumed debt payment. |
| `judgment`, `confidence`, `reason_code`, `normative_gap`, `consent_note` | Locked assessment and operational reason without personal history; no mandatory explanation of refusal. Confidence is self-report, not calibration. |

Report notice-to-start, request-to-reply, assessment time, time willing, entry-request time, gate result, timely admitted/requested response, missed cutoff, unserved cases, simultaneous workload, refusal and unresolved remedy separately. A supplied opening is excluded from voluntary filing counts. A supplied mistaken verdict is excluded from observed decision-error counts. Unread or unrequested evidence cannot be counted as exposed. A shared origin cannot supply an independent-confirmation count.

The only end-of-session dispositions are: a recorded conditional workflow worked under its stated fixture assumptions; a particular step was refused, late, unserved or blocked; or the observation was incomplete. There is no invented acceptable innocent-harm threshold or automatic next-stage permission. The owner receives the specific operational gaps and the commitments still needed; further work needs its own assignment.

Preparation validation: all packet request labels have finite reply cards; factual keys and participant packets are separated; blank logs distinguish imposed, conditional and voluntary actions. `cargo test --release` passed, including links, anchors and English-only guards. Those checks validate the retained source/documentation, not human performance or the unimplemented Council deferral. No exercise or new population experiment was run. Preparation is complete and its bounded Grow Loop is stopped.

## Proposed access and retention for a separately authorized session

These are defaults for owner/participant approval under [the pending session authorization](../../DECISIONS_NEEDED.md#q25-d-actual-voluntary-work-and-permission-for-a-session), not a grant of data-collection authority. Collect optional aliases, event times, task choices and synthetic case decisions only; no audio/video, names, contact list, real network history, faces, identity documents or financial records. The facilitator and observer would hold raw paper privately; other volunteers receive only the synthetic shared case sheet and evidence they actually request. The owner would receive de-identified findings and explicit missingness, not a participant ranking.

Proposed retention is seven days after the session for raw forms, to permit checking/withdrawal requests, then destruction; de-identified findings may be retained in the project only with the separately agreed consent and owner approval. A volunteer may stop or request removal of their unshared notes without giving a reason; record resulting missingness without inventing an outcome. Do not promise that already shared anonymous aggregate facts can be individually erased. Access, retention, withdrawal handling and publication consent must be settled before a future session starts. There are no collected records to retain or delete now.
