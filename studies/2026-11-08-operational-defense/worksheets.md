Apply `window(n) = max(3, 8-n)` days and `deadline = max(previous, joined + window)`. An observation, reply, private recommendation or ballot is not a new position. A case still open when the exercise stops is PENDING. The exercise lock does not close it under the protocol.

Recorded opening stays pending 24 hours; maturity alone activates case/restrictions/clock and an appeal's Council vote/first seven days/fresh snapshot. Keep phases distinct; fixtures validate no admission/maturity execution or revalidation power. Before maturity cancellation forfeits no stake; once active the author may cancel only its own open case, including deferrals, not after final decision. Split actual author-stake collection equally among joined ordinary defenders, without stake/XP weights or collective Council payout. FCTY has twelve decimals. For position n compute exact nominal `7 * (6/5)^(n-1)` FCTY and required stake `ceil(7 * 10^12 * 6^(n-1) / 5^(n-1))` quanta. Round upward once at the final value; exact values remain unchanged and rounded stakes never feed later compounding. The first stakes remain 7, 8.4, 10.08 and 12.096. For equal payouts record actual pool `P`, positive ordinary-recipient count `N`, quotient/remainder, joining order and allocated sum `P`. Use no floating-point money or invented remainder funds. Zero-backed nominal 7 pays no defender prize. A losing top stake without an ordinary recipient burns actual collection, including Council-only entry above it; Council receives none and the shortfall remains FIFO debt. No Treasury/fund is created. Appeal finances/refunds remain OPEN; single-act and identity monetary bases remain distinct.

For an authorized reversal, restore status and only this verdict's revoked units to their original recipients, recalculate integrity without this loss while retaining later own maturities and other losses, and cancel only its unpaid debt. Burned FCTY stay burned; other legitimate cases/holds, current support/path requirements and no catch-up issuance remain. Use FIFO repayment: A7 then B7 and repayment5 gives A2/B7; reversing A cancels2 only, leaving B7 and the prior5 burns. This is conditional arithmetic on the existing sheet, not a new case, actual payment or executed remedy.
