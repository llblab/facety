## Bind before a runnable registration

1. **Predicate and thresholds.** Keep rho1/3/10 and multiple s_max/h_max/t_max/eps profiles. Fix absolute net payoff versus lawful-reference premium, common units and nonpositive denominator handling; comparable authority by arm; honest-type weighting and harm units. Define collapse/absorption/recovery. Bind the probability estimand, confidence/precision and label rule: an unexecuted point is not MARGINAL, and an untested attack class is not SAFE.
2. **Primary claim and CORE semantics.** Supply the uncancelled-claim window, pending/mature/void/disputed handling and pending-invitation lapse. Define Standing eligibility and decay/correction. Native legacy terminal XP versus ordinary maturity has authority/case-order effects; label/control rather than silently completing OPEN migration.
3. **Real funding and backing.** Choose BOND payer, eligible assets, P/T/K roles, covered violations and V start/expiry/replay. Keep creator-backed, own-right and sponsor-backed alternatives distinct. Define CORE's separate voucher bonds, slashing/return/finder allocation and spent-value shortfall without fictitious debt recovery. Prehistory, budgeted seed capital or genesis exceptions have different warmup/privilege costs; none is free.
4. **Jury and verification.** Specify actual genesis edges, neighbourhood projection, N_min and both requested small-N variants' quorum/shortage/tie/finality. At N100,101 distinct outside-neighbourhood jurors do not exist. Bound paid, fallible/correlated in-person evidence. Genuine collusion means f<1/3 is not itself a truthful-vote guarantee.
5. **Environment and search.** Mark each unsourced range 'no basis'. Price-demand/buyback needs finite backing, wages need time units and media multipliers need a cost map to CORE's main evidence. Bind sale/rental consent, cohort selection and norm-crowding-out models without a free oracle. Fix generated-policy space, information, training/validation, in-world beta and computational effort; report best-found boundaries again at2x effort.
6. **Map inference.** Freeze space filling/refinement, seed precision, rule extraction, held-out theta and confirmation seeds, sensitivity/dependence and full correction family. Conditional SAFE labels do not mean unrestricted optimal-attack safety. The native fixed-context API needs a proper map/metric/search adapter, not a schema-shaped stub.

## Reality contract and abandon conditions

A future recommended region must list measurable preconditions and thresholds for liquidity/backing, effective response within expiry, honest availability/cost, information quality, topology and attack budgets. Unmeasurable assumptions stay explicit. Report empty price/wage windows, unsupported/censored regions, budget-limited failures and counterexample traces as first-class outcomes. Abandon a preregistered boundary rule if reserved points/seeds reject it; do not move thresholds, hide bad model forms or reopen confirmation. No-effect mechanisms enter a candidate kill list only with equivalence/power evidence, never automatic deletion of selected rules.

## Pilot follows measured sensitivity, not a story

Current candidates are unranked: actual attestation/error correlation and enforceable delay; regional honest attention/reservation cost; vouch-sale/collateral/rental willingness and norm crowding-out. Each measured parameter must be linked to the arm/boundary decision it could change. Compute sensitivity/VOI after maps; economic VOI additionally needs a decision-loss/prior model. No human recruitment, calibrated distribution or real-world resistance claim is authorized.
