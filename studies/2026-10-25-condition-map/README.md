## Explicit viability predicate

Viable(theta) must hold against every declared adaptive class at budget beta. Register several threshold profiles, never only a favorable one:

- **V1:** honest-to-fraud payoff at rho1/3/10 in one fixed common resource/denominator convention.10 is the D50 target, not the sole proof objective. Resolve zero/negative fraud payoffs; no infinite-ratio pass.
- **V2:** attacker emission and comparable authority share at T below s_max. CORE's missing Council is not zero authority.
- **V3:** honest dilution, policing cost and wrongful-exclusion rate below a unit-consistent h_max vector, including negative-profit sabotage.
- **V4:** weighted median honest type's `p*E-attention_cost >= 0` AND honest10-to100 growth within t_max; also test the1000-person scale. Frozen networks fail.
- **V5:** absorbing bad-state probability below eps over the registered horizon, with separate collapse/recovery definitions. A jury majority or failure to recover by horizon is not automatically proven absorbing.

Threshold grids, aggregation/estimands, common unit, precision and ranges remain unbound. P(viable), joint trajectory pass probability, P(bad), finite-data confidence and surrogate uncertainty are not interchangeable. The label rule in the method is a candidate until bound: conditional support for all criteria/classes, supported violation by a class, or unresolved/marginal evidence. An unexecuted point has no label. SAFE never means unrestricted-adversary or real-world safety.
