## Voucher choice and observation environment

Verification effort is a voucher action `e in {none, chat, video, meeting, joint_act}`, not an evaluator-assigned response to hidden truth. Supply c_e by actual time/work/travel/liquidity and any independently supplied private opportunity cost; supply d_e for **honest / farm / mule-backed persona** under a declared claim/environment. All costs/powers/ranges and correlations are uncalibrated unless sourced; no monotonic ordering or perfect meeting/joint-act detector is assumed.

- Define the claim and positive/negative/unknown received evidence for each effort. For an honest valid identity, adverse evidence is false suspicion, not successful fraud detection: retain false-positive/abstention rates and error covariance, not only a single detection-power scalar.
- Distinguish chosen/advertised e, actual paid/performed e and fresh attestations received. No-effort creates no fresh personal verification receipt, but any public/background signal remains separately attributable. Failed/unaffordable verification does not produce free evidence.
- Actual meeting/joint activity needs participant/subject binding, freshness, repeat capacity, location/access and fallible evidence semantics. Meeting a real mule does not prove that the claimed persona is unique or that the same person is not reused; joint participation does not automatically prove useful work or truthful claims.
- Vouchers choose, decline, skip, seek cheaper evidence or misreport only through their declared information, prospects, willingness and budget. Do not force all honest vouchers to meet or code all lazy vouchers to skip and call that an endogenous result. Hold operator custody/work separate from the subject's procedural authority.
- Keep evaluator persona/ownership/availability truth outside protocol state. The environment may generate fallible received observations under its declared d_e; defense, evidence admission and verdicts may not read true labels. Existing paid-observation/shared-error primitives are reusable infrastructure, not an implemented effort/mule market.

**B(e)** is a supplied evidence-class bond schedule, not a selected reward/fine rule. Bind payer/asset, eligible actual evidence versus claimed e, amount, lock start/expiry/return, covered duties, collection and overlap, and what happens when effort/source is changed or evidence expires. Keep forfeiture, returned principal, locked collateral/time cost and debt distinct; no free initial bonds, no assumed collection from innocent vouchers and no automatic slashing merely for membership or a bad private motive. Do not silently equate these bonds to the earlier BOND or CORE voucher contracts.

## Information cascade: public burn versus independent evidence

Retain the owner's comparison: vouchers see the inviter's burn versus must produce independent evidence. Bind which actual burn receipt/value/history is public (and any price/cost interpretation), when it is visible, what vouchers infer and how a source is independently obtained/attributed. A public burn is costly signal/custody history, not direct identity verification; one shared signal repeated across vouchers is not many independent looks. Define independence by a source/error/dependence contract, never by a declaration or hidden owner labels.

The two endpoints change **both information and evidence obligations**, so their difference alone is a package effect, not proof of an information cascade. Preregister the minimal orthogonal controls: burn visible/hidden crossed with independent evidence required/not required, with matched actual resources/opportunities and consistent source/funding rules. Counterfactual withholding of normally public data is a labeled diagnostic ablation, not a new protocol option. If only the requested diagonal is run, explicitly limit the claim to that package.

Retain voucher decision ordering, information available before each decision, provenance of observations, shared errors, direct-work receipts, reliance on prior endorsements, skip behavior, adoption and false/honest outcomes. Separate rational use of a costly signal, mechanically forced verification, encoded selection and a residual social-cascade candidate. Any mechanism claim needs committed presence, necessity/ablation and named-alternative claims, reserved seeds AND held-out theta, matched search and the existing multiplicity policy before confirmation. No unearned independent-error assumption, oracle or posterior certainty is introduced.
