## Reduce first, preserve raw theta

[`dimensionlessGroups`](dimensionless.mjs) is a pure algebra helper: no model, random generator, search, simulation or SAFE classifier. All supplied groups require their exact fields; missing groups/null fields remain explicitly unbound. Products/divisions never coerce null into zero. Outputs distinguish defined, unbound, undefined, no-period, unbounded and numerically unrepresentable states; JSON never invents Infinity or a zero. Arbitrary unit-test fixtures are not sampled theta or calibrated values.

- **R1:** `c*B*Pcatch/(E*V+X)`. Input `bond:{c,B,Pcatch,E,V,X}` uses common monetary units, E per day and V days. c counts the supplied exposure, not inferred culpable people. Interpreting numerator as attacker loss additionally requires actual coalition ownership/paid liability, collectible collateral and enforcement before expiry. Innocent guarantor losses/finder transfers cannot silently become attacker costs; refunds are principal, not profit. BOND's backing need not equal CORE's c-voucher backing. Capital timing/cost remains separate even at equal R1.
- **R2:** `Td/Tdbl`, with `timing:{Td,Tdbl}` in days. Bind whether detection means suspicion, admissible case or effective intervention. Censored/nonexistent events use null, not zero or an infinite safety claim. Median-race success alone does not establish tail control or negative reproduction.
- **R3:** `g_a/lambda`, with `race:{gaPerDay,lambdaPerDay}` on compatible per-day/at-risk bases. Lambda is the supplied nonnegative hazard; a signed adverse causal intervention effect must be reported separately, not relabeled a hazard. Keep local-veto and global channels separate; neither paid looks nor opened cases equal successful suppression.
- **phi:** `b/(P*E)`, with `burn:{P,b,E}` in days/FACY/FACY-per-day. P null denotes no period (arm0), not zero days. E is usable own emission, not an automatic price-adjusted private income. At phi1 funding and cooldown co-bind; phi>1 strictly lowers the stationary invitation-service cap. This is not itself a growth or profitability theorem.
- **Price window:** `p*E/wDaily`, with `income:{p,E,wDaily}` normalized to common currency/day. If regional w is currency/hour, compute the denominator from declared hours/day and other reservation/attention costs before calling; there is no hidden one-hour/full-day conversion. Keep the raw participation predicate and uncertainty even where a ratio is undefined.
- **Jury:** `jury:{f}` reports f relative to the proposed1/3 threshold. This is not a safe-jury certificate: genuine collusion, observation error, sampling bias and unavailable voters remain explicit.

The helper exposes `gaLog2PriorPerDay=ln(2)/P` separately. That is the owner's proposed population-growth approximation, not `1/P`, `min(1/P,E/b)` or a fitted rate. An age-structured reproducing population can differ even when each established creator obeys its service cap. The current simulator must test both interpretations under matched funds, claims, bootstrap and time grid. Every deviation is retained and diagnosed (units, finite phase, admission/generation lag, another encoded gate, implementation bug or residual candidate), never dismissed as noise or called emergent without registered controls.

Keep horizon T separate from voucher cooldown T (use distinct schema names), and calendar days separate from 4h opportunities.

A conditional analytic check makes the first-birth assumption explicit: in an ideal immortal branching process with first birth at age a>0 and subsequent births every P, the Euler–Lotka geometric series is `exp(-g*a)/(1-exp(-g*P))=1`. Only at a=P does this give g=ln2/P. The similarity variables are a/P and gP; a is a lifecycle-derived delay, not a new chosen default or requested sweep. Resource limits, graph gates, mortality and intervention are excluded from this check. This is algebraic reference reasoning, not a Facety result or the required independent CORE boundary implementation.

These groups are partial reductions. Keep every original theta, joint assumption and timing/stock state; equal groups do not guarantee equivalent models. No nonexistent/zero-cost/zero-hazard axis is silently dropped from a map.

## Viability is not one unqualified Bernoulli

Preregister V1–V5 at multiple threshold profiles, including rho1/3/10. Specify emission versus comparable authority shares per arm (CORE's absent Council is not zero authority), harm thresholds as a unit-consistent component vector, the honest-type weighting/median and growth-time functional, and the bad-state/recovery/horizon definitions.

V5 is a probability across repetitions, while several other criteria concern within-run or across-type outcomes. Therefore do not label every seed with a guessed V5 Boolean and call the average P(viable). Required reporting separates joint seed-level pass probability for the registered trajectory criteria, bad-event probability, threshold-function estimates/confidence, and classifier/model-form uncertainty. No product of marginal pass rates, no independence assumption and no averaging away a vulnerable attacker class. Bind the final P(viable) estimand and precision before a real registration.

**Candidate label rule, not a selected confidence level:** SAFE only where simultaneous evidence supports all registered criteria for every tested class under the declared model/search budget; UNSAFE where an admissible class yields supported violation; MARGINAL for unresolved/overlapping evidence or the registered boundary band. Distinguish an unexecuted point (no label) from a measured marginal point. Unknown payoff denominators, unreached growth and computational censoring cannot create SAFE labels. A best-found attack envelope is conditional, never a supremum over every possible policy or real-world safety.

V1's absolute-payoff versus lawful-reference-premium denominator and nonpositive-domain policy remain [C6 candidates](../2026-10-25-invite-cost-vs-core/audit.md). V4 must test net income AND reaching100: a network frozen at10 cannot pass. Record time to1000 for the required scale comparison. A jury majority can be a bad state without being absorbing; absorption requires a declared transition/recovery argument. Otherwise report occupancy and non-recovery by horizon, not a permanent-failure theorem.

Repeat boundary assessment at 2x computational search effort, not silently 2x attack wealth/work. Independent training/validation/confirmation and per-arm effort/candidate failures remain visible. Reserve confirmation seeds AND untouched parameter points on both sides of a frozen rule with a specified boundary-distance scheme. No confirmation point/seed trains the classifier, chooses rules, shifts thresholds or extends a favorable region.
- **Native v2:** current evidence accepts a closed existing experiment/metric contract (32 arms/16 contexts/32 claims per hypothesis), not an arbitrary adaptive theta sampler or the new V1–V5 outputs. Implement/bind the map/selection adapter before generating an actual v2 registration.
