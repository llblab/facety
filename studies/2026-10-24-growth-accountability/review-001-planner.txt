## Risks

- **Snapshot drift:** working-tree D49 text may change while participants read.
- **Semantic substitution:** available recruitment, attention, relay, or incentive machinery may resemble—but not implement—the requested causal chain.
- **Oracle leakage:** evaluator identity/history truth may enter protocol choices without a declared observation channel.
- **Chronology contamination:** C may require unresolved event ordering, disputes, debt, eligibility, or prior-credit effects.
- **False causal certainty:** supplied valuations and scripts can discriminate conditional consequences without demonstrating endogenous real-world behavior.
- **Evidence inflation:** a registration, README, deferred capture, or multi-reviewer agreement is not a measured result.

## Validation Gates

1. **Coverage:** all eight mandatory documents have verified complete reads; discovered study paths are exact.

3. **Classification:** selected rules, directions, hypotheses, legacy executable behavior, and observations remain distinct.
4. **Feasibility:** each pilot names existing entrypoints/contracts, controlled variation, observable outcome, assumptions, and a falsifier—or specifies the missing contract that prevents execution.

6. **Handoff:** synthesis incorporates coordinator source findings without claiming that research delivery, code acceptance, or execution has occurred.
