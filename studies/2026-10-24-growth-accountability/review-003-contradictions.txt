## Resolution Evidence

### A — Smallest supported contrast, and blocker for the requested pilot

**Existing narrower candidate:** reproduce accounting/utility discrimination among waiting, invitation-only, and invitation-plus-guarantee under explicit valuations and loss assumptions. This does not test finite-pool recruitment.

**Requested pilot requires source evidence of:**

- a finite, pre-existing acquaintance pool outside current membership;
- observable opportunity, availability and depletion rules;
- an explicit recruitment-choice policy consuming applicable guarantor Self-ID-XP value;
- funded invitation/admission through `FacetyModel`;
- evaluator-only genuine-person labels, inaccessible to the choice policy.

Then compare paired opportunities/resources while varying only the relevant XP valuation. Report invitations, admissions, pool depletion and costs separately.

**Precise current blocker:** that connected adapter is not established by the supplied documentary evidence. Whether it exists in source is **not verified**, not proven absent.

### B — Smallest permissible endpoint is pre-case scrutiny

First verify source-level insertion points for public defense, defender XP, own-history selection and evidence routing.

A bounded contrast should hold history and admissibility rules fixed and compare defense/no-defense, with clean-history and equivalent non-defense scrutiny controls. To attribute an effect specifically to **high-XP defense**, vary or otherwise control XP rather than letting XP, resources and defense prominence move together.

Measure staged inspections, observations, usable packages, refusals/requests, expenditure and displaced inspections.

**Stop before sanctions** unless a lawful, independently admissible route is verified. The documented act-adjudication guard remains a blocker for full act-level consequences.

**Rejection condition:** defense alone changes guilt or causes punishment of clean history. A null scrutiny effect rejects the attention claim in that tested setting.

### C — Restricted contract test is specifiable, not established executable

A proposed ordinary-state test can exclude disputes, debt, locks, link changes and prior credit while fixing eligible guarantors. Test:

- cancellation before maturity;
- idle passage through exactly 24 hours;
- continued mature custody;
- later withdrawal or invitation use.

Required invariants: one conditional maturity credit, expiry of the original free veto, retained mature right, no automatic Collector transfer, and no disposition re-credit.

**Precise current blocker:** no inspected source/test evidence establishes an executor implementing those transitions. Existing F46 controls establish legacy behavior, not the proposed checker. At-boundary cancellation/event ordering must not be silently selected if unspecified.
