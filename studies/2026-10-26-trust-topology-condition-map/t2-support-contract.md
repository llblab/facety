## Minimal test-only observation

Every vector supplies observation `{subject, atHour, difficulty, incoming, lineage}` plus parameters `{sourceCap, minimumAgeHours}`. Both parameters are mandatory; examples q2/24h are arithmetic choices, not protocol defaults, calibrated rates or D74 K. incoming is a complete current-live **raw** incoming support list or null for unavailable capture; known[] is genuinely empty. Each row has exact relationId/guarantor/proposedAt/activatedAt. Guarantors and relation IDs are unique and no guarantor equals subject; no duplicate/parallel-edge inflation. Known times are nonnegative safe integers≤atHour, activation not before known proposal. Missing chronology is explicit null, never inferred from ID/revision/person age.

lineage is a possibly incomplete public observation list of exact id/origin/parent rows. origin is genesis (explicit parent null), invitation (one recorded parent ID), or unattributed (non-genesis with no invitation parent). An absent row/parent chain outside captured coverage or unattributed origin yields unknown source. An invitation chain is followed to an explicitly declared genesis, whose exact SubjectId is its source key. Distinct subjects/keys do not verify independent people. A missing parent is not automatically a genesis root; no default source per unknown guarantor. Duplicate origin rows, contradictory/multiple parents, cycles, malformed/future times and impossible shapes are invalid observation input, not a negative identity verdict. The scorer cannot consume evaluator fake/genuine/coalition labels, case name, expected result or unobserved full history.

## Four named diagnostic arms, no weight product

Let N be known raw support rows. For attributable guarantors let n_s count rows reaching observed genesis s; U_source is the number with unknown source. Values are count-equivalent support, not universal trust, probability or XP.

- **I0 raw:** interval[N,N] for a complete list; no age/source discount. It is not selected verified d_eff.
- **I1 lineage-root one-source:** lower=number of distinct known roots, upper=lower+U_source. Full invitation-root grouping is this named counterfactual; immediate-inviter/community/physical-controller grouping is not silently substituted.
- **I2 lineage-root source cap:** lower=Σ_s min(q,n_s), upper=lower+U_source, q=sourceCap≥1. This is local score clipping for one issuer, not reservation across users, D74's per-guarantor K, cluster quota or verified independent source allocation. I2(q1)=I1. With complete source observations and q at least every group size, I2=I0; incomplete lineage does not license raw-equality imputation.
- **I3 active-relation age:** lower=known rows with atHour−activatedAt≥minimumAgeHours, upper=lower+unknown-activation count. Equality at the age boundary qualifies. Recreated relation age starts at that relation's activation, not prior pair history, proposal/reservation time or oldest Self-ID. I3 is a separate alternative, not an undisclosed composition with I1/I2. A patient fake can age; a genuine new relation can be excluded.

All finite bounds must satisfy0≤lower≤upper≤N. They are conservative observation bounds, not exact extrema over assumed hidden-controller assignments or guaranteed future genesis completion. If incoming is unavailable, every arm has null bounds/unknown status, not zero. Classification against supplied difficulty D is pass iff lower≥D, fail iff upper<D, unknown otherwise. Fail means the stated counterfactual predicate lacks support, not fraud; unknown is not a negative observation. No scalar namespace is added and no current Model invitation is authorized/denied by these test outputs.
