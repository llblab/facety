## What the challenge establishes, and what it does not

**Per-issuer reachability is not shared capacity.** D35/D36 apply guarantee AND path requirements to the creator before invitation, not to the recipient; a future invitee must later qualify to emit. D internally vertex-disjoint paths constrain each issuer's supplied routes. They do not consume those intermediaries for every other query. D61 already explicitly distinguishes G local gate, C epoch allocation and G+C their conjunction, with C unresolved/research-only. Its anchors are path targets, not Council seats. Existing WP-G native certificates/cache conformance are software evidence, not funded fake-population growth or an implemented global allocation. General D35 anchors/direction/verification are not completed by supplied WP-G ranks.

A useful **analytic raw-support counterexample family**, not a funded protocol attack: take D bridge fakes b_i, D externally supported anchors a_i, and M inner subjects u_j. In the declared directed-from-anchor arm store a_i→b_i and every b_i→u_j; separately authorize every directed b_i→b_k for i≠k. Any u_j has D raw incoming guarantors and subject-first reversed-edge paths u_j,b_i,a_i of length2. These paths are internally disjoint per u_j, while all u_j reuse the same D boundary relations/intermediaries. A separately declared D35 single-center variant appends one shared final center z after each a_i, with explicit stored z→a_i core arcs: the reversed paths have length3, and only issuer/final-center endpoints are shared. Each b_i also has D raw incoming guarantees (one anchor plus D−1 bridge fakes) and routes to D distinct anchors of length1/2. Pad the supplied anchor set if the named A/D reference requires it; padding does not create new boundary support. This is a D-path/no-extra-slack construction: an arm demanding a spare route needs an independently funded/accounted extra support, or must be reported unsupported—not silently passed. No reverse guarantee consent is inferred; the stored directions and reversed traversal are explicit. A genuinely independent d_eff may reject this entire construction. Verification of these particular wires has not run in this cohort.

For this family, purchased boundary relations B=D, total assumed-fake subjects F=M+D, and Λ=F/B=1+M/D. At fixed D and hop limit r≥2, it grows with M **when the needed internal degrees are unrestricted**. It therefore supplies an analytic distinction between per-query disjointness and a global stock bound, not a theorem that all graph topologies/all d_eff/all finite K pass. If K counts live outgoing distinct-subject guarantees, each bridge fake here uses M+D−1 slots, so this particular construction requires M≤K−D+1. That counting interpretation is still OPEN under D74. A bound on one edge direction is not automatically a bound on every undirected traversal degree; general finite-K/hop/relay bounds must specify orientation and constrained roles. Reusing D bridges is not evidence that D deficient/shared-bottleneck paths pass.

**Liability is not universally one chain per bought bridge.** D34 preserves historical event-time exposure; §21.1 includes the false subject's inviter and that inviter's relevant guarantors. A bridge guarantor can therefore face more than one child chain when its guaranteed subject creates several suspect invitations. A narrower attack can try to separate invitation and guarantee roles or push later growth downstream, but must demonstrate its actual historical roster/duty/case allocation and collected exposure. D34/D57/D59 do not grant an automatic whole-component fine either. Neither one-chain cap nor unbounded collective guilt may be assumed. Debt and nominal penalties are not recovered loss; source restoration and punitive collection stay separate.

**A daily own-emission flow is not a live/lifetime stock cap.** At most one new own Self-ID unit/24h cannot be counted twice for guarantee/invitation funding. Mature banked rights can fund bursts; §19.2 guarantee amount/source/disposition remain OPEN, so it does not imply one guarantee/day. Newborn-lineage24h maturity+24h invitation freeze is a conditional first-use latency, not a universal48h cooldown per established issuer. D74 now selects a guarantor-limit parameter/default100, but its stock/renewal/party-count/adoption contract remains unspecified; neither an unlimited-credit argument nor the existing Native K-slot/lifetime counterfactual silently supplies it.

**XP coupling is a real question, not edge-minting.** Only qualifying Self-ID source receipts credit author+creation-time guarantors (D42/D54); invitation/guarantee creation alone produces no XP. Under full directed m-person vouching, one qualifying act/person/day and no cancellation/penalty/expiry/eligibility loss, each member gets m XP/day. For a positive initial XP gap and positive constant rate difference, T≈(X_benchmark(0)−X_clique(0))/(r_clique−r_benchmark). Thus the supplied zero-start/540-gap r_clique=m versus honest6 example is coherent under its assumptions:540/(12−6)=90days and540/(30−6)=22.5days. If r_clique≤r_honest and the clique starts below the benchmark, this constant-rate catch-up model gives no finite crossing, not a negative capture time. Actual initial member XP, the moving11th order statistic/ties, seat count/recusal, credit delays, existing commitments and penalties matter. K100 would not bind a fresh clique with m−1≤100 outgoing guarantees, **if that is its chosen meaning**. Top-A targets, local Council majority and global2/3 XP are different endpoints; C0 already exhibits their non-equivalence. Reaching either is not itself an independently proven violation or successful wrongful verdict.
