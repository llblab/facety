## Exact gaps; candidates are not selections

**C1 — Primary clock/claim/lapse.** The owner now requires an uncancelled Self-ID in the window and gate evaluation at use. The window's duration/epoch/endpoints, treatment of pending versus matured acts and adjudicated voids, and pending-invitation lapse are not specified. A new input must bind these before execution. Candidates: a rolling P-length claim window versus the declared active window (tighter maintenance versus a distinct eligibility horizon); pending claims versus only mature claims (prompt use versus a full veto interval); retain, cancel without refund, or suspend pending invitations on lapse (continuity versus stale-authority risk). These cannot be inherited from the old b0-no-claim engineering variant. In the present native financial API, use is invitation creation; any different acceptance-time meaning requires a separately named candidate. Calendar epochs, slot cap m and claiming are secondary-only.

**C2 — Funded bootstrap and CORE eligibility.** Ten pre-vouched founders do not imply free bonds, Standing or money. If a complete mutual10-person graph is used, each voucher has9 vouchees, exceeding placeholder K5; a different explicitly supplied graph need not. Candidates: matched paid prehistory, matched seed capital charged to resources, or explicit genesis exceptions (different liquidity/warmup/privilege risks). Standing's vouch threshold, pending/overturned-case treatment and activity predicate need definition. No XP or hidden genuine-person label may substitute. Ordinary native guarantees require an existing subject, while CORE requires c pre-admission vouches: admission needs a distinct funded lifecycle, not a free call to add graph nodes.

**C3 — BOND and vouch exposure.** Part1 does not say who funds BOND, which assets qualify, which subject's violation triggers loss, or whether backing is per invitation or reused capacity. Candidates: creator-backed liquid collateral versus own-right escrow versus sponsor backing (rental access/liquidity versus provenance/coverage differences). CORE separately specifies a B bond from each voucher. V may run from signing, admission or the last covered act; ongoing duty may need renewed backing. Fix covered acts, exit, expiry during a pending case, return, partial slashing, replay/overlap and rental counterparty obligations. Returned principal is not income. With fixed capital C and per-invitation locks, finite-horizon capacity can also be capital-limited around C/(B*V); this is a conditional accounting consideration, not a measured steady-state result with endogenous wealth.

**C4 — Merits, jury and small N.** Both owner-proposed small-N variants must remain flags, but N_min, graph projection, sampling frame, shortage handling and quorum/tie/absence/finality are still missing. At N100 there cannot be101 distinct outside-neighbourhood jurors; 'about J' needs an explicit rule. A genuine juror can collude: fake fraction f is not corrupt-vote fraction. In-person co-presence reports need paid acquisition, fallible/correlated evidence and an attestation validity model; photos/hashes are not a truth oracle. No verdict may read evaluator personhood or ownership directly. Candidates in the decision packet do not fill the repo's OPEN court procedures.

**C5 — Consequences and traceability.** CORE says fake-chain seizure/burn but omits debt. Spent/transferred money cannot be unminted; current source-correction only collects author liquidity and records shortfall as debt. CORE therefore needs a declared reachable collection boundary, residual loss, finder share and allocation across overlapping liabilities rather than reusing native fines unchanged. Define genuine-person violations separately from fake identities; membership, defense, density or coalition ownership are not guilt. Define how erroneous decisions and corrections affect Standing, bonds and membership without creating new amnesty or double penalties.

**Headline-metric addendum.** [The metric contract](metrics.md) adds a required commensurate growth/response race with channel-conditioned N* and below-threshold exposure, separate profit/harm and non-monetary sabotage, and network-effect alpha1/1.5/2 with a mandatory1.5 gate. Lambda's law/units and density/crossing definitions remain to be bound. This is not permission to assume superlinear free attention, treat a threshold as unique/monotone, count share dilution as calibrated price harm or declare negative-profit sabotage deterred. Statistical alpha remains separate; full registered-context failure cannot be hidden by the mandatory1.5 panel.

**C6 — Payoff, beta and selection.** Absolute attack payoff, incremental fraud premium over lawful behavior, nominal FACY, XP and private value are different endpoints. Existing `deterrenceSummary` computes a premium over the supplied lawful reference, not the requested 10x ratio. Fix the headline denominator and common resource unit, actor/operator/coalition basis, outside value, price conversion, capital cost and the budget beta. Do not credit innocent voucher loss as attacker loss, count internal transfers twice, or value debt as collected. Price paths affect FACY-valued BOND too unless an indexation/funding contract says otherwise. Choose fixed X/R before data or an explicitly preregistered discovery/selection/confirmation procedure; no post-confirmation winner/region search.

**C7 — Isolation and learning implementation.** Legacy terminal XP credits on disposition; varying b can therefore change procedural XP unless timing/authority is controlled. Ordinary D42 maturity avoids that in supported states but frozen/debt/dispute migration is OPEN. Propose a labeled limited reference/control rather than silently completing that migration. Adaptive attacks require a declared generated policy space, own observations, online updates, independent training/validation/confirmation, and separately reported search effort/in-world beta. Existing bribery scripts, prepaid strategy menus or exhaustive menu maxima are insufficient. Slot/key rental does not transfer Standing or erase SubjectId clocks/liability. Exactly-D attachment needs distinct D_attack and changing D_gate, funded cross-boundary edges, a declared canonical projection and evaluator-only ownership accounting.

## Inference constraints before registration

- `min(1/P,E/b)` is an asymptotic invitation-service prior under stationary usable income/demand. Banked stock, first-use/maturation, admission freeze, recurring newborn delay, claims and other gates can change finite/population outcomes. Record every deviation and its diagnosis. Equality b=P*E co-binds; it does not strictly lower service capacity. Nonbinding burn can still change money/private return.
- `c*B*P_catch > E*V+X` requires commensurate units, actual net losses borne by the attacker and realizable enforcement before expiry. A chance of suspicion is not P_catch. Refundable collateral still ties up capital when P_catch is low.
- Median Td<Tdbl is not a proof of negative reproduction or safe delay tails. Unobserved detection/doubling/recovery is censored, not zero or infinite safety. Computational saturation is not collapse, survival or an absorbing economic state.
- f<1/3 is an owner-proposed threshold under test, not a derived guarantee for this jury. Test genuine collusion, correlated error, selection bias, capture and missing candidates.
- H5's continuous-claim/maintenance premise must be tested against pause/resume. Signed presence need not require fabricated photos in CORE; media multipliers cannot be copied onto an in-person evidence channel without a cost map. Real genuine-person coalitions must remain admissible attacks.
- Native v2 contradiction control is within one registered hypothesis; `adjustStudy` corrects support across hypotheses, not joint contradiction across H1–H7. Fix the headline/kill family explicitly; do not claim an undeclared joint error guarantee.
- LHS is a sampling design over declared ranges/joint assumptions, not human calibration or proof over every point of a continuum. Fix tested scales, effects, probability targets, power and the full correction family before labels; power is not established by the minimum sample size that merely permits a significant sign test. Freeze scenario-discovery/region-selection rules and independently confirm resulting claims.
