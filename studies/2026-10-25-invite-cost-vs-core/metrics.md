# Added headline metrics — owner study addendum

**Required study panels, not protocol rules, fitted laws or measured findings.** [The draft](design.json) records the additions; [BACKLOG](../../BACKLOG.md#core-v2-study-invitation-cost-versus-core) remains the only task owner. These panels must enter the committed metric/selection/correction contract before research runs. They do not remove the existing readiness gates.

## Race: attacker growth versus effective response

Report `g_a(P)` versus `lambda(N_h, density, channel)` separately for **local veto** and **global challenge**, then any explicitly defined combined response. P is shorthand conditional on fixed b, budget, price/work/evidence, demand, bootstrap and adversary/search context; it is not permission to ignore those variables.

- **Growth series:** preregister whether g_a describes fabricated subjects, controlled subjects or independently defined violating activity. Retain those raw series separately: genuine coalition membership is not a violation. Estimate undetected growth under a declared no-effective-intervention control; the individual invitation service bound is not population log growth. Fix units, fitting interval, policy/search pairing and treatment of zero/extinction/censoring before data.
- **Response series:** lambda must measure an effective growth-suppression rate on the same time/at-risk basis as g_a, not raw looks, notices, allegations, jury seats or cases opened. Local veto prevents candidate admissions; global adjudication can remove existing growth or recover value after delays. Their different denominators/competing risks need an explicit mapping or matched causal contrast. Do not simply add rates that overlap or have different units. Retain exposure, paid inspection, evidence, authority, verdict, actual effect and delay.
- **N_h and density:** bind active-honest person counting, graph/projection and density definition. N_h is an evaluator measurement, never a hidden personhood/ownership input to defenses. Real-person colluders do not become honest merely because their identities are genuine. Define how changing density/case load along a trajectory maps to the conditioned curve; no automatic monotone lambda-N relationship is assumed.
- **N\*:** seek `lambda(N*) = g_a` per declared density/channel/arm/context, with uncertainty. Discrete samples may only bracket a crossing; never manufacture exact equality. Retain multiple crossings, no crossing in the tested range, a crossing outside it and insufficient precision. A unique/sustained-crossing estimator and interpolation rule must be preregistered; no universal threshold is inferred from one topology.
- **Exposure from genesis:** report calendar duration below the relevant N* from the10-person start, not just the final N. Bind initial activity rather than silently equating10 supplied founders with10 active eligible reviewers. With a known threshold, the target is the integral of `1[N_h(t)<N*]` over the declared horizon; exact deadline durations matter, not only tick counts. Under threshold uncertainty report duration bounds/unresolved time. Never-reaching or censored cases remain explicit, and do not receive a safe-above-threshold classification. Changing density or repeated crossings cannot be hidden by a first-passage-only statistic.

A non-growing saboteur can cause large harm with g_a=0 or no meaningful cluster-growth series. A degenerate growth race is then not evidence of safety: evaluate the harm panel independently.

## Two separate economic accounts

**Attacker net profit:** report realized receipts, non-recovered/pending positions, work/media/bribe/rental/financing/other supplied private costs, actual net bond losses and actual recovered payments. A returned bond principal is not profit; a lock is not a burn; internal coalition transfers cancel; innocent bond losses are not attacker losses. Distinguish absolute net return from incremental fraud premium over the matched lawful reference. Keep price/utility units and terminal valuation explicit, with unknown/censored values preserved.

**Honest harm:** report a vector, not just a subtraction from attacker profit:

- **Dilution:** separate additional illegitimate issuance/stock and honest entitlement/holding-share dilution from purchasing-power loss. The latter requires an explicit valuation/price counterfactual. An exogenous falling price does not itself establish fraud-caused dilution or measured usefulness.
- **Policing:** funded work/time and monetary outflows by actor/cohort, opportunity/availability costs and denied/unfunded demand. No duplicate addition of overlapping work streams or payments already included in net balances.
- **Wrongful exclusion:** mistaken veto/verdict/exclusion counts, affected people and duration, with recovery/censoring; record admission friction and delay separately from an adjudicated wrong. Do not replace missing observation with zero harm.

**Non-monetary sabotage** is an explicit objective within the existing six structural adversary classes, not a new unrelated sweep. A budgeted attacker may accept negative private money return to maximize wrongful exclusion, policing exhaustion, collapse or failed recovery. Specify that objective and search effort separately from profit-seeking; failure of the 10x profit claim to describe sabotage must not be reported as deterrence. Any scalar damage aggregation needs declared weights; preserve component outcomes regardless.

## Network-effect exponent and robustness gate

The owner requests **network-effect alpha in {1,1.5,2}**, with **1.5 mandatory for a robust headline**. This is separate from `registration.alpha`, the statistical error threshold. Store it as `networkEffect.exponents`, never pass1.5 as native significance alpha.

No response/value law has yet been specified for this exponent. The final registration must bind what it modifies, its scale/normalization and how funded work, evidence quality and topology constrain it. Do not silently impose `lambda = k*N_h^alpha`, free N-squared attention or a price law and then call the encoded crossing a discovered network effect. Supplied scenario alpha is not an estimated empirical exponent; any independently fitted exponent needs its own estimator/uncertainty.

The headline must meet its registered payoff/growth/harm/race requirements at alpha1.5 to receive the requested robustness label. Report outcomes at1 and2 explicitly. Passing1.5 cannot hide failure in another registered confirmation context: full-context mechanism robustness still requires every declared claim/context under native v2. Decide before data whether extreme-alpha cases are confirmation contexts or separately labeled stress panels; never shrink the family after outcomes.
