## Funded-stake reference checkpoint

The implemented `disputes.stakeBacking: "reserved"` reference now uses exclusive `FacetyModel` custody; `"unsecured"` remains default. `DisputeStakes` owns a one-time case/position lifecycle without truth or XP authority. The experiment retains target/procedure/position/timing/outcome records and procedural reasons (not human merits explanations). Pending escrow, actual refunds, transferred/burned losses and unpaid unsecured promises differ.

[The constructed financial comparison](stake-backing.mjs) with [raw output](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8b77cd8319f8613269586d1ce665828410e9fd03/studies/2026-10-23-accountability-audit/stake-backing.json) starts from real 6-FACY/8-FACY funding and two cases with 3/4 FACY positions. Unsecured backing allows spending before settlement, then transfers only **1 of 6** nominal losing FACY and leaves **5 unpaid**, not debt. Reserved backing denies that spending, transfers **all 6** losing FACY, releases **8** of winners' own collateral and leaves no unpaid commitment. Both case-book variants reject another settlement before side effects. This demonstrates backing and lifecycle semantics, **not** deterrence or welfare; reduced available capital can also impede honest sponsors.

[Six scheduler differential contexts](stake-compatibility.mjs) and their [raw controls](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8b77cd8319f8613269586d1ce665828410e9fd03/studies/2026-10-23-accountability-audit/stake-compatibility.json) compare the old scheduler from `af9b12e` with the new default unsecured scheduler. The random-key layout/generator did not change. These controls are not historical study reruns and do not prove equivalence beyond their declared contexts.

[Original v1 output](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8b77cd8319f8613269586d1ce665828410e9fd03/studies/2026-10-23-accountability-audit/legacy-stakes.json) is unchanged; [current v2 output](https://github.com/llblab/facety/blob/8b77cd8319f8613269586d1ce665828410e9fd03/studies/2026-10-23-accountability-audit/legacy-stakes-current.json) discloses that mixed diagnostic setup.
