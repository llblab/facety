## Selected versus OPEN semantics

- **SELECTED:** One normal rewarded act per rolling 24 hours, regardless of key rotation; terminal XP (`docs/facety.protocol.en.md:486–496`). The witness respects that interval and credits XP only at disposition.
- **SELECTED:** Multi-subject shared artifacts and non-rejecting cross-subject face collisions (`docs/facety.protocol.en.md:375–400`). These controls must survive remediation.
- **SELECTED:** Identical content hashes cannot be re-registered (`docs/facety.protocol.en.md:335–340`). That check works, but does not prevent F1.
- **Explicit simulator assumption:** Artifact age 24 hours is included (`docs/simulator.en.md:23`). Changing `> DAY` to `>= DAY` would change this declared boundary.
- **Clarification needed:** Whether §14.4 prohibits a subject’s second rewarded use of the same registered artifact, including choosing a different face. The broad wording suggests this direction, but the concrete rule specifies duplicate registration. No new rule selected here.

## Proposed minimal changes/tests

1. **Clarify and record same-subject artifact-reuse semantics.** If forbidden, enforce it centrally in `createSelfId`, before mutations.
2. **Prefer subject/artifact uniqueness over global artifact rejection or face-only uniqueness.**
   - Reject a prior claim by the same subject on the same artifact, regardless of selected face.
   - Preserve distinct subjects’ shared-media claims and collision recording.
   - Do not rely solely on shortening the reference window: that alters a documented boundary and leaves the intended reuse invariant implicit.
3. Replace the existing acceptance regressions with a compact boundary matrix:
   - Same subject/same artifact at 24 hours: same and different face.
   - Same subject/fresh artifact: reject at 23, accept at 24, including key rotation.
   - Different subject/shared artifact: preserve acceptance at 24; reject at 25.
   - Cross-subject same-face claims: preserve both records and collision flags.
   - Rejected reuse must not change minted supply, XP, reward timestamp, or collision state.
4. Retain the terminal-finality control: one disposition per accepted act; no XP on creation under default policy.
