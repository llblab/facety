### 4. Reproduced: the identical subject/artifact/face can earn twice at exactly 24 hours

The reference window rejects `age > DAY`, whereas the reward limiter rejects elapsed time `< DAY`. Therefore both allow the exact boundary. Claims only detect **different-subject** collisions; they do not reject repeat same-subject claims. See `src/model.mjs:260–291`.

## Selected versus OPEN semantics

- **Selected:** Challengers supply a monetary stake; Chekharda alternates positions with increasing XP and monetary stakes (§§25–26.1, `docs/facety.protocol.en.md:766–808`).
- **Direction:** Each winner receives the stake directly below (§26.5, `:840–859`).
- **Selected:** Seven-day restart-on-entry finality and freezing accused unspent rights (`:861`).
- **OPEN:** Stake asset/amount and other detailed mechanics remain unresolved; the losing top stake treatment is explicitly OPEN (`:875–877`). The inspected §§25–27 do **not** settle escrow versus reservation versus unsecured liability. Overcommitment is reproduced implementation behavior, not proof of violation of a selected escrow rule.
- Media rules permit shared artifacts and require recording cross-subject FaceRef collisions (`:312`, `:375–390`), while §14.4 says the same media cannot be reused (`:335–340`). The same-subject exact-boundary behavior creates a specification tension; this audit does not choose its resolution.

## Proposed minimal changes/tests

1. **Make dispute backing an explicit owner/model-contract choice.** Do not silently introduce escrow. If funded stakes are intended, centralize reservations/releases in `FacetyModel`, and exclude reserved value from all liquid spending paths.
2. **Expose exact settlement accounting:** Nominal stake, collected transfer, collected burn, and unpaid amount. Never report the unpaid amount as burned or as funded winnings.
3. Add regressions for concurrent commitments, attempted spending while committed, exact partial/zero collection, and duplicate settlement lifecycle handling.
4. For media reuse, clarify whether same-subject reuse is forbidden. If so, reject repeated `(subject, artifact)` use without banning legitimate multi-subject shared artifacts or cross-subject collision records. Do not merely change the 24-hour comparison.
5. Existing dispute tests check conservation and aggregate resolution counts, not funded stake integrity (`test/disputes.test.mjs:66–116`).
