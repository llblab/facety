Evidence-backed observations:

1. **Reserved collateral is excluded from ordinary spending and fines.**  
   `src/model.mjs:402–413` removes committed funds from `liquidFacy` and credits separate custody. Existing liquid-spending paths therefore cannot reuse that balance. `penalize` collects only remaining liquid funds, leaving reserved collateral intact and recording the shortfall as debt.  
   **Witness:** `test/dispute-stakes.test.mjs`, “reserved stakes have exclusive model custody across concurrent cases and every liquid spending path,” passes concurrent reservation, transfer, guarantee-confirmation, link-break and fine checks.

2. **Model reservation/disposition rejection and replay guards precede mutation.**  
   `src/model.mjs:402–437` validates duplicate reservations, disposition, recipient existence and receiver balance before changing custody. Settled commitments cannot be settled again.  
   **Witness:** Passing tests “stake disposition preflights invalid requests…” and “zero and invalid stake commitments…” compare subjects, commitments, ledger and events before/after rejection.

3. **Case lifecycle guards protect normal settlement and moot replay.**  
   `src/dispute-stakes.mjs:15–18,44–89` requires an open case, settles actual custody, retains accounting and closes the case. `src/experiment.mjs:787–811` checks the case before Council/settlement processing; `:860–875` rejects repeat timed resolution and refunds mooted collateral.  
   **Witness:** Passing book tests cover duplicate settle/moot rejection, losing-top burns, winner collateral release, shortfalls and moot refunds. These demonstrate helper lifecycle protection, not proof of a formerly exploitable scheduler path.

4. **Invalidation does not silently destroy custody or restore spending authority.**  
   `src/model.mjs:419–423` deliberately permits passive refunds/winnings to invalidated recipients; ordinary transfers still require accepted identities.  
   **Witness:** The disposition test invalidates payer and recipient, exercises release/transfer/burn, checks conservation, and confirms prohibited ordinary spending.

5. **Pending custody remains measurable rather than disappearing from holdings.**  
   `src/experiment.mjs:1177–1239` derives pending subject custody from model reservations, retains group/system stake facts and includes false-owned custody in false-circulating holdings. `:1255` retains case outcomes.  
   **Witness:** Passing integration test “all dispute references expose exclusive reserved custody…” checks all three procedures, pending custody, group totals, conservation, unpaid accounting and absence of truth/cohort fields in model commitments.
