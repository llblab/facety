### 1. Medium — Verdict overflow partially enforces sanctions and strands an unresolved case

**Source:** Branch 1 finding, independently reproduced by the verifier.

Execution order exposes a partial-mutation boundary:

- `src/accountability.mjs:212–217`: settle custody, enforce verdict, then mark the case resolved.
- `src/disputes.mjs:36–45`: apply sanctions sequentially.
- `src/model.mjs:434–448`: reject aggregate debt overflow before applying the current penalty.
- `src/accountability.mjs:44–51`: propagate non-`ProtocolRejection` errors without producing a decision-journal record.

The verified counterexample uses `causalWorld()` with normalized dispute overrides:

**Concrete impact:** Accepted numeric inputs permit partial economic mutation and a stranded unresolved case. No decision-journal record captures the failed operation. Protocol events nevertheless retain the settlement and first penalty; this is not total absence of evidence.
