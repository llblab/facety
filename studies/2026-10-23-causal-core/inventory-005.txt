## Specific falsifiers and evidence gaps

1. **Reward-label finding:** Falsified by an implementation that filters incoming transfers to the intended reward memo before aggregation. Current source and direct probe instead demonstrate all-transfer aggregation.
2. **Connectivity distinction:** A counterexample should place a protocol-invalidated non-root node on an honest subject’s only guarantee path. Raw and blocked-path connectivity should diverge; baseline endpoint agreement does not establish equivalence.
3. **Burn-category interpretation:** Cancel a frozen invitation after its funding emission burns, or void an already invitation-consumed act. The exact-disposition `inviteBurns` count should cease to describe all invitation-origin burns. No new targeted witness was executed for this source-supported finding.
4. **Notice/route counters:** Enable extra gossip/regular noticers, or provide both local and watched inspection candidates. Compare actual selected/noticing IDs with reported counters. Source supports the discrepancy; no event-level probe was added.
5. **Parameter consolidation:** Any proposed fusion must preserve interventions that independently change the two fields. Equal defaults, correlated outputs, or low sensitivity do not prove semantic identity.
6. **Historical compatibility:** Study filenames establish retained consumers exist, not which schema migrations are safe. Complete that census before renaming/removing exported fields.
7. **Coverage bound:** The 73 passing tests establish the inspected behavior checks at this checkpoint. They do not prove all default fields causally active in every procedure, validate supplied human utility, or cover all analysis/CLI defaults.
8. **Snapshot boundary:** Claims bind to the computed implementation digest above. Coordinator changes after inspection require dependency-specific refresh, not blanket reuse.

**Recommended next action:** Preserve all defaults. Have the coordinator distinguish **rule, behavioral assumption, evaluator setting, primitive state, materialized index, cumulative event flow, stock, and diagnostic label** in its causal inventory. Address the measurement/name ambiguities first; retain compatibility aliases and independent accounting checks rather than deleting information.
