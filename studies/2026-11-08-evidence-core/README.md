## Final joint confirmation: selection and stop rules

After A-C, commit the exact frozen configurations before final runs. Fresh reserved seeds: `grown-8001..8004`, never used in local selection. Three required arms: default honest control, the most dangerous actually observed attack, and the worst substantive combination of repeat-error dependence, collection/recovery and limited response found above. Pair each materially changed environment with an attack-free control if the default control does not isolate the attack effect; at most two extra controls. Same snapshot resources, honest behavior and admitted attacker resources apply within every pair. Rank attack candidates first by D129 breach; if none breach, preserve both extraction and honest-availability harm and select the one with the lower mean paired honest/control ratio (ties by dirty share). Local X5-environment ratios to the default control are descriptive, not isolated causal effects; final environment controls remove that gap. For observation stress select the mode with the lowest mean paired honest/control ratio, also report persistent blind spots separately if another mode ranks worst. Combine that mode, false accusations, proof 0.7/14 days and Council rate 0.1/turnout 0.3. Honest participants retain partner/public-watcher help, without guaranteed truth or victory. Attack scripts use owned accounts and observable graph/XP, never hidden identity labels.

Horizon: 730 days after snapshot, with the 365-day endpoint also extracted. Up to 20 runs, four seeds each; the D129 test is at the longest declared horizon (dirty FACY at most 10%; clean accounts and FACY at least two thirds). Any per-seed breach refutes that context. Fewer than four completed seeds in an arm is insufficient for its registered joint conclusion; there is no retry on new seeds. No claim of profitable honesty follows from dirty share. Accounts, issuance, retained balances, mistaken verdicts, collection, debt, ongoing cases and recovery accompany the shares. Zero denominators stay null. Four successes do not establish universal attack resistance or a calibrated failure probability.

Total computational bound: at most 38 population runs (18 local + at most 20 final), at most four concurrent, at most 30 minutes wall time for each population run and 2 hours aggregate wall time for the population series. Observe live process handles; a timeout is censored unknown, never a passing zero. Stop each arm at its declared horizon or the subject cap; stop additional synthetic work after this budget. Deterministic witnesses run once, with regression tests; no matrix expansion after an unfavorable result. Fixing a material bug requires a new executable hash and an explicit applicability note, not silently mixed results. Stop the Grow Loop after the final evidence table and proceed/revise/do-not-proceed verdict. Human-data gaps end in a conditional or negative conclusion, not further synthetic generations.
