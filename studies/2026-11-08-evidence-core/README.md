For private utility, let G be the supplied benefit of misconduct, C the actually collected loss including later repayment, E private effort, and V the account's continuation value actually forfeited. For a defender, expected **collected** reward and supplied external benefits must cover effort plus expected monetary/continuation loss. Returned principal is excluded. These are conditional accounting inequalities, not a fitted utility model or an equilibrium.

Fast escalation cannot shorten the initial seven-day window; the three-day floor is no total-duration bound. A terminal Council entry caps further ordinary entry and leaves the current maximum deadline intact. Two overlapping accusations and a later repeat keep one target restricted for 17 days, while summing case durations gives 21 days. Four preparation FACY, two FACY of collected stake burns and eight later repayment units accompany 19 created debt units; 11 remain. An outsider emits on all 17 days. The runner ordinarily suppresses overlapping cases against the same target; the `World` witness establishes holds and economic transitions, not their population frequency. Admissibility of a new case grounded in already adjudicated evidence remains an OPEN repeat-event contract (§21); this is not proof of a legally admitted replay exploit. No new rate limit, deduplication rule or duration cap is selected.

**C decision:** correctable outcomes require a reachable **and willing** senior responder or a Council that actually has time before the cutoff, plus accepted evidence. Neither growing nominal stakes nor a high per-case attention probability establishes that capacity. The application/community must expose deadlines and evidence, support public help, and measure aggregate response/refusal/appeal performance. No further synthetic attention curve can establish those human properties.
