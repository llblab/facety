# Funded Self-ID act-evidence relay: engineering checkpoint

**Author software validation, not a new research study, detector calibration or mechanism confirmation.** This is the second bounded D46–D48 implementation cohort after [`ad28fb2`](../2026-10-24-act-evidence/README.md). The [model contract](../../docs/simulator.en.md#funded-self-id-act-evidence-relay-d46d47-pre-case-only) owns API/assumptions and [BACKLOG](../../BACKLOG.md#new-behavioral-model-directions-d46d48-documentation-first) owns pending work. It narrows the prior funded-act→package→sponsor task without selecting act-level merits/remedies.

## Implemented and deliberately blocked

- Opt-in `claim: 'act-defect'` in `EvidenceRelay`. Omitted claim remains `false-identity`; existing cases/enforcement are unchanged for that legacy reference.
- `observeAct`/`reviewAct` bind public environment records to a native funded Self-ID's ID/subject/kind/recorded hour. Observations use the model's actual current hour and registered actor identity. A declared guarantor role must match the actor's registered subject in the act's recorded guarantor snapshot. This is role consistency, not independently verified people, role-overlap precedence, historic duty or new authority.
- The existing paid observation/package/route/review/accounting is reused. Environmental acquisition occurs after actual work/availability/conflict/pending-route/capacity checks; denied/unavailable looks stay missing, not negative evidence. Role/opportunity/policy metadata persists in observations/reviews with tracing disabled. Source tags are declared provenance only, not authenticated independent evidence.
- Packages allege an act defect, not false personhood. Existing strict XP/liquid-stake checks survive; even a reviewed/eligible sponsor receives `act-defect-adjudication-unimplemented` before any case/stake is opened. `resolve` fails closed rather than reusing identity verdict enforcement. The attempted action charges its reference work unit, not money or an XP penalty. A defective genuine act gains neither automatic immunity nor an invented sanction.

## Remaining boundaries

This adapter covers native Self-ID only, not invitation/guarantee acts, real media authentication, invented act requirements, calibrated suspicion, endogenous exposure/contact/search, sponsor willingness, verdict accuracy, new fault/remedy rules or D48 learning. The environment port is supplied scenario input, not authenticated truth. Deliberately blocked act cases do not demonstrate low dispute rates, successful defense, safety, deterrence or voluntary retention. No research label, experimental method/capture or mechanism claim was added; old confirmations must still refuse changed source.

Next safe cohort: visible-evidence/prospect/goals/cost-sensitive willingness assessment under explicit caller-owned valuation/profile, separating eligibility, willingness, funded attempt and blocked act entry.
