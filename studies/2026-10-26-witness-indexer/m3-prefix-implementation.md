## Frontier, joins and telemetry

Private `growPrefixes` builds the bounded FIFO simple-prefix list first. Queued records are lifetime admissions, not unique vertices or refillable slots. `stats.expanded` counts prefixes dequeued for processing, not successful routing, completed scans or actual human work. It retains distinct arrivals at a vertex and charges raw neighbor trials before excluding prefix-visited vertices. Low-scored nonroot nodes cannot extend but may terminate. Removed anchors may transit when current edge/score policy allows.

The `prefix` result record contains reference `bounded-prefix-tree/v1`, composite randomness descriptor, normalized advice once, admitted-prefix and compact route-summary arrays, work stats and coverage. Coverage is null before availability; afterward prefix/expansion/branch/depth/pool/lookup flags distinguish truncation from deliberate top-M omissions. Sampling and actual enqueue counts are separate. Route summaries store the full prefix, pool id, composite query id, selected peers and child work counts, not repeated full load snapshots. Counts/flags describe bounded software work, never protocol capacity or calibrated effort.

Direct current-anchor prefixes are considered before each prefix's stored forest tails. Tail lookup exhaustion skips more tail retrievals but cannot suppress direct-prefix candidates already queued. Raw candidates are charged before full simple/hop/current edge/terminal/removal/intermediary-score checks and dedup. Join overlaps and counted endpoint/transit reuse cannot become valid merely because each piece was valid separately. Every found wire passes both M1 implementations; all other outcomes retain feasible:null. Positive results can survive frontier/depth/lookup/build truncation. No implicit fallback or Model/work/load mutation occurs.

Composite `path-prefix-routing/v1` hashes exact JSON `[policy,callerQueryId,fullPrefix]` into a64-byte child query id, using unchanged sha256-xoshiro128starstar-v2 / path-pool-keyed/v1. No cryptographic/private-routing claim follows.
