## Scope, order and failures

Whole anchors invalidate **terminal anchors recorded in whole witness families only**; they do not cover an internal occurrence of the same ID. Segment vertices invalidate **any retained path vertex**, including endpoints and intermediaries. Both owners conservatively index both edge orientations, but this is eviction policy, not reciprocal canonical consent. Edges sent to one envelope are not automatically copied to the other; vertices are not translated to whole anchors or incident edges. Omitting either intended observation is caller policy, not an assertion of complete invalidation. No global graph/ledger/domain equality can be inferred from these cache instances: stores can contain multiple supplied scopes, and notices apply across that owner's matching retained entries. Segment ledger labels, record IDs and cached roots are not authenticated lineage or people.

After complete preflight and both native before snapshots, execute exactly **one whole.invalidate(normalized.whole), capture/check whole after, then one segments.invalidate(normalized.segments), capture/check segment after**. Both are called even when all lists/stores are empty. The first owner disagreement throws before the second invalidation. A second-stage error retains actual earlier eviction/counters; exceptions propagate without retry, rollback, fabricated success/unknown, partial receipt report or compensating reinsertion. This order is engineering execution order, not priority of canonical rights or simultaneous atomicity.

There is no supplied/derived tick, TTL sweep, verification/use update, expiry renewal or FIFO movement for retained entries. Each actual native clock stays exactly its own before value; differing whole/segment lastTick is valid. Whole invalidations increases by1 and invalidated by receipt.evicted, including a zero-eviction call; all other cumulative whole counters remain unchanged. Segment invalidate has no cumulative invalidation counter, so empty notices may leave its entire native stats equal even though a call occurred. Repeating a notice is not once-only/idempotent event execution: it makes another whole invalidation admission, typically with zero further evictions. No event/case/operation ID deduplication is introduced.

Each receipt is the exact native shape: whole `{evicted,edgeNotices,anchorNotices}`, segments `{evicted,edgeNotices,vertexNotices}` with safe natural values, counts equal supplied array lengths and evicted<=that owner's before.entries. For each owner, after.entries==before.entries−evicted; bytes and reverse-key inventories cannot increase, and no clock changes. Evicted0 leaves byte/key inventory unchanged; empty owner inventory has zero bytes/key counts. Whole counters follow the exact deltas above. Segment after.records must be the unchanged FIFO-preserving subsequence of before.records: no inserted ID, reordered/modified retained metadata, verified root/time/use/storedAt/expiresAt change; removed record bytes sum exactly to the byte difference and removed record count to evicted. Native before/after schemas/config/record shape and safe counters are checked without exposing paths or private indexes. These are local consistency guards, not proof which canonical facts changed or external-data validity. Astronomical headroom/fault branches may be source-inspected without private-state injection and must be labelled honestly.
