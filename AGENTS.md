# Facety simulator context

Canonical open work: BACKLOG.md

## Durable boundaries

- This is a Node.js 22+ research simulator using ESM `.mjs` modules and native `node:test`; it has no third-party dependencies or build step.
- [The protocol](docs/facety.protocol.en.md) is a self-contained living manifesto/specification maintained here, not a project-navigation hub, shipped-runtime contract or numbered edition (D40). Keep purpose, connected mechanisms, current selected rules and intelligible OPEN contracts in its main text; project navigation/APIs/tasks belong in their own surfaces. Integrate later decisions into the relevant main section while retaining dated history; supporting links must not supply missing protocol meaning. Record every owner decision in it with its label and date, add it to Appendix A, and update the [insight register](docs/insights.en.md). D33/D35/D36's selected topology design is not yet implemented: retain the explicitly documented legacy reference until verification/path-anchor/genesis-activation/epoch/voting contracts are resolved and a separate migration is authorized; do not infer verified independence from evaluator truth, raw degree or XP. D35 requires both difficulty-sized guarantee and XP-centered path support, permits pre-guaranteed genesis, and defers cluster quotas/boundary gates; do not reactivate them or silently select reciprocal edges or XP anchors. D36 selects internally vertex-disjoint invitation paths: issuer/center endpoints may be shared, intermediaries may not; do not weaken this to edge-disjoint or merely distinct routes. D34 historical event-time liability and early self-correction are also not implemented; preserve the labeled current-link reference until event/duty/replay and correction-funding contracts are specified. D42 selects ordinary XP credit and expiry of the original free guarantor veto at 24-hour maturity, even for unused value; it supersedes D2's consumption/conversion trigger and indefinite unspent veto. D42's clarified custody choice retains a matured own-emission right until separately chosen withdrawal or invitation use, with no automatic Collector conversion. An opt-in `xpFinality: 'maturity'` implements ordinary unencumbered24h snapshot credit/veto expiry, not the full migration; unsupported frozen/debt/identity maturity fails explicitly. Label default terminal-credit/unspent-veto behavior as legacy until remaining dispute/debt/chronology, eligibility and prior-credit effects have a contract; do not reopen the resolved no-automatic-withdrawal choice or replay/relabel historical evidence. Other simulator defaults follow SELECTED rules; the [coverage table](docs/simulator.en.md#protocol-coverage) maps each rule to its mechanic. Do not silently choose rules for OPEN items: name every simulation assumption in [the model contract](docs/simulator.en.md) and in experiment output.
- D54 XP comes from actual Self-ID receipts plus chronological authorized adjustments; balances/recipient indexes are reconstructible caches. Creation-time guarantors retain pending XP/veto/history after exit; late joiners do not enter that act. Preserve D9's separately recorded older-credit behavior. D55 trusted per-act penalties use each act's own snapshot: Self-ID halves only the author; invitation halves creator plus guarantors. Explicit monetary inputs and same-case replay protection do not define depth arithmetic, merits/remedies or general cross-case deduplication. Positive-guarantee24h cooling is still only a proposal. D56 personal-chain positions/predecessors survive cancellation, voiding, disposition and key changes; do not equate those historical positions with chargeable acts or silently derive `N`.
- D57 guarantees include preceding personal-chain correctness, not only real-person correspondence. A new historical claim does not backdate old NFT guarantor rosters; endpoint, fault/risk and per-party financial contracts stay explicit. Inviter mentoring and rare deep late cuts are owner direction/hypothesis, not implemented behavior, Bitcoin-like finality or age/XP/view-count proof. Preserve D34 attribution, specific-act veto protection and D55 XP-role distinctions; collective responsibility is not guilt of every neighbor/viewer.
- D59 distinguishes upheld author self-correction of one act from any non-author's affected-suffix cut; preserve the valid earlier prefix and all historical records. Native source resolution is a trusted verdict-execution layer, not case access/merits or punitive sentencing. Keep actual recovered principal, already burned sources and unpaid debt separate; locked/debt-used restoration and per-party historical N/fault remain explicit boundaries, not silent defaults.
- Keep every insight: a finding goes into the insight register with its evidence level (confirmed, exploratory, diagnostic) and source study, and the backlog keeps its follow-up.
- Use the owner's [D32 strategic lens](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#accountability-for-emission-and-growth-d32): accountability for farming-resistant emission distribution, retention and network growth—not merely disputes. [D49](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#structural-trust-must-earn-its-growth-cost-d49) requires testing structural resilience benefits against genuine-growth friction, not crediting a closed network as safe. [D50](docs/facety.protocol.en.md#honest-participation-and-incentive-asymmetry-d50) makes 10× incentive asymmetry a research target, not a proven property: keep effort, realizable net profit and actual recovery distinct, treat zero/negative/missing denominators honestly, and test long-lived genuine-person violation coalitions as well as Sybils. Label it as intent, separate uniqueness, contribution/reputation and distribution, and never treat XP or traceability as verified usefulness/truth. Keep oracle/evidence validity, Sybils, collusion, arbitration capture, bootstrap vulnerability and the real cost-of-attack condition explicit; contribution-proportional distribution requires a scoped metric and verification model, not a new assumed reward rule.
- D53 separates autonomous FACY economics from conditional ATMO-governed support. Do not infer a lifetime stock cap or price floor from finite people, daily rate limits, N² potential pairs or declining relative issuance; do not import free outside funding/protected prices. Governance/market/health-signal/recovery-fork contracts remain explicit, and graph labels alone cannot identify violators or authorize exclusions, debt erasure or a frictionless reset.
- Preserve D51/D52's causal boundaries: invitation alone grants no guarantor XP; finite external recognition is not verified personhood or an admission exemption. Public defense/association/loss is not an independent violation; secondary scrutiny must establish its own evidence, admissibility, authority and authorized consequences. D42 cancellation and ordinary maturity share one preventive condition; independence concerns only later disposition. Do not use exogenous activity, attention flags or automatic cluster cases as proof that these feedbacks are implemented.
- Keep evaluator ground truth (attacker ownership, genuine/false identity labels) outside protocol state. Defense algorithms may consume declared observations, never omniscient labels.
- A membership path certifies reachability in a supplied canonical graph, not unique personhood. Keep invitation lineage, guarantees and identity judgments distinct.
- Report attack probabilities only conditional on scenario, policy, horizon, seed sampling and success criterion. Include uncertainty and real recovered costs; debt is not a token burn.
- Route all protocol-economic mutations through `FacetyModel`. Direct graph mutation is appropriate for standalone algorithm experiments and cloned snapshots, not a substitute for funded admission.
- Preserve measurement missingness: expected daily endpoints come from the declared horizon, not the last retained row. Zero total XP has undefined concentration (`null`), not zero inequality.
- Incentive analyses must separate recorded protocol consequences, explicitly supplied private utility and independently justified application externalities. A maximum over scripted strategies is not endogenous adoption or an equilibrium. Keep operator custody/work distinct from subject-level procedural XP authority. Game equilibria hold only for the declared strategy menu and valuation; report valuation sweeps and individual deviation tests, not only population best responses.
- The dispute layer is a declared abstraction of OPEN Chekharda/Council procedures. Verdicts may use evaluator truth only through the environment's declared accuracy, and sanctions must go through `FacetyModel`.
- Exploratory descriptor thresholds do not establish an emergent mechanism. Retain raw measurements, check encoded-rule and control explanations, and reserve new seed labels for confirmation before making a mechanism claim.
- A mechanism claim needs a registered `src/evidence.mjs` confirmation with presence, necessity (ablation) and named-alternative claims. Commit the registration before running its confirmation. Report contexts not tested and alternatives not controlled. Ablations are labeled counterfactuals, never protocol options.
- Results are reproducible only with the same `provenance.implementation` digest and randomness manifest. Registered confirmations refuse a changed implementation unless explicitly overridden.
- D37/D38: new v2 registrations bind `family-contradiction/v1` over all registered claims × contexts, with the support intersection-union rule unchanged. V1 keeps its original digest and labeled unadjusted statistics by default in native/CLI analysis. Implementation-drift permission is not permission to switch methods; explicit new-method analysis of old data must produce a separate method-identified reanalysis artifact. Preserve historical registrations/results; do not automatically replay studies or conflate method tags/content digests with authenticated evidence.
- Decision/observation randomness must not couple unrelated actions through consumed stream offsets. Preserve the inert-inspection and future-intervention-prefix controls. Changing the logical random-key layout or the generator requires a new randomness-policy identifier; seeds alone do not identify an implementation revision. Scheduler optimizations must consume the same keyed draws and be checked bit-identical against the reference behavior.
- Delete verified dead code without routine approval requests. Check executable entrypoints and consumers first; preserve historical evidence and distinguish a source-fingerprint change from a behavioral change.
- At meaningful validated checkpoints, commit scoped changes and push to the branch's configured upstream. Do not force-push, rewrite history, or include unrelated changes.
- Run `npm test` for behavior changes: current tests run on current source; the four explicitly source-pinned historical groups run unchanged in an isolated local checkout of their compatible reference. Do not weaken their guards or call archived-source passes current-source validation; current tests separately verify drift refusal and immutable historical bytes. Compare optimized path algorithms with the reference result and independent certificate verifier before comparing performance.
