## Surfaces and ownership

- **English only, no prompt quotes.** The repository contains no Russian text and no literal quotes of owner prompts, voice notes or chat messages. Restate every owner decision as a rule or a research choice in plain English.
- [Protocol](docs/facety.protocol.en.md) owns meaning: a self-contained specification (D40) that states the current rules clearly, not a navigation hub, API manual, task list or history. It carries no provenance: no sources, quotes, owner attributions, decision dates or supersession history. A decision ID may appear as a bare trace tag, for example `(D104)`. Integrate each owner decision by rewriting the affected rule in its main section. Record the ID, date, status, a one-line paraphrase and what it supersedes in the decision log ([`docs/decisions.en.md`](docs/decisions.en.md)), and add an [insight register](docs/insights.en.md) entry. Research-only choices belong in the decision log and study pages, not in protocol rules. Supporting links must not supply missing protocol meaning.
- [Model contract](docs/simulator.en.md) owns implemented subset, deviations and the [implemented rules](docs/simulator.en.md#implemented-rules). Defaults follow SELECTED rules; never silently choose an OPEN item — name every simulation assumption there and in experiment output.
- BACKLOG alone owns tasks; CHANGELOG records milestones; [DECISIONS_NEEDED](DECISIONS_NEEDED.md) records assumptions and owner questions; studies retain their own evidence. No parallel plan or copied narrative across files.
- Keep every insight with its evidence level (confirmed, exploratory, diagnostic) and source; its follow-up goes to BACKLOG.
- **Consolidation protocol (D132, D139).** Consolidate periodically but moderately: at a milestone (a rule adopted, a baseline axis fixed, a large study closed), when BACKLOG or the insight register has grown noticeably past what a newcomer can read in one sitting, or when the owner asks. Never as a reflex and never in a single sweeping pass; one meaningful pass per milestone is enough. Procedure:
  1. **Inventory** what is new since the last pass: owner statements, decisions, findings, open questions, files.
  2. **Classify** each item: *established* (restate it in the [consolidated findings](docs/insights.en.md#consolidated-findings) with its evidence level and justification, or drop it as obvious once written down), *still a hypothesis or open question* (keep it where it is), *superseded* (stub it or link to the tag, naming what replaced it), *retired* (record once why it no longer makes sense).
  3. **Preservation check before deleting or folding anything.** Every owner statement and every useful concept must still have a home: a protocol rule or concept, an insight, an open question, or an explicit retirement with the reason. If in doubt keep it. Delete only what git history or a tag already holds and what no current page, test or entrypoint reads.
  4. **Fold, then delete.** Fold exploratory entries into the consolidated item they support; move bulky raw artifacts to git history behind a tag; rewrite links to permanent links at the tag.
  5. **Verify and record.** `cargo test --release` (links, anchors, English-only) must pass; add one CHANGELOG line naming what was folded or retired.
- **Phases (D140).** Until the baseline is final, the project forms hypotheses and the best way to build the baseline, and looks for feedback; targeted checks only, no exhaustive sweeps or large benchmarks. The baseline is final when every axis has a named default and is fixed in the [simulator contract](docs/simulator.en.md) and the BACKLOG axis table. Only then does a broad benchmark and a search for pits at particular values make sense. Within the baseline phase the order is stability and predictability first, then analysis, then understanding, then representativeness (D141). BACKLOG states the current phase.

- **Settled 2026-10-29:** D110 free link termination cancels the terminating party's valence; D111 one equal Council vote regardless of XP; D112 re-entry by ordinary mutual guarantee without a new invitation, Self-ID/emission need ≥1 current mutual guarantee of any origin (D95's invitation requirement superseded); D109 binds the D50 falsifier design.
- **Settled 2026-11-07:** D162 each dispute position has its own window `max(3, 8 - n)` days from its own join and the deadline is the latest end point (never shortens; the Council enters once, collectively, on the same clock); D163 Self-ID XP is earned at 24-hour maturity for the author and the creation-time snapshot, independent of withdrawing, burning or leaving the entitlement, and economic use never rewrites the history that generated XP. The spend-credit simulator is the tag `baseline-spend-credit-d156`.
- **Settled 2026-11-07 (D164):** the XP halving of losing participants stays for everyone, Council members included; no verification before a case and no second confirming participant (the ladder keeps one participant per position). A collapse of honest growth under it is a defect of the simulator's examiner model, not a reason to change the rule.
- **Settled 2026-11-07 (D165):** XP is units of responsibility (one per Self-ID of the account or of a guarantor of its creation-time roster, lost when the act is cancelled or revoked) times an integrity coefficient in [0, 1], rounded down; a lost dispute halves the coefficient, each matured own Self-ID adds 1/1000, never above 1. Default on; `--no-integrity` is only an ablation.
- **Unselected — keep as alternatives:** D87–D90 K-referendum weights, three-day Council duration, guarantee suspension, continuous-invite Self-ID (D96/D101). D113: K referendums pause nothing; the per-invite 24h veto freeze stays.
- Route all protocol-economic mutations through `World`; direct graph mutation is for standalone algorithm tests only.
- XP comes from actual Self-ID receipts plus chronological authorized adjustments; balances are caches (D54). Per-act penalties use each act's snapshot (D55). Personal-chain positions survive cancellation (D56); guarantees include preceding chain correctness without backdating rosters (D57); author self-correction vs non-author suffix cut (D59). D9 older credit stays separate.
- D77: open-dispute participants cannot create Self-IDs, invitations or guarantees; uninvolved life continues. Opt-in relay references are bounded, not a court.
- A membership path certifies reachability in a supplied canonical graph, not personhood. Keep invitation lineage, guarantees and identity judgments distinct; graph hashes are not proof of inviter or authentication. Raw guarantee counts use stored incoming edges.
- WP-G (D61) indexer output is advisory: canonical liveness, compatible slack families and bounded verification are never delegated to an untrusted cache. Anchors grant no Council authority; Standing is not XP.
- The dispute layer is a declared abstraction of OPEN Chekharda/Council procedures; verdicts use evaluator truth only through declared accuracy; detection is not correction; financial reservation is not an admitted complaint; trusted D55/D59 execution is not adjudication.

## Research boundaries

- Strategic lens: D32 accountability for farming-resistant emission and growth; D49 structure must earn its growth cost; D50 10× asymmetry is a target, not a property; D62 51%/two-thirds are conditional questions, not thresholds; D68 core-first human-operated defense; D53 no price floor from finite issuance; D76 minimum causal evidence over more mechanisms, longitudinal over static.
- **Attack simulations always run with detection on.** Without detection nothing opposes a cluster of fakes, so a no-detection arm is self-evident and carries no information about a real network. Never launch, propose or report a no-detection attack arm as evidence. The open research question is how detection is designed (looks, vetoes, leaving, public evidence, escalation, verdict accuracy, false-positive cost), and that is what to vary. Existing no-detection (`gate`-only) results stay in the tree as labeled legacy baselines (D115, D125), not as findings to extend.
- Keep evaluator ground truth outside protocol state (`World` never sees cohort or identity labels); defense consumes declared observations only. Hidden identity/control labels never reach defense.
- Separate uniqueness, contribution/reputation and distribution; XP and traceability are never verified usefulness or truth. Keep effort, realizable net profit and actual recovery distinct; debt is not a burn; zero/negative/missing denominators stay honest.
- Report attack probabilities only conditional on scenario, policy, horizon, seed sampling and success criterion, with uncertainty and real recovered costs.
- Preserve missingness: endpoints come from the declared horizon; zero total XP has `null` concentration.
- Incentive analyses separate recorded consequences, supplied private utility and justified externalities; a max over scripts is not an equilibrium; report valuation sweeps and individual deviations.
- Mechanism claims need a committed registration (presence, necessity by ablation, named alternatives, a kill criterion), written before running, on reserved seeds. The Rust simulator has no registration tooling yet; until it does, every result is diagnostic. Exploratory thresholds are not mechanisms; ablations are counterfactuals, not protocol options.
- D37/D38: v2 registrations bind `family-contradiction/v1`; v1 keeps its digest and statistics. Drift permission is not method switching; new-method analysis of old data is a separate artifact.
- D108 (2026-10-29) released the master-programme order lock and the WP1/G1 method precondition; every claim still needs a committed registration, kill criterion and reserved-seed confirmation, and real/personal data needs explicit license/ethics approval.

## Engineering

- Reproducibility: seeds and the randomness policy identifier (`facety-sim-rng/v1`) are part of every result; a new key layout or generator needs a new identifier. Randomness is decision-keyed: unrelated actions never couple through stream offsets. Scheduler or data-structure optimizations must not change outcomes for a given seed unless declared as a deviation in the [simulator contract](docs/simulator.en.md).
- **Two baselines (D145).** The mechanics baseline (rules and baseline axes) defines results. The efficiency baseline (search order, caches, data layout, scheduling) must not change outcomes for a given seed: a mechanism that is faster simply becomes the efficiency default, and the change is checked by identical saved results before and after. When an optimization can change outcomes only through a declared channel (a search that runs out of its work budget), declare that channel in the simulator contract and report the counter. Results record the efficiency settings in force.
- Compare optimized path algorithms with the brute-force reference and the independent verifier before measuring performance (`paths.rs` tests).
- Delete verified dead code without asking; check consumers first.
- Success criterion for the topology defense (D129): dirty-FACY share at most 10% on the longest declared horizon; a clean share below two thirds fails the scenario.
- Run `cargo test --release` for behavior changes; it includes the documentation integrity guard (links, anchors, English-only text). Do not weaken it.
- Commit scoped changes at validated checkpoints and push to the configured upstream. No force-push, history rewrite or unrelated changes. Every commit that completes a milestone (a rule adopted, a baseline axis fixed, a study closed, a simulator capability or fix of note) also adds its CHANGELOG entry in the same commit; check the CHANGELOG before committing.
