# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Added deterrence grids: `runGameGrid` / CLI `game-grid` reruns a game over simulation-parameter axes and summarizes each cell. The summary covers fraud gain over a declared rule-following reference, the equilibrium fraud premium and fraud probability, honest value and wrongful invalidations. `assessViability` / `grid-viability` apply declared welfare criteria.
- Exploratory deterrence-threshold study (`studies/2026-10-22-deterrence-threshold`, 10,560 runs). Within the model, deterrence with acceptable honest welfare needs:
  - verdict accuracy of at least about 0.8 (below that, a harassing coalition invalidates many genuine people);
  - false-suspicion rate near 1% (at 5%, false free vetoes make vigilance unprofitable and farming pays);
  - per-inspection detection of only 0.2–0.35.
  Not yet confirmed on reserved seeds.
- Added strategy games (`src/games.mjs`, CLI `game`, `game-analyze`, `game-sweep`). Roles declare strategy menus (activity profiles and schedules). Every profile and every individual deviation is simulated on shared seeds, keeping per-operator payoff facts, and explicit linear valuations are applied offline. The analysis reports population best responses with paired sign tests, pure and mixed equilibria (support enumeration), individual incentive compatibility, social-dilemma flags and valuation sweeps.
- Added an optional one-step dispute layer: an XP-gated challenge with stake, a verdict of declared accuracy, fines with configurable liability scope, a challenger reward share, loser XP halving and optional exclusion of liable guarantors ("persona non grata"). Sanctions go through `FacetyModel`, and wrongful invalidations are counted. It is disabled by default; with it disabled, behavior is bit-identical.
- Added deviant groups (a seeded subset of initial operators with an alternative strategy), per-operator effort, per-group payoff facts (`agents`, including FACY and debt held by fakes), `system` facts and cumulative `sanctions` observables.
- The Council is now, by default, the XP leaderboard among currently active subjects (`councilEligibility: "active"`), following the protocol owner's decision on the §27.1 OPEN item. `"accepted"` restores the previous behavior. Tests about initial-state Council capture set it explicitly. The 2026-10-22 coalition study ran under the previous default.
- Exploratory inspection-game pilot (`studies/2026-10-22-inspection-game`). Vigilance deters farming (−121 versus +19 per coalition operator), while passivity makes farming pay about ten times honest income. There is no pure equilibrium in the menu. Challenger rewards make individual vigilance profitable only while others are lax, so the incentives form a public-good dilemma. Not yet confirmed on reserved seeds.
- Added a confirmatory evidence pipeline (`src/evidence.mjs`). Hypotheses declare a statistic, arms, presence/necessity/alternative claims and robustness contexts. Registration digests the normalized design with the implementation and randomness policy, and confirmation runs on the reserved `confirm:<hash>` seed namespace with exact binomial intersection-union tests. Unknowns count against both support and contradiction, and Holm adjustment covers whole studies. A `mechanism` verdict requires an ablation-necessity claim and at least one controlled named alternative. An A/A null control and tamper/implementation-change refusals are tested.
- Fixed a debt-recovery veto bypass. A debtor's Self-ID used to burn at acceptance, so guarantors could never cancel it and its XP became final immediately, unlike everyone else's. The new default `debtRecovery: "reserve-until-mature"` keeps the right cancellable for 72 hours before it repays debt. `"immediate-burn"` remains as a comparison policy. Debt-free and experiment runs are unaffected.
- Replaced the randomness generator with `sha256-xoshiro128starstar-v2`, validated against reference vectors. The former 32-bit FNV/mulberry32 seeds gave distinct decision keys identical streams at roughly 2^16 forks per run. Fixed-seed outputs intentionally change; the manifest records the new generator.
- Added labeled counterfactual ablations (`withholdGuarantorXp`, `fixedInvitationThreshold`, `randomGuaranteeTargets`). They are recorded in configurations and assumptions and are never protocol options.
- Added structural observables (reciprocity, largest weak component, attack edges, lineage depth, attacker excess XP share, XP/out-degree rank correlation) and attached a source-content implementation digest (`provenance`) to every run and batch result.
- Fixed fee accounting: `guaranteeFeesPaid` counted only still-active relations. It now includes ended ones, and `linkBreakFeesPaid` is reported per cohort.
- Added worker-thread execution (`mapTasks`, `runLandscapeParallel`, `--workers`) with sequential-identical results, and optimized scheduler target choice and activity counting with bit-identical outputs. Large sybil-cone runs are about 3× faster.
- Delivered the JSON-only CLI (`bin/facety.mjs`) for runs, batches, sweeps, landscapes, paired controls, offline reanalysis, evidence and graph artifacts. Existing outputs are refused without `--force`. The graph workbench now has regression tests.
- First worked study (`studies/2026-10-22-coalition-council`). Two preregistered mechanisms were supported within the model on 720 reserved-seed runs: guarantor-XP coupling amplifies a dense coalition's Council share (+0.14 to +0.17 seat share; requires coalition guarantee intensity; survives equal Self-ID rates), and a growing Sybil cone's Council under-representation is a transient of exponential admission (−0.29 to −0.31 seats minus subjects; halting growth raises the gap by 0.45; survives immediate XP finality). Untested alternatives are listed in the study.
- Put the project under git. Registrations are committed before their confirmations.
- Added evaluator-only ownership counterfactuals for retained incentive forks: independent genuine newcomer versus sponsor-controlled false identity under explicit assumptions. Custody pools FACY but not procedural XP; controlled work includes the newcomer's actions and a separately identified fabrication charge. Conditional cash/work witnesses distinguish relative private preference from adoption. Shared protocol traces do not establish live Sybil feasibility, authentic identity, calibrated costs or network benefit.
- Fixed NUM-1 numeric-boundary composition: model confirmation, link termination and compound admission preflight graph capacity before fees or subject state change. Rejected operations preserve their complete exposed state, and exact-headroom operations still succeed. This closes the injected/imported exhaustion counterexamples, not a demonstrated ordinary-run attack; no general rollback or whole-model restore API is claimed.
- Added explicit offline terminal-payoff analysis for retained incentive forks: required valuations and loss assumptions, null-preserving components, XP break-even thresholds and qualified finite-menu comparisons. Retained facts are rederived from checked checkpoints and per-subject work rather than cached comparisons. Numerical tolerance is declared, raw differences remain visible, and no market calibration, adaptive choice or network welfare is inferred.
- Added deterministic marginal incentive forks: compare waiting for Collector, admission alone and admission plus a separate guarantee on a common funded prefix. Timed native protocol traces and per-subject effort distinguish sponsor returns from newcomer money/work. Utility and network externalities remain unknown; a declared-preference counterexample refutes unconditional privately profitable recruitment without assuming actual human preferences.
- Corrected offline landscape endpoints (STAT-1, Medium): align saved daily measurements to explicit horizon metadata, retain absent snapshots as null gaps and withhold full-ensemble endpoint means when an expected final observation is missing. Paired controls reuse the same daily alignment.
- Hardened extreme numeric boundaries (R1/R2, Low): exhausted graph edge/revision counters reject mutations atomically, including compound node deletion; fines preflight aggregate debt before changing balances, XP or events. These findings did not demonstrate failures in ordinary bounded cohort runs.
- Made the zero-XP measurement convention explicit (STAT-2, Low): XP Gini is null without effective XP, distinct from equal positive authority. Final metrics and histories share the implementation; generic and degree Gini conventions remain unchanged. Earlier saved zero-XP measurements are not silently rewritten.
- Paired counterfactuals and temporary activity disturbances: preserve both arms, phase-level differences and explicit finite-window return criteria, with unknown/censored outcomes retained and conservative paired risk uncertainty.
- Fixed cross-channel RNG coupling: inert inspections could change growth and XP merely by consuming behavior/observation draws. Decision-keyed randomness is now versioned and retained in results; fixed-seed outputs from the previous sampling policy intentionally change.
- Emergent-behavior instrumentation: passive time series of XP concentration, Council churn, normalized cohort mixing, connectivity, admission readiness and realized activity. Bounded parameter landscapes retain raw histories and expose descriptive candidates for offline reanalysis, with explicit missing-data, censoring and inference limits.
- Cohort experiments: reproducible honest growth, Sybil cones and genuine-person cartels with separate human ownership, activity by action and noisy veto-only defense.
- Measured attack/defense effort: exact budgeted work accounting, charged rejections and separate FACY accounting. Monte Carlo batches and parameter sweeps report conditional success, Wilson uncertainty and unresolved computational truncation rather than false defense victories.
- Deterministic Facety foundation: separate identity graphs, seeded synthetic topologies and mutation journals, selected Emitter timing and monetary conservation, explicit experimental XP/guarantee policies.
- Directed membership-path certificates: BFS and bidirectional search, canonical-state verification and invalidation after relevant graph mutations. These certify graph paths, not human uniqueness.
