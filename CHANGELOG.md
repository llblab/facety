## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-19

- D199: a monetary dispute filing is one atomic transaction that validates, prices, checks and exclusively reserves the full first stake from spendable Collector FCTY, records the pending opening and occupies the accused's slot, or is refused with no case ID, slot, balance change or custody. Pending cancellation releases the reservation once; activation at 24 hours reuses it without a funding check. Supersedes D197's activation-time funding and its `Unfunded` outcome.
- D199 applies to ordinary identity openings (7 FCTY) and the trusted Self-ID fixture (filing-frozen `N`). The core appeal fixture keeps a trusted unfunded slot because appeal finances remain OPEN; no zero-cost or mandatory appeal stake is inferred. Later positions keep soft backing; sanctions, FIFO debt, Council rounds and restoration are unchanged.
- D197: accepted filings share one unresolved slot per accused subject across ordinary cases, trusted Self-ID corrections and linked appeals. Pending cancellation is free for the author or the trusted opening-roster guarantor. Active cases close only by verdict. D198 limits every stake to ordinary Collector FCTY; mature Emitter rights are not pledged or automatically converted, though merits collection can still reach them.
- Removed active withdrawal and moot closure. Defense wins settle by ladder position, with no extra compensation; later stakes keep soft backing and Council/no-recipient rules.
- Completed D196 credit provenance: apply verdict effects before releasing valid due Self-IDs, stamp their actual credit time, and release the subject slot last. A blocked appeal reversal retains its slot and holds. The scheduler credits maturities before an event hour, activates due openings, then credits that hour's maturities, replacing the activation-candidate boundary with the same outcomes.
- Evidence: deterministic checks for zero and partial backing refusal, exclusive reservation, competing filings, replayed cancellation, same-hour ordering, balance changes during pendency, suspended same-hour maturity, separate accounts and frozen `N`; the event-log prologue now refuses, cancels, replays and activates funded filings under a reservation-equals-pending-plus-custody invariant.
- Compatibility: engine `facety-sim/runner-v9`, schema `facety-sim/result-v9` (`openingTransactions.refusedUnfunded` replaces `unfundedAtMaturity`), case admission `liquid-reserved-at-filing/v5`, Self-ID fixture `liquid-reserved-at-filing/v5`. Randomness v3 is unchanged; outcomes change for seeds with underfunded filings. Frozen D168 evidence and earlier runner results keep their recorded builds and are not reinterpreted.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-17

- Suspended the maturity of the accused participant's not-yet-processed Self-IDs for the duration of an active dispute (D196): no XP to author or creation-time guarantors, no spendable entitlement, no collateral use, no debt repayment through maturity and no integrity repair, while the record, chain position, roster and rolling creation slot stay unchanged and the act is not cancelled by its own deadline.
- Maturation is suspended, not blocked: deferred maturities leave the settlement cursor so unaffected accounts keep maturing, and a maturity scheduled for exactly the activation moment is withheld until the case has activated. Overlapping cases carry independent hold causes, so resolving one releases nothing still held by another.
- Extended the preventive cancellation window to suspended acts: the author or a creation-time guarantor may cancel throughout the freeze, including past 24 hours, and the cancellation is final. A suspended act that is a case target, an authorized case source, another case's reserved backing or an already corrected source refuses instead, so cancellation is no escape from an established obligation.
- Release credits each valid uncancelled deferred maturity exactly once with its original recipients and the state then in force; a deadline still in the future is kept, and no XP is backdated, no eligibility recomputed and no emission caught up. An adverse identity verdict releases the hold but postpones release processing until the authorized correction runs, so an invalidated chain is cancelled before it could be credited.
- Inside the Self-ID transaction fixture, an affected act of the filing-frozen scope that has not matured is invalidated at settlement instead of being credited later; the journal records it, and the isolated 1/1/0/0 accounting plus D195's frozen charges are unchanged.
- Mechanics change population outcomes for a given seed, so the engine identifier becomes `facety-sim/runner-v6`; schema v7, randomness v3, configuration digests and frozen evidence are unchanged. The declared before/after panel is a divergence check, not an equivalence claim: `base` stayed byte-identical and the other two configurations moved as expected.
- Evidence: `tests/suspension.rs` covers all twelve commissioned transition boundaries, the event-log prologue adds a suspended-and-released case with hold/deferred invariants, and the earlier "credit during the case" witness is retained with its superseded expectation.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-17

- Frozen a non-author Self-ID correction case's disputed scope and monetary liability at filing (D195): the challenged act, chain endpoint and inclusive `N` are snapshotted at recording, so a Self-ID created afterwards cannot raise that case's stake, the accused's charge or the guarantor charges. The 24-hour pendency still delays only activation, holds, custody and the clock. Supersedes D190's activation-time endpoint for this case kind.
- Made the trusted Self-ID fixture lifecycle-complete in its declared domain: any filing-frozen `N` settles through the selected soft-backing collection (D166) instead of only the isolated `N = 1` witness, so a pendency Self-ID, a consumed target right, changed guarantor backing or a competing reservation reaches an authorized result rather than an activated case with no exit.
- Settlement accounting unchanged in meaning: award equals actual collection, source burn is zero, guarantor charges burn only actual collection, every uncollected part is case-attributed FIFO debt, the identity and valid prefix are preserved, and the isolated D192 witness is now an outcome of that rule rather than a separate priority rule.
- Added `self_id_case_plan`, a read-only effect plan naming the frozen scope, charge, collectible amount and unpaid remainder, or the precise reason the case cannot settle yet; plan and settlement share one predicate set. Role-conflict refusals moved to recording, where the case owns no record, custody or hold.
- Kept the lifecycle-gap witnesses as regressions with their new expected outcomes and added filing-frozen immutability, later-act separation, disposition-change, reservation, batched-time and role-conflict checks; extended the event-log prologue with a filing-frozen deep case that converts its target and cancels an out-of-scope act before settling and reversing.
- Compatibility: fixture policy is `trusted-core-only/typed-self-id-target/filing-frozen-n/soft-backing-settlement/v2`. Schema, randomness, digests, result payloads and frozen evidence are unchanged; the declared panel re-ran byte-identically. The population runner never creates such a case, so no saved result exercises it. Boundaries stay explicit: transferred-value recovery, multi-role overlap, repeat-enforcement identity, active cancellation, restriction horizon, appeal finances.
- Runner and settlement milestone: three-choice Council rounds with deadline-only recognition and fresh electorates, linked core appeals with idempotent restoration, 24-hour openings, no-recipient top-stake burning, 12-decimal FCTY exact stakes and rounding, result-v5/v7, randomness v3 and explicit execution/voting policies.
- Evidence and documentation milestone: README restructure, Self-ID public-domain clarification, path-cache/certificate/phase/overflow defect repairs, Q26 reconciliation, active/frozen context consolidation, D188-D192 rules, D194's accepted procedural-authority rationale, plus unknown-ID refusals and the mechanical `world.rs` decomposition into emission/links/dispute children.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08

- Established an English-only protocol with separate decision history and a coherent participant-facing Rules of the Game. Consolidated selected participation, emission, guarantee, dispute and recovery defaults while keeping unfinished contracts explicit.
- Replaced the retired Node.js simulator with a standard-library-only Rust simulator. Added exact path verification against independent references, deterministic configuration and build provenance, repository integrity checks, and outcome-preserving performance improvements. The legacy implementation remains at the `legacy-node` tag.
- Implemented 24-hour maturity XP, receipt-based responsibility and integrity, overlapping dispute windows, staked escalation, soft backing and emission debt. Added held-right collection, historical-guarantor liability, custody and restriction audits, exact stake arithmetic, recovery scenarios and reproducible economic witnesses; remaining deviations stay explicit in the simulator contract.
- Closed D168 with 18 local and 20 final Rust runs on a frozen evidence core: REVISE operational assumptions. Preserved the killed B4 honest-growth claim and earlier negative findings. Conditional attack containment does not establish independent evidence, collectible rewards or finite human correction capacity. Research stopped; follow-ups remain in Q25.
- Prepared an operational-defense owner decision, end-to-end contract and synthetic manual-validation kit with voluntary roles, concealed case keys, shared clocks and blank observation logs. Admission, execution and appeal gaps remain open. No people were recruited, exercise run, personal data collected, or runtime/pilot started.
- Selected a third Council ballot choice: defer seven days from the current latest deadline, repeatably. An entered referendum without a majority automatically defers at cutoff; holds and restrictions continue. The binary simulator and frozen research results do not implement or validate this later rule.
- Renamed the monetary ticker from FACY to FCTY across current documentation and Rust-generated captions. Facety's name and asset mechanics are unchanged; immutable evidence, archived figures and compatible serialized/historical API identifiers retain their original bytes and names.
- Consolidated project context and reconciled obsolete backlog blockers with implemented maturity XP and settled referendum choices. Folded the milestone ledger into this versioned summary, preserving detailed history through permanent links and retaining study evidence, negative results and owner-gated work in their authoritative homes.
